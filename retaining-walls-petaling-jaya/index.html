Why retaining wall matters here.

Dense urban suburb of Wilayah Persekutuan Kuala Lumpur with mature SS-numbered sections (SS 1-25), Damansara enclaves, and continued hillside redevelopment near TTDI / Bukit Damansara / Section 17 ridges.
