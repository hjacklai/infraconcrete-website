02 / Our micropiling service scope

Five work categories.

1. New foundation micropiling

Hillside and remote-site new building foundations where variable depth to bedrock makes bored pile uneconomic. Design + install 150-300 mm micropile to competent stratum with rock socket where required.

2. Foundation underpinning

Existing buildings with differential settlement, scour, or added load. Micropile transferred load to deeper stratum via bracket-and-corbel detail; building stays operational during works. See dedicated underpinning page.

3. Basement and low-headroom work

Basement extension, crawl-space conversion, lift pit excavation. Our smallest rig operates in 3 m clearance. See basement underpinning page.

4. Slope stabilization

Micropile wall (pali radice in-situ reinforcement) through sliding mass into stable ground beyond failure surface. Typically combined with soil nailing above and horizontal drains for de-watering. See micropile slope stabilization page.

5. Retrofit micropiling

Bridge pier scour countermeasure, seismic retrofit of existing shallow foundations, transmission tower uplift resistance, historic / sensitive-structure load transfer.

03 / Capability base

Why we can execute micropiling now.

Micropiling is a drilling + grouting operation. The drilling methods (rotary, down-the-hole hammer), grouting equipment (silo + mixer + pump), reinforcement handling, grout mix design, and QA discipline are the same whether the drilled element is a soil nail or a load-bearing micropile. Our existing soil-nailing capability gives us the equipment, trained crew, and quality framework; micropiling adds the structural reinforcement engineering and load-test discipline on top. This is why we can confidently expand into micropiling as a service line without starting from zero.

What we add on top: FHWA-NHI-05-039 structural capacity design, load-test coordination with independent testing agencies (ASTM D1143 / D3689 / D3966), bracket-and-corbel detailing for underpinning, inclinometer monitoring for slope-stabilization applications, and vibration monitoring per BS 7385 for sensitive-building work.
