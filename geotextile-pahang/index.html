01 / Pahang applications

Hill-station drainage, Central Spine Road, coastal corridor.

  • Hill-station MSE wall drainage filter (Genting, Cameron Highlands, Bukit Tinggi, Fraser's Hill): nonwoven PP 200-300 gsm filter behind StrataGrid-reinforced fill, weep hole drainage.
