01 / East Coast applications

Petronas Kerteh, aquaculture, landfill, salt-spray.

  • Petronas Kerteh O&G corridor secondary containment: HDPE 2.0 mm with salt-spray durability spec; full chemical compatibility for hydrocarbon and refinery effluent.
  • Terengganu coastal aquaculture pond liners (Setiu, Marang): LLDPE or PVC for seawater-compatible aquaculture ponds.
  • Kelantan / Terengganu landfill basal and cap systems: HDPE + LLDPE composite per DOE Cawangan approval.
  • Industrial palm-oil and rubber mill effluent ponds: HDPE for acidic process effluent containment.
  • Highland reservoir lining (Hulu Terengganu area): HDPE or LLDPE.
