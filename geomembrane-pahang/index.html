01 / Pahang applications

Mining, highland reservoirs, landfill, coastal containment.

  • Mining tailings storage facilities (where applicable): HDPE 2.0-2.5 mm textured for slope stability on impoundment side slopes; compatible with mining process chemistry.
  • Highland reservoirs (Cameron Highlands, Bukit Tinggi area irrigation and farming reservoirs): HDPE 1.0-1.5 mm or LLDPE for water containment.
  • Kuantan area landfill basal and cap systems: HDPE 1.5 mm + LLDPE composite per DOE Cawangan Pahang.
  • Palm-oil mill effluent ponds: HDPE for acidic effluent chemistry.
  • Coastal Kuantan industrial secondary containment: HDPE for hydrocarbon and chemical bunds.
