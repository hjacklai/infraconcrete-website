01 / Penang applications

Hillside drainage filters, coastal basal mats, industrial.

  • Penang Hill / Tanjung Bungah / Air Itam hillside MSE walls: nonwoven PP filter behind StrataGrid-reinforced fill, weep hole drainage to surface drains per MBPP Hill Slope Guideline.
  • Penang Bridge approach embankments: StrataTex HSR basal mats on marine alluvium of approach corridor.
  • Bayan Lepas industrial-zone: nonwoven PP separator under aggregate sub-base over weak subgrade; working platforms.
  • Seberang Perai / Butterworth alluvial road embankments: StrataTex HSR + biaxial geogrid combined basal system.
  • Penang Port reclamation: high-strength HSR 400-800 kN/m for soft marine clay basal reinforcement.
