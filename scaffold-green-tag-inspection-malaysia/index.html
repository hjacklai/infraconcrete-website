01 / The DOSH tag system

Three tag colours.

TagMeaningAction
GreenScaffold has been inspected within the required interval and is safe for use at the loading and configuration certified.Normal use permitted.
YellowRestricted use. Specific limitations noted on the tag (e.g. limited loading, specific area off-limits, authorised persons only).Use only within tag limitations.
RedDo not use. Scaffold is being modified, has failed inspection, or has been damaged.Do not use. Rectification required before re-inspection.

Tag must be visible at every access point to the scaffold. Workers checking in should see the tag before stepping onto the scaffold.

02 / Inspection frequency

When we inspect.

  • Initial inspection: immediately after erection, before first use.
  • Weekly: standard interval for scaffolds in continuous use.
  • After modification: any time the scaffold is altered (tubes added, bracing moved, planks relocated).
  • After weather event: after heavy rain, high wind, or any event that could have affected scaffold stability.
  • Before high-risk work: before specific high-load or high-hazard activities on the scaffold.
03 / What the competent person checks

Full inspection scope.

  1. Base jacks and sole boards: level, settled, no softening of ground.
  2. Standards (verticals): plumb, no deflection, no visible damage.
  3. Ledgers (horizontals) and transoms: proper connection, correct spacing.
  4. Bracing: full pattern per design, no missing members, no loosened couplers.
  5. Couplers: all torqued to spec, no visible damage, correct type for the connection.
  6. Platforms: full width, no gaps, no damaged planks, no overloading.
  7. Edge protection: toe boards, guard rails all in place.
  8. Ties: all per design, properly anchored, no missing or loose ties.
  9. Access: ladders secured, platforms free of trip hazards.
  10. Housekeeping: no stored materials beyond design load, no obstruction.

Findings documented in the inspection logbook; tag affixed per outcome.
