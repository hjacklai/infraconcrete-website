01 / Penang applications

Hillside vegetated facing, channel lining, industrial.

  • Penang Hill / Tanjung Bungah / Air Itam / Balik Pulau steep cut faces: StrataWeb 100-150 mm depth vegetated facing per MBPP Hill Slope Guideline (Class III/IV slopes). Combined with soil nailing for steeper cuts.
  • Stormwater channel lining at MBPP and MBSP infrastructure works: concrete-filled StrataWeb for high-shear hydraulic protection.
  • Bayan Lepas industrial-zone working platforms: StrataWeb 150 mm filled with crushed aggregate over weak alluvial subgrade.
  • Seberang Perai mainland road embankments: load support combined with biaxial geogrid.
