01 / Klang Valley applications

Industrial containment, landfill, basement waterproofing.

  • Industrial secondary containment (Shah Alam, Subang, Klang industrial zones, Bukit Raja): HDPE 1.5-2.0 mm for hydrocarbon and chemical containment per chemistry-specific design.
  • Leachate evaporation ponds at industrial facilities: HDPE 1.5 mm with composite GCL backup; double-liner option for high-risk effluent.
  • Landfill basal liners and caps in Selangor: HDPE 1.5 mm basal + LLDPE 1.5 mm cap per DOE Cawangan Selangor approval.
  • Basement waterproofing (KL CBD, Mont Kiara, Bangsar): HDPE or LLDPE outside basement walls (between waterproofing membrane and the soil).
  • Irrigation reservoirs and decorative water features: PVC 0.5-1.0 mm or LLDPE 1.0 mm.
