01 / Sabah context

Crocker Range, oil palm interior, monsoon east coast.

Three project patterns drive soil nailing demand in Sabah: Crocker Range hillside developments (Kota Kinabalu, Tambunan, Keningau residential and resort platforms cut into Class III-IV slope), oil palm estate access platforms (mill platforms, estate roads, transhipment yards), and post-monsoon remediation on the Sandakan-Lahad Datu-Tawau corridor where seasonal slope failures take out state roads. Coastal exposure demands galvanized reinforcement, salt-spray-rated grout, and durability-class concrete on facing systems.
