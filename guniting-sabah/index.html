Use cases

Where shotcrete (guniting) typically appears.

Shotcrete (guniting) appears in many development scopes, both as facing and as structural lining. Wherever a sprayed mortar layer adds protection or capacity, guniting is the answer. The five typical scenarios:

Utility infrastructure

TNB substation slope facing, Air Selangor reservoir lining, IWK structure lining, water tunnel and intake lining. Underground and water-retaining structures rely on shotcrete for waterproofing and durability.

Residential development

Hillside development cut slope facing, podium retaining facing, hillside lot slope finishing. Standard finish on residential cut faces with soil nailing.

Hillside and special terrain

Soil nail wall facing, post-landslide slope facing, monsoon-failed slope facing, ground anchor wall facing. Reinforced shotcrete with mesh or fibre is the default for engineered facings.

Commercial and industrial

Warehouse pad cut slope facing, industrial estate cut slope facing, commercial development slope finishing. Architectural finishes can be applied over the structural shotcrete layer.

Public infrastructure

Tunnel and culvert linings, highway cut slope shotcrete facing, rail tunnel lining, bridge approach slope facing. EFNARC and ACI 506 grade standard.
