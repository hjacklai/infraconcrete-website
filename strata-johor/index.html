01 / Johor context

Iskandar mixed-use, Pasir Gudang petrochemical, port reclamation.

Johor's project mix is dominated by Iskandar Malaysia mixed-use development (Iskandar Puteri, Medini, Bukit Indah, Horizon Hills) and the Pasir Gudang petrochemical zone. Soil profiles vary from residual soil (inland) to marine clay along the western coast (Pontian, Batu Pahat, Muar) and tropical alluvium near JB and Pasir Gudang. Pasir Gudang projects often specify higher chemical-resistance materials for hydrocarbon contact zones.

STRATA products on Johor projects:

  • Iskandar Puteri / Medini cut platforms with StrataGrid PET-reinforced MSE walls (StrataWall panels typical for federal-grade abutments).
  • Pasir Gudang petrochemical secondary containment with HDPE geomembrane (Strata-brand or compatible) + nonwoven cushion.
  • Port reclamation (Port of Tanjung Pelepas, Pasir Gudang Port, Kuantan-Johor land bridge) with StrataTex HSR basal mats on soft marine alluvium and peat.
  • Highway and rail embankments (NSE, ECRL Section 3, Senai-Desaru) with StrataGrid Biaxial under road sub-base.
  • Senai logistics platforms with combined biaxial geogrid + nonwoven separator.

Singapore-influenced consultancies operating in Iskandar Malaysia typically specify to BS / EN / Eurocode 7 standards, which is the native framework for STRATA products supplied through Starwall.

02 / Authority spec for Johor

MBJB, JKR Cawangan, and federal references.

  • MBJB and MPJBT (Johor Bahru local authority): hillside and urban development control.
  • Iskandar Regional Development Authority (IRDA): regional master-plan compliance for Iskandar Malaysia.
  • JKR Cawangan Jalan / Cerun (Johor): JKR-SPJ Section 7 specifications.
  • Lembaga Pelabuhan Johor for port projects.
  • DOE Cawangan Johor for waste containment and landfill projects.
