For /geocell/ + /erosion-control/ + /geosynthetics/

StrataWeb HDPE Geocell, Vegetated Slope Protection

02 / 3D cellular confinement filled with topsoil + vegetation, anchored top-of-slope

Top-down view of a vegetated geocell slope. Hexagonal cell pattern (running-bond layout) drawn directly on the slope face, filled with green grass-fill pattern (vegetated topsoil). Red anchor pins at the top of slope establish the load path. Slope angle 1V:1.5H labelled at bottom right. The honeycomb shape is the visual signature of geocell so the customer instantly recognises the product.

For /geotextile/ + /earthworks/ + /geosynthetics/

StrataTex HSR Woven, Basal Reinforcement on Soft Ground

03 / High-strength geotextile mat below embankment fill, soft clay subgrade

Engineering cross-section the way consultants draw it: pavement / embankment fill / red geotextile mat / soft clay. Compaction lines in the fill suggest layered placement. Red tensile-load arrows (T) extending out the sides of the mat show the mechanism, the geotextile takes lateral spread load, prevents bearing-capacity failure on the soft clay.
