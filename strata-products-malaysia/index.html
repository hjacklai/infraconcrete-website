STRATA Geosystems products in Malaysia.

STRATA Geosystems is a global manufacturer of high-performance geosynthetic products with manufacturing facilities in India and operations in Spain and USA. In Malaysia, the full STRATA product family is supplied by Starwall Sdn Bhd, the sole appointed STRATA Asia distributor, which is part of the same ownership group as Infraconcrete Construction Sdn Bhd. Combined supply plus install under one contract via Infraconcrete (CIDB G7 + ISO 9001:2015). 12 product brochures available for download below. Geogrid family

StrataGrid + StrataBase + SGA-C.

StrataGrid (uniaxial PET geogrid)

Knitted high-tenacity polyester geogrid for vertical applications: MSE wall reinforcement layers, reinforced soil slope (RSS), basal reinforcement. 50-800 kN/m tensile, widths to 5.7 m. Over 100 years design life. MoRTH, IRC, AASHTO compliant.

View hub → · Download brochure (PDF)

StrataGrid Biaxial

Knitted biaxial geogrid variant. Two-direction reinforcement with knitted manufacturing. For applications requiring biaxial PET performance rather than rigid PP biaxial.

Download brochure (PDF)

StrataBase (rigid PP biaxial)

Extruded polypropylene rigid biaxial geogrid. Aperture interlock with granular fill plus membrane tension. For road base, railway ballast, airport runway, embankment basal reinforcement, haul roads, tank pad basal reinforcement. 25-50 percent granular base thickness reduction typical.

View product page → · Download brochure (PDF)

SGA-C StrataGrid Asphalt-Composite

PET geogrid + non-woven fabric + bituminous coating composite for asphalt pavement reinforcement. Mitigates reflective cracking, enhances fatigue life, reduces rutting. 50 + 100 kN/m MD + CMD. Roll widths to 3.75 m.

View product page → · Download brochure (PDF)

Drainage family

StrataDrain + StrataDrain PVD.

StrataDrain (geocomposite wall drain)

Composite drainage product with HDPE drainage core wrapped in non-woven geotextile filter. For wall drainage behind reinforced soil walls and conventional retaining structures, slope drainage blankets, prefabricated drainage strip, landfill drainage layer.

Download brochure (PDF, large)

StrataDrain PVD (Prefabricated Vertical Drain)

PP core (corrugated or fishbone) with non-woven PET/PP filter. Accelerates consolidation of saturated soft clay soils from years to months. For reclamation, embankment preload, port hardstanding, industrial pad, tank pad consolidation. IS 17371 certified.

View product page → · Download brochure (PDF)

Geotextile family

StrataTex HSR + StrataTex ST.

StrataTex HSR (woven PET high-strength)

Woven high-tenacity polyester geotextile for high-tensile basal reinforcement of embankments on soft ground. 50-500 kN/m design tensile range. Standard for soft-ground embankment construction.

Download brochure (PDF)

StrataTex ST (non-woven separation + filtration)

Non-woven geotextile for separation, filtration, drainage, soil stabilisation, and protection. Built on three decades of STRATA non-woven manufacturing. ISO 9001 certified.

View product page → · Download brochure (PDF)

Wall family

StrataWall EC + StrataBlock 2E.

StrataWall EC (Earth-Concrete RE wall)

Reinforced soil wall system with proprietary connection design using shear strength of concrete facing. No additional steel for facing connection. No rear anchorage steel. 2.5 kg per m2 steel saving. IIT-tested. 3-25 m typical height range.

View product page → · Download brochure (PDF)

StrataBlock 2E (modular block reinforced soil wall)

Updated modular block system with uniform fascia, 100 percent grid coverage, lightweight handling, no corrosion, compaction control, flexibility for differential settlements. Researched by Indian and US tech teams, IIT-approved. Used on national highway projects in India and globally.

Download brochure (PDF)

Slope facing + load support

StrataWeb.

StrataWeb (HDPE cellular confinement / geocell)

High-density polyethylene cellular confinement system. For slope facing protection, channel lining, load support over weak subgrade, retaining wall facing. Confines infill (soil, aggregate, concrete) within cells to develop bearing capacity and shear resistance.

Why distributor matters

What sole distributorship delivers.

  • Single mill source. Every shipment traceable to STRATA Geosystems manufacturing.
  • Direct manufacturer warranty. Material defects covered by manufacturer through Starwall, not a spot-market trader.
  • Mill certificate of conformance. Per shipment, batch-traceable.
  • Long-term design strength data. BS EN ISO 13431 creep certificates for reinforcement-grade products.
  • Replacement supply commitment. If rolls are short-supplied or damaged, manufacturer commitment to make good.
  • Combined supply plus install. Starwall supplies, Infraconcrete installs as CIDB G7 contractor. Single contract, single accountability for federal corridor and large projects.
