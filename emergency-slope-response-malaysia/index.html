When to call

Five emergency triggers.

  1. Visible new cracks, tilt, or movement. Of slope face, retaining structures, or buildings since the previous walkover. Growing during observation is the highest priority.
  2. Bulging or displaced earth at slope toe. Or new springs or saturated ground in unusual locations. Late-stage indicator of progressive failure.
  3. Rumbling sound or ground vibration heard. Failure is imminent. Evacuate the catchment immediately.
  4. Broken underground utility lines or burst water mains. Tension cracks below surface have sheared rigid pipes. Failure mechanism advanced.
  5. Incident response request from Bomba, NADMA, JKR, or local authority. Coordinate response within their command structure.

Should I evacuate?

Decision framework.

ObservationAction
Rumbling sound or ground vibrationEvacuate immediately. Failure imminent. Contact Bomba 999.
Broken underground utility lines or burst water mains on slopeEvacuate. Failure mechanism advanced.
Visible new cracks growing during observationEvacuate. Active progression.
Three or more documented warning signs togetherEvacuate as precaution while waiting for engineering arrival.
One or two warning signs without dynamic changeCordon the slope catchment, stay clear, wait for engineering arrival.

Documentation. Photograph the trigger, timestamp the evacuation decision, log actions taken. The documentation supports later insurance and civil claims and JMB office bearer liability defence.
