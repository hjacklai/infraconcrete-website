01 / What consultants get

The specialist that respects your design.

Design execution, not redesign

We deliver to your specification. Where buildability needs to be discussed, we raise it formally with method statements and RFIs, never quietly substitute.

Method statements at IEM grade

Method statements per BS/EN/Eurocode 7/JKR specs, submitted ahead of works. ITP (Inspection and Test Plan), HIRARC, JSA all included.

Test results back to you

Pull-out tests, cube/core strengths, piezometer/inclinometer readings, reported to you before next-stage works proceed. Closed-loop verification.

RFI and variation discipline

Design queries raised through formal RFI. Variations costed transparently. No silent assumptions, no surprise claims.
