01 / Penang applications

Core micropile use cases.

  • Penang Hill hillside foundations: 200 mm micropile with rock socket into granite bedrock for hillside condominium and commercial development subject to MBPP Hill Slope Guidelines.
  • Hillside slope stabilization: micropile wall combined with soil nailing on steep granite residual-soil slopes above George Town and Air Itam.
  • Historic George Town underpinning: low-vibration micropile for underpinning historic shophouses and heritage buildings in the George Town UNESCO zone; vibration kept well under BS 7385 limits.
  • Seberang Perai industrial underpinning: foundation strengthening on Bayan Lepas industrial estate for building reuse or added-load retrofits.
02 / Penang ground conditions

Why micropile fits here.

Penang geology is granite-dominant on the island, with coastal alluvium in Seberang Perai. Granite residual soil + weathered granite over fresh granite bedrock; rock depth highly variable on hillside sites. Micropile is often preferred over bored pile because of access constraints on hillside and cost-efficiency across variable rock depth.

04 / Authority spec

Compliance framework.

MBPP Hill Slope Development Guidelines for all hillside work; JKR-SPJ for federal and state road-adjacent work; FHWA-NHI-05-039 and BS EN 14199 for design; load testing per ASTM D1143 and D3689; vibration monitoring per BS 7385 for underpinning near heritage buildings.
