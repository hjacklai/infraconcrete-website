01 / Civil construction use cases

Six core applications.

Bridge pier access

Scaffolded access to bridge piers for construction, inspection, painting, and maintenance. Often cantilever or suspended configurations; PE certification normally required.

Formwork support (shoring)

GI tubular with U-head jacks supports concrete formwork for slabs, beams, transfer plates, and bridge decks. Design per BS EN 12812 falsework code.

Retaining wall construction

Scaffolded access during retaining wall formwork, rebar fixing, concreting, and backfill. Direct overlap with our MSE wall and reinforced soil slope capabilities.

Underpass and culvert access

Scaffolding inside underpasses, box culverts, and tunnels for construction, lining, waterproofing, and finishes work.

Soil nailing and shotcrete face access

Scaffolded work platforms on slope faces for soil nailing head beam construction and shotcrete application. Core to our geotechnical delivery.

Highway maintenance

Scaffolded access for sign gantry maintenance, overhead service work, slope repair, drainage structure maintenance along the federal and expressway network.

03 / Authority spec

JKR and LLM compliance.

Federal civil construction work typically runs under JKR Standard Specifications; expressway work under LLM / PLUS specifications. Both commonly require PE sign-off on scaffolding regardless of height, weekly DOSH green tag inspection, competent scaffolder cards for erection crew, and specific tie design on bridge-adjacent and highway-adjacent scaffolds. Our full service bundle covers all four requirements under single-point accountability.
