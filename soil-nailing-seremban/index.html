Soil Nail Contractor in Seremban.

Specialist soil nail contractor for Seremban (Seremban NS), Negeri Sembilan (state capital). High-strength steel bars drilled into the slope at engineered angles, grouted in place, and faced with shotcrete or panels. Locks the soil mass against sliding. Coverage across Bandar Seremban, Senawang, Rasah, Mambau, Lobak, Bandar Sri Sendayan, Nilai border, Tampin border, Port Dickson access. MBS (Majlis Bandaraya Seremban) local council compliant. Designed and installed to BS 8006-2, FHWA-NHI-14-007, Eurocode 7, JKR. CIDB G7 (highest grade), ISO 9001:2015 certified. 100+ delivered projects, 5 million m² stabilized across Malaysia.
