01 / Our story

From founding 2018 to federal-grade scope.

Infraconcrete Construction Sdn Bhd was founded in 2018 to fill a specific gap in the Malaysian geotechnical contracting market: a single specialist team that delivers the full slope and ground-engineering toolkit in-house, end to end, without subcontracting the specialist scope to multiple downstream vendors.

The thesis was simple: Malaysian residual-soil terrain, monsoon-driven groundwater regimes, federal infrastructure pipelines (highway, rail, ministry works), and hillside development pressure together create a constant baseline of slope and geotechnical scope. But that scope is typically split across multiple subcontractors (drilling, grouting, shotcrete, mesh, drainage, monitoring) with handoff gaps and timeline drift between them. We chose to own all six in-house.

From the first projects (residential developer cuts, post-monsoon distress remediation, small federal scopes), the work scaled into tier-1 federal references. By year 4 the portfolio included a significant portion of the East Klang Valley Expressway (EKVE) slope protection. By year 6 the East Coast Rail Link (ECRL) Section 3 drilled-drainage scope and Central Spine Road slope works were on the books. By year 8 (now) we hold approximately 100+ delivered projects covering 5 million m² of slope face stabilised across all 13 Malaysian states.

The founding thesis still holds: own the six in-house systems, hold programme through end-to-end accountability, deliver to BS / EN / FHWA / AASHTO / JKR standards, and treat the geotechnical scope as a single integrated discipline rather than a chain of trades.

02 / Group structure

Three companies, one accountability chain.

Infraconcrete operates as part of the Panthera Group. The structure lets us deliver design + supply + install + certification under single ownership, removing inter-vendor handoff risk on the geotechnical scope.

Infraconcrete Construction Sdn Bhd (this company)

The specialist contractor. CIDB G7, ISO 9001:2015. Six in-house geotechnical systems. Federal panel registered. Tier-1 references. The company you engage to design and install slope, retaining, and ground-engineering scope on your project.

Starwall Sdn Bhd

Sister company, sole STRATA Geosystems Malaysia distributor. Supplies StrataWall (precast-panel MSE wall), StrataBlock (modular block segmental retaining wall), StrataGrid (uniaxial PET geogrid for reinforced soil), StrataWeb (NPA-polymer geocell for erosion control and load support), and StrataDrain (geocomposite drainage strip). All STRATA Geosystems product supply for Malaysian projects routes through Starwall. See strata-malaysia/.

Panthera Group

The parent group. Holds operational policy, brand standards, and cross-company shared services. The group brand on our footer and stationery indicates the operating-company family.

Why the structure matters for you

When your project needs both engineered geosynthetic supply (geogrid, geocell, drainage strip, modular block facing) and specialist installation, you get a single point of accountability across both. Starwall supplies, Infraconcrete designs and installs. No third-party importer markup. No inter-vendor handoff timeline drift. No interface gap between supply spec and install method statement. One ownership family, two operating companies, one accountable delivery team.

This matters most on tall MSE walls, large reinforced-soil-slope scopes, geocell-faced erosion control, and any scope where the geosynthetic supply needs to be matched precisely to the in-situ design and construction.

Independent of OEM royalties

Where projects specify an alternative proprietary system, we install to the manufacturer's certified detail. Where the design is open, we recommend the STRATA Geosystems family (because we own the supply chain). Independence from any single OEM royalty stack is a core part of how we price competitively.

04 / Six in-house specialist systems

The full geotechnical toolkit, one team.

We own the crew, plant, supervisors, and engineering capability for each of the six core specialist systems. No subcontracting on the geotechnical scope.

Soil nailing

Drilled-and-grouted nails, self-drilling hollow bar (R32 / R38 / R51), tensioned and passive variants. To BS 8006-2, BS EN 14490, FHWA-NHI-14-007. Federal references EKVE, ECRL, Central Spine Road.

Guniting / shotcrete

Wet-mix and dry-mix sprayed concrete on slope faces, tunnel linings, structural repair. FRS energy classes E500 / E700 / E1000. ACI 506, BS EN 14487. ACI CP-60 certified nozzlemen.

Rock bolting

Tensioned and grouted rock anchors, cable bolts, self-drilling rock anchors, GFRP non-metallic anchors. BS 8081, BS EN 1537, AASHTO LRFD.

Rockfall barriers

Energy-rated flexible barriers 100 to 5000 kJ to ETAG 027 / EAD 340059, plus drape mesh and active anchored mesh. Federal highway and rail corridors.

Retaining walls

RC cantilever, RE wall, RS wall, MSE wall, modular block, gabion, crib wall, sheet pile, soldier pile. BS 8006, FHWA-NHI-10-024, AASHTO LRFD. Geosynthetic supply via Starwall.

Horizontal drains

Drilled subsurface drains 30 to 100 m long, slotted UPVC / HDPE pipe with geotextile filter sock, piezometer-monitored drawdown. BS 6031, FHWA-RD-97-130. ECRL Section 3 reference 320,000 m drilled.

06 / How we work

The approach that holds programme.

In-house from drilling to facing

The drilling crew, grouting team, shotcrete crew, rope-access technicians, and supervisors are all Infraconcrete employees. No subcontract chains on the specialist scope. Programme holds because there is no inter-vendor coordination drift. Quality is consistent because the same crews repeat across projects.

Engineering-led, not just installation

We engage at concept design where consultants want a specialist input, not just at tender. Pre-tender engineering review reduces design contingency and aligns the build method with the design intent. Optimisation typically saves 10 to 25 percent vs pre-tender spec.

Standards-native

BS / EN / Eurocode / FHWA / AASHTO / ACI / JKR fluent on every project. Suits any consulting engineer's spec without translation, including Singapore-aligned specs for Iskandar / Johor projects.

Live-traffic / tight-site / monsoon-resilient

EKVE, ECRL, dense urban hillsides, post-failure rectification: we plan and execute under live highway closures, near-occupied buildings, in possession-window rail logistics, during monsoon season. Method statement, HIRARC, ITP, and TMP submitted with every proposal.

Same-day site response (Klang Valley)

Office at 8B Jalan SS22/25, Damansara Jaya, Petaling Jaya. Engineers and supervisors on site within 4 hours of WhatsApp request across KL / Selangor / Putrajaya. Emergency mobilisation 2 to 5 days for post-monsoon slope distress. Sabah / Sarawak project-specific mobilisation 3 to 7 days.

Federal grade documentation

JKR / LLM / MOW / KKR-grade submittals on every project: design calculations, method statements, ITP, HIRARC, material certs, test programmes, as-built drawings, final test reports. Same documentation framework applied to a 1,000 m² residential cut and a 450,000 m² federal expressway alike.
