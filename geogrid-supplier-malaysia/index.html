01 / What we stock and supply

Three geogrid families, full grade ladder.

StrataGrid uniaxial PET (polyester)

Tensile strengths 30 kN/m to 1000 kN/m. PVC-coated PET filaments for chemical and biological durability. Used for MSE wall reinforcement, reinforced soil slope (RSS) reinforcement, basal reinforcement on soft ground, and segmental retaining wall (SRW) construction. Long-term design strength data per BS EN ISO 13431 (creep, durability, installation damage factors) included with reinforcement-grade orders. Stock at PJ warehouse in 30, 50, 80, 100, 150, 200 kN/m grades.

Biaxial PP (polypropylene)

Tensile strengths 20 to 40 kN/m, equal in machine and cross-machine directions. Apertures 30 to 50 mm typical for granular interlock with crushed aggregate. Used for road base reinforcement (reduces sub-base thickness over weak subgrade per AASHTO + Giroud-Han 2004 methodology), working platform over soft ground, sub-base stabilisation. Aggregate savings 20 to 50 percent typical on CBR 1 to 3 subgrades.

Triaxial PP (polypropylene)

Advanced punched-and-drawn PP geogrid with three-direction load transfer (triangular aperture geometry vs the square biaxial pattern). Higher stiffness at low strain, used in critical road and rail subgrade applications, working platforms for heavy cyclic loading, and where standard biaxial does not deliver sufficient confinement.

03 / What ships with every order

Material certificates, spec compliance, sample.

  • Mill certificate of conformance from the STRATA manufacturing plant for the specific batch.
  • Wide-width tensile test report (BS EN ISO 10319 or ASTM D6637), MD and CD directions.
  • Junction strength report (ASTM D6638) for biaxial and triaxial.
  • Pullout coefficient with reference soil (ASTM D6706) for PET uniaxial reinforcement grades.
  • Long-term design strength data per BS EN ISO 13431: creep reduction factor (RF_CR), installation damage factor (RF_ID), durability factor (RF_CH), join efficiency (RF_W). Converts ultimate tensile T_ult to long-term design tensile T_d for design submission.
  • NCMA SRW connection capacity data with named modular block facing systems on request.
  • Spec compliance statement mapping the supplied product against JKR-SPJ Section 7, BS 8006-1, FHWA-NHI-10-024, NCMA SRW Design Manual, or the project-specific consultant specification.
  • Sample roll for consultant inspection before bulk delivery, available on request at the order stage.
  • Delivery note + roll tags traceable to mill batch for site QA records.
04 / Three buying routes

Supply only, supply and install, or design and build.

Supply only

For main contractors, developers, and consultants who have their own installation crew. We deliver to your site, hand over the documentation pack, and the installation responsibility sits with you. Best for repeat buyers. Lowest of the three routes on price.

Supply and install

CIDB G7 installation crews to your site, placement per consultant spec (layer spacing, length, orientation, anchorage), on-site QA per BS 8006 / NCMA SRW, as-built record with pullout test results where spec requires. Pricing covers material, crew, supervision, plant, QA. Most common route for MSE wall, RSS, and basal reinforcement scope.

Design and build

Infraconcrete carries the geotechnical engineering responsibility from project geotechnical report through to construction. Design analysis (BS 8006-1 / FHWA-NHI-10-024 / NCMA SRW stability + pullout + connection checks), material specification, supply, installation, supervision. Best for projects where the geogrid is structurally responsible (MSE wall, RSS, basal mat over peat).

07 / Common applications we supply for

Where buyers in Malaysia actually use it.

MSE wall

StrataGrid uniaxial PET 50 to 200 kN/m typical, vertical spacing 0.4 to 0.8 m, length 0.7H to 1.0H. See MSE wall and MSE wall design with geogrid.

Reinforced soil slope (RSS)

StrataGrid uniaxial PET for slope angles 30 to 70 degrees. Slip-circle analysis with geogrid tensile capacity per BS 8006 / FHWA. See RSS.

Basal reinforcement on soft ground

StrataGrid or composite geogrid at embankment base over peat or soft marine clay. Rowe-Soderman or slip-circle analysis. Pairs with PVD. See basal reinforcement.

Road base reinforcement

Biaxial PP at sub-base / base interface, reduces aggregate thickness on weak subgrade per AASHTO + Giroud-Han 2004. See geogrid for road subgrade.

Segmental retaining wall (SRW)

StrataGrid with modular block facing (e.g. StrataBlock). Connection capacity per NCMA SRW Manual. See retaining walls.

Bridge abutment with reinforced soil

StrataGrid uniaxial PET 100 to 200 kN/m typical, gabion-faced or modular block facing, AASHTO LRFD design. See bridge abutment and gabion wall.

08 / Why through Starwall and STRATA

Single-source manufacturer route vs spot-market sourcing.

Most geogrid buyers in Malaysia source through spot-market traders who pick up rolls from whichever manufacturer has cheapest stock that month. Three risks. Traceability: mixed-mill stock means no batch traceability and no manufacturer warranty if a roll fails junction strength or pullout in the consultant audit. Spec drift: nominally identical SKUs from different mills can differ significantly on creep, junction strength, and durability reduction factors, breaking the consultant's design assumptions. Replacement liability: if rolls are short-supplied or arrive damaged, the trader has no commercial commitment to make good.

Starwall is the sole appointed STRATA Geosystems (Asia) distributor in Malaysia. Single mill source. Direct manufacturer certificate per shipment. Manufacturer warranty against material defects. Replacement supply commitment. Long-term design strength data per BS EN ISO 13431 in the documentation pack. Same engineering desk through the entire project, from quote to as-built record. Worth the small premium over spot-market sourcing on any project where the consultant or authority audits the geogrid credentials.

09 / FAQ

Buyers, consultants, and contractors usually ask:

Do you also handle install or just supply? +
Both. Supply-only contracts for buyers with their own crew, supply-and-install with CIDB G7 crew, or full design-and-build where Infraconcrete carries the geotechnical engineering responsibility. Pricing differs per route. See section 04 above.
What if my consultant has specified a different brand? +
Equivalence is the standard route. We supply an equivalence statement mapping the consultant-specified properties (tensile, junction strength, pullout coefficient, reduction factors) against the StrataGrid range and submit for consultant approval. Equivalence approval is routine when the technical properties match; the consultant retains design authority. For projects where the consultant insists on a named brand, we can broker direct supply through a manufacturer agent.
Can I get a sample roll for inspection before placing the bulk order? +
Yes. Sample rolls of common SKUs (biaxial PP 30 kN/m, StrataGrid PET 50 and 100 kN/m) are available at the engineering desk for collection or short-courier delivery. Higher-tensile or custom-spec samples are mill-supplied on request, 2 to 4 weeks lead time.
Do you supply for JKR, LLM, or KKR tender works? +
Yes. StrataGrid range is supplied with compliance statements against JKR-SPJ Section 7, LLM technical specifications, and KKR project requirements. Standard requirement on federal works is the certificate of conformance per shipment plus the long-term design strength data for reinforcement-grade SKUs; both are included by default with Starwall supply.
What's the minimum order quantity? +
No minimum on Klang Valley stock SKUs (single rolls collected from PJ stockyard). Direct mill orders typically require a full container minimum for the freight economics to work. Quotes confirm the minimum against the specific SKU and delivery point.
