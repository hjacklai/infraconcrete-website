06 / Common Questions

Frequently asked.

What is land creation? +
Engineered conversion of constrained, sloped, or unbuildable ground into compliant, developable platforms. Combines earthworks, retaining structures, slope protection, drainage, erosion control as one design-build package.
When does a site need it? +
Hillside residential, industrial platforms on sloped ground, floodplain development, fragmented parcels where most of the land isn't usable until engineered.
How is it different from earthworks? +
Earthworks is cut-fill-compact only. Land creation is earthworks plus retaining, slope protection, drainage, erosion control, sequenced so the platform is buildable, compliant, and stable for design life.
What standards? +
BS 6031, BS 8002, BS 8004, BS 8006, BS EN 1997, FHWA-NHI-14-007, ACI 506, JKR Standard Specifications, local authority requirements.
How does it interact with planning approval? +
Land creation is upstream of architectural design. We engage early with the consulting engineer and local planning authority on platform layout, retaining wall geometry, drainage, slope class.
Typical timeline? +
Small residential platform (1-2 ha): 3-6 months. 10+ ha mixed-use with multiple retaining structures: 6-18 months. Critical path is retaining wall sequence + monsoon weather window.
Turn-key engagement? +
Yes, often as design-build directly with developer or under consulting engineer's design. Geotechnical scope is delivered entirely by Infraconcrete in-house crews.

Where earthworks and land creation typically appears.

Earthworks underpin every major development. The starting point for converting raw or marginal land into buildable platforms. Six typical scenarios:

Utility infrastructure

TNB substation site preparation, Air Selangor reservoir earthworks, IWK treatment plant compound earthworks, telco tower site preparation.

Residential development

Hillside township platform creation, developer housing platform earthworks, gated community earthworks, individual lot earthworks.

Commercial development

Commercial podium earthworks, retail park platform creation, office tower site preparation, mixed-use development earthworks.

Industrial development

Industrial estate fill platforms, free trade zone earthworks, factory pad earthworks, warehouse and logistics pad earthworks, data center site preparation. Heavy floor loads dictate compaction spec (95 to 98 percent BS heavy).

Public infrastructure

Highway alignment earthworks, bridge approach earthworks, rail alignment earthworks, road widening earthworks. JKR Standard Specification and BS 6031 default.

Hillside and special terrain

Reservoir and dam earthworks, coastal reclamation and bunding, hillside terrace earthworks, post-landslide remediation earthworks.
