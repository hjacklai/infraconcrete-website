01 / Three polymers, three roles

HDPE, LLDPE, PVC.

HDPE, Primary containment

High-density polyethylene. Excellent chemical resistance, wide temperature range, long design life (50+ years). Stiffer than LLDPE, harder to install on irregular subgrade, more careful welding. Use: landfill base liners, mine tailings ponds, hazardous chemical containment, leachate collection, hydrocarbon storage secondary containment.

LLDPE, Flexible & cap systems

Linear low-density polyethylene. Softer than HDPE, conforms to settlement and rough subgrade. Slightly less chemical resistance but more puncture-tolerant. Use: landfill cap systems (where settlement is expected), pond liners with irregular subgrade, secondary containment under tanks, retrofit applications.

PVC, Water-only legacy

Polyvinyl chloride. Soft, easy to weld, cheaper. Limited UV resistance and chemical compatibility. Use: aquaculture ponds (fish, shrimp), irrigation reservoirs, ornamental ponds, decorative water features. Not recommended for landfill, chemical, or long-term UV exposure.

03 / Welding & QA

Hot-wedge primary, extrusion repair.

Hot-wedge (dual-track)

Automated welder runs along the seam at 1-2 m/min, fusing two parallel tracks with a small air channel between. The channel allows air-pressure testing: pressurise to 30 psi, hold 5 min, drop <3 psi = pass. Used for all primary panel-to-panel seams.

Extrusion welding

Manual extrusion gun lays a bead of molten polymer over an overlap joint. Used for patches, T-joints, penetrations, corners, repairs. Vacuum box test: cover seam with soapy water under a transparent vacuum box at 5 kPa; bubbles indicate leaks. Or spark-tested.

Destructive sampling

Per GRI-GM19, one sample per ~150 m of primary seam tested in laboratory for shear and peel strength. Failed samples → seam tracked back, re-welded, re-tested.

Documentation

As-built panel layout, weld log, pressure test records, vacuum box records, destructive sample reports, manufacturer certificate of conformance. Submitted to consultant + DOE / authority for sign-off.

05 / Installation method

How geomembrane goes in.

01

Subgrade preparation

Smooth, compact, free of stones >25 mm, sticks, sharp objects. Final-trim with hand tools. Compaction test records. Subgrade pH check for HDPE compatibility (alkaline soils require neutralising).

02

Cushion geotextile (if specified)

Nonwoven 200-400 g/m² over rough subgrade. Heat-bonded seams 100 mm overlap, no holes. Acts as puncture-protection for the geomembrane above.

03

Geomembrane deployment

Roll out one panel at a time during low-wind window. Anchor edges in compacted earth or concrete-filled trench. Allow 2-3% slack for thermal contraction. Sequence panels to minimise field-weld length.

04

Hot-wedge primary welding

Calibrate welder daily, temperature, speed, pressure. Trial welds at start of shift, destructive-tested. Production welding with continuous QA records. Air-pressure test each completed seam.

05

Detail work

Penetrations (pipes, anchors), T-joints, repairs handled with extrusion welder. Each detail vacuum-box or spark tested.

06

Cover & commissioning

For landfill base: drainage stone (cushioned by geotextile). For pond: water test for 7-14 days, monitor levels, repair any leaks. Document for DOE sign-off.

06 / FAQ

Engineers and developers usually ask:

HDPE, LLDPE, or PVC? +
HDPE for chemical / hazardous / long-life. LLDPE for caps / settlement-tolerant. PVC for water-only legacy.
What thickness? +
2.0+ mm landfill base. 1.5 mm landfill cap. 1.0-1.5 mm contaminated ponds. 0.75-1.0 mm irrigation. 0.5 mm aquaculture.
Hot-wedge vs extrusion welding? +
Hot-wedge for primary seams (with air-pressure testing). Extrusion for repairs, patches, details (with vacuum box / spark testing).
Cushion geotextile needed? +
Almost always. 200-400 g/m² nonwoven below; 400-600 g/m² above before drainage aggregate.
Standards? +
GRI-GM13, GM17, GM19, ASTM D5994/D5397/D6392, ISO 10318, BS EN 13491-13495, JKR + DOE.
Quality control documentation? +
As-built panel layout, weld log, air-pressure / vacuum box / spark test records, destructive sample reports, manufacturer certificate.
