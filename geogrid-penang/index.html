01 / Penang geogrid applications

Hillside MSE walls, coastal embankments, industrial.

  • Hillside MSE walls and RSS (Tanjung Bungah, Tanjung Tokong, Air Itam, Penang Hill, Balik Pulau): StrataGrid PET 80-200 kN/m. MBPP Hill Slope Guideline Class I-IV designs. See MSE wall design guide and RSS design guide.
  • Coastal mainland road embankments (Seberang Perai, Butterworth): biaxial PP under road sub-base with optional StrataTex HSR basal mat where alluvium is soft.
  • Bayan Lepas industrial-zone projects: working platforms with biaxial geogrid + StrataWeb + nonwoven separator.
  • Penang Bridge approach embankments: federal infrastructure with StrataGrid in MSE wall reinforcement.
