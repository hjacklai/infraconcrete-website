01 / Sibu context

Why slope repair matters here.

Sibu is the Sarawak central commercial city on the Rejang River. Timber, palm oil, fishing industries. Riverine flatlands with significant ground improvement requirements (soft alluvial soil), industrial platforms, hillside cuts on Bukit Lima periphery.
