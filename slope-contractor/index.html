01 / Slope contractor scope

Five categories of slope work.

Slope contracting in Malaysia divides into five engineering categories. Most live projects involve two or more in combination. Infraconcrete delivers all five families in-house.

Slope stabilisation

Engineering a slope to a target factor of safety against sliding. The umbrella discipline. Combines reinforcement (soil nails, rock bolts, ground anchors), surface protection (guniting, vegetation), and drainage (horizontal drains, surface drains) as an integrated package. Used on new cut slopes, slopes adjacent to live structures, and slopes showing early distress before failure occurs. See slope stabilisation.

Slope rectification

Correcting a slope that has shown distress (cracks, seepage, settlement, leaning trees) before catastrophic failure. JKR slope rectification framework applies. Federal-grade documentation. Specifically targets distress diagnosis, root cause analysis, and remedial design plus execution. See slope rectification.

Slope repair

Post-failure restoration of a slope that has already failed. Emergency mobilisation 2 to 5 days. Sequence: emergency stabilisation (cover the scarp, drain the body, install temporary works), site investigation, permanent remediation design, execution. See slope repair and post-landslide remediation.

Slope reinforcement

Adding structural elements (soil nails, rock bolts, ground anchors, geosynthetic reinforcement) to a soil or rock mass to increase its shear strength and factor of safety. The specific reinforcement engineering. See slope reinforcement.

Slope protection

Surface protection against erosion, weathering, and progressive surface failure. Shotcrete face, vegetation (vetiver, hydroseeding, geocell), rock netting and rockfall barriers. See slope protection.

02 / Slope contractor sub-disciplines

Six in-house systems, one accountable team.

The technical sub-disciplines underneath the five slope-work categories above. Infraconcrete owns crews, plant, and supervisors for every one of these, with no subcontracting on the geotechnical scope.

Soil nailing

The Malaysian default reinforcement for residual soil cut slopes. High-yield steel bars drilled and grouted at engineered angles. To BS 8006-2, FHWA-NHI-14-007, Eurocode 7, JKR.

Rock bolting

Tensioned and grouted rock anchors for cut rock faces, tunnel portals, quarry benches. To BS 8081, EN 1537, AASHTO.

Ground anchors

High-capacity tieback anchors for high retaining walls, deep excavation propping, slope reinforcement. To BS 8081, EN 1537, FHWA-IF-99-015.

Guniting / shotcrete

Wet-mix and dry-mix sprayed concrete face. The structural skin on soil-nailed and rock-bolted slopes. To ACI 506, EN 14487, JKR.

Rockfall barriers

Energy-rated flexible barriers (100 kJ to 5000 kJ) plus drape mesh and dynamic netting. To ETAG 027 / EAD 340059.

Rock netting

Passive drape mesh and active high-tensile anchored mesh for rockfall surface protection.

Horizontal drains

Drilled sub-horizontal drains for slope groundwater management. Often the most cost-effective single intervention.

Slope monitoring

Inclinometer, piezometer, prism, GNSS, crackmeter for post-rectification and pre-failure monitoring.

Independent contractor, any proprietary system.

The Malaysian slope-works market has several proprietary brand-tied systems supplied by various international and local manufacturers. Infraconcrete installs any proprietary system the project specification mandates, while also offering the equivalent engineering function as the independent contractor when the design is open. Infraconcrete is the leading independent slope contractor in Malaysia, with no commercial bias toward any single product line.

Reinforced soil and erosion-control systems

Reinforced soil walls and erosion-control mats are available from multiple international suppliers. To the same BS / EN / ETAG / FHWA design framework.

Soil-nail systems (proprietary or generic)

Soil-nail systems are supplied through several proprietary brand lines in Malaysia. Infraconcrete delivers equivalent engineering function with standard soil nailing (Y20 to Y32 deformed bar, deformed threadbar, or R32 / R38 / R51 self-drilling hollow bar) to BS 8006-2, FHWA-NHI-14-007, Eurocode 7, and JKR. Standard specification, broad supply chain, no proprietary brand royalty.

Energy-rated rockfall barrier systems

Energy-rated dynamic rockfall barriers are supplied by several international manufacturers and are commonly installed under their licensed-installer networks in Malaysia. Infraconcrete delivers the equivalent engineering function across the 100 to 5000 kJ energy class range, all to ETAG 027 / EAD 340059 certification framework, with the flexibility to install any spec-mandated proprietary kit or specify an equivalent independent system.
