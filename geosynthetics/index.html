03 / StrataWeb HDPE geocell

Cellular confinement for poor ground.

StrataWeb is a 3D cellular confinement system fabricated from black HDPE strips ultrasonically welded into a honeycomb panel. When opened on site, the cells are filled with granular material, vegetated topsoil, or concrete depending on application. Confined fill behaves stiffer than free fill, bearing capacity rises, lateral spread is suppressed, erosion resistance jumps.

Slope protection & vegetation

Open cells filled with topsoil + grass / Vetiver. Holds soil on slopes 1V:1.5H to 1V:0.7H. Common application on Malaysian highway and rail cut slopes.

Channel lining

Concrete- or aggregate-filled cells line drainage channels and spillways. High-velocity flow without scour. Replacement for cast-in-situ concrete lining.

Load support, paved & unpaved

Sub-base reinforcement on weak subgrade. Mining haul roads, plantation access roads, parking platforms. Reduces aggregate thickness 30-50%.

Retaining wall & gravity wall

Stacked cells become a gravity retaining wall. Lower-cost alternative to gabion wall in low-height applications. Vegetatable face for green slopes.

04 / StrataDrain geocomposite

Subsurface drainage without aggregate.

StrataDrain is a geocomposite drainage layer combining a polymer drainage core with geotextile filter fabric on one or both sides. Installed vertically behind retaining walls, on bridge abutments, on basement walls, or horizontally as a planar drain. Replaces conventional aggregate drainage, saves excavation volume, accelerates programme, eliminates haul of imported aggregate.

05 / Reinforced soil systems

Full MSE-wall packages from one supplier.

StrataSlope

Reinforced soil slope (RSS) at 30°-70° face angle. StrataGrid reinforcement layered into compacted fill. Vegetatable face. Cheaper than vertical MSE wall when ROW allows. Used for highway widening, hillside platforms.

StrataWall

Vertical (90°) MSE wall faced with precast concrete panels. StrataGrid uniaxial PET reinforcement. For federal highway, rail embankment, bridge abutments. To FHWA-NHI-10-024 / BS 8006.

StrataBlock

Segmental (modular) retaining wall, concrete block facing with StrataGrid geogrid tail-back into reinforced fill. Architectural finish. Commercial and residential applications, height typically 1-12 m.

Sleeve-It

Specialised post anchoring system embedded at the top of MSE / segmental walls. Allows fence posts, signage, or pool fencing to be installed in the reinforced backfill without overstressing the geogrid.
