01 / Johor geogrid applications

Iskandar mixed-use, Pasir Gudang industrial, port reclamation.

  • Iskandar Puteri / Medini cut platforms: StrataGrid PET 80-200 kN/m in MSE walls (StrataWall panels typical for federal-grade abutments). See MSE wall design guide.
  • Pasir Gudang petrochemical zone: StrataGrid in working platforms and access road sub-base.
  • Port of Tanjung Pelepas / Pasir Gudang Port reclamation: StrataTex HSR + StrataGrid Biaxial combined system on soft marine alluvium and peat. See road base design guide.
  • Senai logistics platforms: biaxial geogrid + StrataWeb + nonwoven separator.
  • NSE, ECRL Section 3, Senai-Desaru: federal highway embankments with StrataGrid Biaxial under sub-base.
