01 / Klang Valley context

Hillside developments, urban basements, federal infrastructure.

The Klang Valley sits on a mix of soil profiles. The KL bowl is largely residual soil over granite, with the heavily-developed PJ-Shah Alam-Subang corridor showing typical residual fines content of 30-40 percent. Coastal Klang has alluvial deposits transitioning to marine clay near the port. Hilly outskirts (Hulu Selangor, parts of Gombak, Cheras) carry steeper ground requiring slope stabilization with MSE walls, RSS, or geocell vegetated facing.

STRATA products see continuous demand across the Klang Valley project mix:

  • Hillside platform fills with StrataGrid PET-reinforced MSE walls (StrataWall panels or StrataBlock modular blocks). Common on Damansara, Cheras, Hulu Selangor hillside developments.
  • Urban basement perimeters with StrataDrain geocomposite behind diaphragm or sheet pile walls. Common across KL CBD, Mont Kiara, Bangsar.
  • Highway and rail embankments with StrataTex HSR basal mats over alluvium near port and federal corridor; StrataGrid Biaxial under road sub-base on weak subgrade.
  • Federal infrastructure (EKVE, MRT extension corridors, federal road widening) using full STRATA range under JKR-SPJ Section 7 and FHWA-NHI-10-024 specifications.
  • Industrial and logistics platforms in Klang, Pulau Indah, Subang Industrial with StrataGrid Biaxial + StrataWeb load support over soft ground.
02 / Authority spec for Klang Valley

Local authorities and federal references.

  • DBKL hill land controls (Dewan Bandaraya Kuala Lumpur): hillside developments require slope stability submission with JKR Class I-IV slope categorisation, often using geogrid-reinforced solutions and geocell vegetated facings.
  • MBPJ, MBSA, MBSJ, MPSJ, MPS (Petaling Jaya, Shah Alam, Subang Jaya, Subang, Selayang local authorities): each has its own hill land development control and slope works specifications.
  • JKR Cawangan Jalan / Cerun for federal works: JKR-SPJ Section 7 specifications, AASHTO M288 for geotextile, BS 8006 for reinforced soil.
  • Putrajaya Corporation for Putrajaya area projects.
  • DOE Cawangan Selangor for landfill liner approval (mostly outside immediate Klang Valley, but relevant for waste handling facilities).
