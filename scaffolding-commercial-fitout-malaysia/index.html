01 / Six commercial fit-out use cases

What we typically see.

Mall shop fit-out

Interior scaffolding inside a mall tenancy for ceiling work, services installation, feature wall construction. Typical 1-3 weeks on site; tight programme coordinated with mall management.

Office refurbishment

Office tower floor refurbishment: ceiling grid replacement, HVAC retrofit, lighting upgrade, fit-out of new tenant layout. Scaffold required at ceiling level across the floor plate.

Hotel renovation

Guest room refurbishment, corridor repainting, lobby renovation, facade cleaning on hotel operations. Night-shift and weekend work windows common.

Restaurant fit-out

New restaurant tenancy or restaurant renovation: ceiling, exhaust hood installation, feature wall, bar area. Short programme (typically 2-4 weeks), low-headroom rigs needed inside restaurant spaces.

Bank branch refit

Bank branch refurbishment: ATM installation, counter construction, teller line, ceiling work, security vault access. Weekend and after-hours work.

Mall tenancy change-of-use

Converting one tenancy to another (previous retailer out, new retailer in). Involves demolition access, structural modification scaffolding, and new fit-out scaffolding.

02 / Why GI tubular suits commercial fit-out

Three decisive advantages.

  1. Short rental economics: commercial fit-out rentals are typically 1-4 weeks, where GI tubular rental cost per week beats system scaffolding significantly.
  2. Irregular interior geometry: commercial interiors have columns, pipes, ducts, dropped ceilings, mezzanines. GI tubular adapts to any geometry; system scaffolding often cannot.
  3. Low headroom indoor rigs: our smallest crew can erect in 3 m headroom, essential for inside-mall and basement commercial work.
03 / Operational notes

Mall and operator coordination.

Mall tenancy work typically requires: mall management permit application, after-hours or early-morning access for material delivery (via loading bay), fire exit clearance maintained throughout, dust and noise protocols respected, security pass for crew. We handle all these as part of our full service bundle; for rental-only you handle the operator interface.
