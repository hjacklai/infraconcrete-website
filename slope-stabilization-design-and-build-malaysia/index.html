01 / Six applications most suited to D&B

Where this delivery model delivers most value.

Cut slopes greater than 4 m

Standard hillside cut: soil nailing + shotcrete + drainage + facing. BS 8006-2 + FHWA-NHI-14-007 design. Common Malaysian hillside township and commercial development.

Distressed slopes

Slopes showing visible distress (cracks, tilt, movement, post-monsoon damage). Urgent stabilisation: site survey + interim works + permanent design + execution. Often 24-48 hour mobilisation.

Post-failure remediation

JKR or LLM directive response after slope collapse. Four-phase sequence: stabilise (immediate), diagnose (geotechnical investigation), redesign (full slope analysis), build (permanent works). PI insurance on design.

Hillside township platform

Multiple cut/fill slopes across township platform. Coordinated design across slopes prevents standalone-design conflicts. Drainage layout integrates across whole platform.

Road widening at toe of cut

Sequential staged works with traffic management. Soil nailing into existing slope from base, sometimes with horizontal drains to manage groundwater. Live-traffic working method.

Groundwater-driven slope failures

Where piezometer monitoring shows high groundwater is the failure driver. Horizontal drains often alone restore factor of safety. Drainage-first design rather than reinforcement-first.

03 / Five design analysis tracks

What the design actually covers.

  1. Slope stability analysis: limit equilibrium (Bishop, Spencer, Janbu) on multiple slip surfaces. Pre- and post-intervention factor of safety. Critical slip surface located by iterative search.
  2. Groundwater analysis: piezometer monitoring or design groundwater table. Drainage path analysis. Horizontal drain effect on phreatic surface lowering.
  3. Reinforcement design: nail spacing and length, tensile demand per nail per BS 8006-2 / FHWA-NHI-14-007, pullout check, corrosion protection class (sacrificial / galvanised / DCP per BS 8006-2 Annex C soil aggressivity).
  4. Facing design: shotcrete thickness and welded mesh reinforcement, geocell cell depth and anchorage detail, or vegetated face protection with hydroseeding and turf reinforcement mat.
  5. Drainage design: surface drains (chute, cascade), horizontal drains (3-5 degree upward inclination, 30-100 m length), weep holes through shotcrete or wall face, outlet chute and energy dissipation.

Integrated output: single design report covering all five tracks with consistent assumptions, drawings showing all interventions, construction sequence for staged execution, QA programme covering each technique, monitoring proposal for post-construction validation.

06 / FAQ

Buyers and consultants usually ask:

Can you do design and build on a JKR slope contract? +
Yes. JKR design and build for slope stabilisation is routine. Submission goes through state JKR office plus project consulting engineer. JKR Slope Engineering Manual + BS 8006-2 + FHWA-NHI-14-007 govern technical content. Project-specific PI insurance covers contractor design negligence exposure.
What's the cheapest slope intervention? +
Horizontal drains alone (RM 95-450 per metre) if groundwater is the failure driver and piezometer monitoring shows phreatic surface lowering would restore factor of safety. For soil-driven failures: soil nailing + shotcrete (RM 280-650 per m squared face). Reinforced soil slope (RSS) for fill slopes only. Slope cutting to gentler angle (1V:2H or shallower) avoids reinforcement entirely if footprint allows. Honest recommendation: send geotechnical report to engineering desk for case-specific advice.
Do you handle slope monitoring as part of D&B? +
Yes, included in standard EPC scope or available as separate post-construction monitoring contract. Inclinometer behind slope face, piezometer in critical horizons, optical survey monuments at crest and toe, vibrating-wire crackmeter on existing structures. Typical monitoring period 6-24 months post-construction with trigger levels and response protocol.
How fast can you turn around an emergency slope design? +
Klang Valley: senior engineer + geotechnical consultant on site within 24-48 hours. Interim stabilisation works within 2-5 days. Rapid design (slope stability + reinforcement + drainage) within 5-14 days. Authority submission within 7-14 days. Permanent works execution 4-12 weeks. Other West Malaysia: add 24-48 hours to mobilisation. East Malaysia: 3-7 days mobilisation.
What software do you use? +
Slope/W (GeoSlope, limit equilibrium), Slide (Rocscience, limit equilibrium + finite element), Plaxis (finite element for serviceability and deformation), Geo5 (multi-method), in-house spreadsheets for nail spacing and shotcrete thickness. Software output forms part of calculation backup submitted with the design report.
