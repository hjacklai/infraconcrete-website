01 / Terengganu's slope context

ECRL + Petronas, monsoon coast.

Three project patterns: ECRL corridor (federal rail infrastructure across Pahang/Terengganu/Kelantan, 320,000 m drilled), Kerteh oil & gas platforms (Petronas-spec industrial works, salt-air durability), monsoon post-failure remediation (state roads, coastal cliffs around Marang, Dungun).
