01 / M&E use cases

Seven core applications.

Substation installation

Scaffolded access for 11 kV / 33 kV / HV substation construction, transformer installation, switchgear fit, cable gland work. Equipment layout is irregular; GI tubular adapts.

Chiller plant construction

Rooftop and basement chiller plant installation, pipework runs, electrical connections. Birdcage scaffolding under flat soffits, access scaffolding around chiller units.

HVAC installation

Ductwork installation, air handling unit (AHU) assembly, variable refrigerant volume (VRV) systems. Scaffolding at ceiling level in commercial, hospital, and institutional buildings.

Data centre M&E

Growing sector in Malaysia. CRAC unit installation, UPS room fit-out, raised floor construction, overhead cable tray and busbar installation. Scaffolding often required inside clean controlled environments.

Factory M&E

Food and beverage, pharmaceutical (non-cleanroom), electronics manufacturing, general industrial. Equipment installation, pipe-rack construction, utility runs, maintenance access.

Lift installation

Scaffolding inside lift shafts during machinery installation and commissioning. Height varies from 2-storey to 50+ storey; design per BS EN 12811 with specific lift shaft geometry considerations.

Fire protection systems

Sprinkler main installation, hose reel runs, FM-200 room fit-out, pump room construction. Scaffolding at ceiling level and around tank enclosures.

02 / Why GI tubular suits M&E work

Equipment is not uniform.

M&E equipment layouts almost never match a regular scaffolding bay. There are columns in the way, pipes above, equipment pads below, exhaust ducts crossing the work area, and existing structure to work around. GI tubular lets us build the scaffolding to fit the actual as-built equipment geometry, with couplers positioned where tubes need to go, not where a system scaffolding brand allows. For M&E contractors needing scaffolding fast, built to the site reality, GI tubular is the right default.
