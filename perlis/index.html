01 / Perlis's slope context

Limestone north, border state.

Perlis is Malaysia's smallest state, but slope work is real. The limestone karst geology of north Peninsula Malaysia extends from Perak through Perlis into south Thailand, generating cliffs, caves, and steep rock faces (Bukit Bintang, Bukit Chabang are local examples). The state's flat agricultural plains host modest industrial platforms and rice-belt infrastructure. Padang Besar's border crossing generates federal-scale infrastructure work.

Three project patterns dominate, in modest volume:

Limestone karst stabilization. Rock bolting, rock netting, rockfall barriers on cliffs above access roads or developments. Same playbook as our Ipoh / Lembah Kinta references.

Agricultural and small industrial platforms. Modest cut-and-fill, retaining walls, drainage. Most platforms in Perlis are small-scale, programme is leaner than Klang Valley industrial scale.

Padang Besar border infrastructure. Customs facilities, logistics platforms, access road slope works. Federal-scale federal-procurement work coordinated with PLBN and JKR Perlis.
