01 / Borneo applications

Mining tailings, palm-oil effluent, landfill, plantation.

  • Mining tailings storage facilities (gold, copper operations in Sabah and Sarawak): HDPE 2.0-2.5 mm textured for slope-side stability; composite GCL backup for hazardous tailings. Geoelectric leak detection (ASTM D7007) recommended for high-risk projects.
  • Palm-oil mill effluent (POME) ponds: HDPE 1.5-2.0 mm for acidic POME chemistry (pH 4-5 typical); chemical resistance verified per palm-oil industry spec.
  • KK / Kuching municipal landfill basal and cap systems: HDPE 1.5 mm basal + LLDPE 1.5 mm cap per DOE Cawangan Sabah / Sarawak approval.
  • Plantation irrigation reservoirs: PVC or LLDPE for water-only containment.
  • Bintulu SCORE industrial process containment: HDPE for industrial chemistry.
