01 / Important Disclaimer

Indicative only - site-specific quote always required.

The unit rates and ranges in this guide are indicative for benchmarking purposes only. They reflect Malaysian market conditions for 2025-2026, typical Klang Valley access and ground conditions, and standard project size. Actual project pricing depends on:

  • Site location, access, working hours, security
  • Ground conditions (residual soil, rock, soft soil, karst, mixed)
  • Project size and continuity of work
  • Material lead time and stock availability
  • Programme constraints (rush programme, monsoon overlap)
  • Specification details (corrosion protection class, design life)
  • Local supplier and labour availability (East Malaysia rates differ)
  • Currency / commodity fluctuations (steel, cement, oil)
  • CIDB grade and specialty licensing of contractor
For accurate budgeting. Engage a CIDB G6 / G7 specialist geotechnical contractor at concept design stage. Get a site-specific quote based on preliminary geotechnical input. The ranges in this guide are starting points, not estimates.

14 / Programme Allowances

Mobilisation, monsoon, approval.

Project programme phases (typical)

  • Concept design + initial SI: 3-6 months
  • Detailed design: 3-6 months (parallel with SI completion)
  • Authority approval: 6-12 months (multi-body)
  • Tender / contractor procurement: 2-4 months
  • Mobilisation: 4-8 weeks
  • Geotechnical works execution: project-dependent
  • DLP / monitoring: 24 months

Weather days per month (typical)

  • Klang Valley dry season (Feb-Apr, Jun-Sep): 5-10 weather days/month
  • Klang Valley SW monsoon peak (May, Aug-Sep): 10-15 weather days/month
  • East Coast NE monsoon peak (Nov-Feb): 15-25 weather days/month
  • East Coast inter-monsoon (Mar-Apr, Sep-Oct): 8-12 weather days/month

Frequently asked

Cost questions.

How much does soil nailing cost? +
Indicative RM 200-600 per m^2 of slope face. Standard 32 mm GEWI Grade 670 at 1.5x1.5 m grid with 100 mm shotcrete + BRC mesh runs RM 280-380 per m^2 for permanent works in routine ground. Heavier specs (200 mm shotcrete with steel-fibre, 40 mm bars, HDG) push to RM 500-600 per m^2. Per individual nail: RM 1500-3500 typical for 9-12 m permanent.
What's typical for ground anchors? +
Per anchor: 4-strand 600-700 kN: RM 15,000-25,000. 6-strand 900-1000 kN: RM 22,000-35,000. 8-strand 1300-1400 kN: RM 30,000-50,000. 12-strand 1900-2000 kN: RM 50,000-80,000. Per m^2 anchored wall face: RM 800-2500. Class 2 dual-corrosion: +20-30 percent. Excludes RC capping beam.
What lead times for materials? +
Local stock (1-4 weeks): GEWI 32 mm, rebar, mesh, UPVC, geotextile, cement. Imported (4-16 weeks): prestressing strand, sheet pile non-stock sections, geogrid (Tensar/Strata/Miragrid), HDPE geomembrane, specialty threadbar, rockfall barrier components. Always confirm at design stage to avoid programme issues.
How do I budget hillside development geotech scope? +
Total geotech (SI + design + slope work + drainage + monitoring): 8-15 percent of project value for modest sites, 15-25 percent for steep / complex / tall. SI 0.3-0.7 percent. Consultancy 0.5-2 percent. Slope stabilization 3-15 percent. Drainage 5-10 percent of slope cost. Monitoring RM 100,000-500,000+. Get site-specific quotes at concept design.
What weather day allowance for programme? +
Klang Valley dry: 5-10 weather days/month. Klang Valley SW peak (May, Aug-Sep): 10-15 days/month. East Coast NE peak (Nov-Feb): 15-25 days/month. Sarawak NE: 12-20 days/month. Cameron Highlands / high-elevation: 12-18 days year-round. Programme must avoid critical-path open-cut activities during peak monsoon.
