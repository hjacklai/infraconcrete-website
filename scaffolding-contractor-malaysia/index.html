01 / What a CIDB G7 scaffolding contractor means

Grade as trust layer.

CIDB G7 is the highest contractor grade issued under the Construction Industry Development Board Act 520. It has no upper tender value limit, which means the licence qualifies us to work on any project size, from a RM 20,000 one-week residential job to a RM 20 million infrastructure contract. G7 is also the grade the main contractors and government tender boards look for when awarding scaffolding packages. Combined with ISO 9001:2015 (quality management) and our construction operations base, this is the trust layer that lets us hold single-point accountability on your scaffolding scope.

02 / Our scaffolding delivery model

Capability base plus partner network.

Scaffolding has been part of our geotechnical construction scope for years (every soil nailing head beam, every shotcrete face, every retaining wall formwork needs scaffolded access). We now offer it as a standalone service. For execution we work with a vetted network of DOSH-certified competent scaffolders, BEM-registered Professional Engineers, and material-stock partners. The client signs with us, we manage the whole scope under our CIDB G7 licence and ISO 9001:2015 QA, we invoice once. This is the industry-standard operating model for main contractors offering scaffolding alongside wider construction services.
