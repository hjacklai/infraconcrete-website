01 / Pahang context

Hill stations, ECRL corridor, federal slope work.

Three patterns: Hill-station resort developments (Genting, Cameron Highlands, Bukit Tinggi, Fraser's Hill - Class III/IV slopes with strict monsoon-period downtime windows); ECRL Phase 1 (federal rail corridor crossing Pahang - 32 km of chainage, 320,000 m total drilled length on the alignment for soil nails + horizontal drains); and Central Spine Road (Ministry of Works project - 65,000 m² of slope face delivered with 6 solution types). High monsoon rainfall demands groundwater-led design, not just structural reinforcement.
