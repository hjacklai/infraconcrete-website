01 / Negeri Sembilan's slope context

The Klang Valley spillover, plus the coast.

Negeri Sembilan absorbs the development pressure spilling out of the Klang Valley to its south. Seremban, the state capital, is ~70 km from KL on the North-South Expressway and now hosts substantial residential and mixed-use development on cut-and-fill terrain at the city edges. Nilai, Mantin, and Labu sit between Klang Valley and Seremban, hosting industrial parks and education towns (INTI, Nilai University). Port Dickson is the established coastal tourism town with retaining and beach-edge slope works.

Three project patterns dominate:

Hillside residential platforms. Seremban's expanding residential developments cut into the gentle hill terrain at the city's east and south edges. Platform creation, MSE walls, soil-nailed cut slopes, drainage. Mukim Sentul is one such reference.

Industrial platforms (Senawang, Nilai, Labu). Manufacturing platforms for Klang Valley spillover. Cut-and-fill on terraced terrain, retaining walls, drainage.

Port Dickson coastal works. Beach-edge retaining, tourism platform creation, coastal slope protection. Tropical exposure plus monsoon resilience.
