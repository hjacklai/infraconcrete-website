01 / What nonwoven geotextile is

Random fibres bonded into a felt-like fabric.

Nonwoven geotextile is a permeable textile fabric made from randomly oriented polymer fibres bonded together by needle-punching, thermal bonding, or chemical bonding. The most common Malaysian nonwoven is needle-punched polypropylene (PP): continuous filaments laid down in a random web, then barbed needles repeatedly punched through the web to physically entangle the fibres into a felt-like structure with thousands of mechanical bond points per square centimetre. The resulting fabric has high elongation, good in-plane drainage capacity, and excellent filtration properties.

Contrasts with woven geotextile (yarns interlaced on a loom). Function-first selection: nonwoven for filtration, drainage, protection, light separation; woven for reinforcement, heavy separation, structural applications.

02 / Four core functions

Filter, drain, separate, protect.

1. Filtration

Allow water through while retaining the protected soil. Driver properties: AOS (ASTM D4751, sized to soil grading), permittivity (ASTM D4491, at least one order of magnitude faster than protected soil hydraulic conductivity), gradient ratio (ASTM D5101, less than 3 over design life). Applications: french drain wrap, behind retaining wall back drain, horizontal drain collector filter, leachate collection in landfill cells.

2. Drainage

In-plane water flow within the fabric thickness. Driver property: transmissivity (ASTM D4716) for thick nonwoven or geocomposite. Most nonwoven thicknesses (1.5-3 mm) provide moderate drainage; for heavy drainage demand, geocomposite (thick polymer core wrapped in nonwoven) is preferred. Applications: chimney drains, behind basement walls, sub-pavement drainage.

3. Separation

Keep two soil layers from intermixing. Driver properties: mass per unit area (200-400 gsm typical), CBR puncture (D6241), grab tensile (D4632), trapezoidal tear (D4533). Applications: under light-traffic roads and working platforms where filtration is also needed (woven slit-film cheaper if filtration not needed).

4. Protection

Cushion layer above geomembrane to prevent puncture from angular gravel or stone above. Driver property: high mass per unit area (400-800 gsm). Applications: landfill basal liner protection, pond liner protection, secondary containment cushion, drainage gravel above geomembrane.

05 / Six core applications detailed

Where nonwoven geotextile actually goes.

1. French drain wrap

Nonwoven PP 150-200 gsm wrapped around perforated UPVC or HDPE pipe drain. AOS sized to soil grading (typical 0.15 mm for most Malaysian residual soils). Filter sock for fines retention. Linear application, fast installation.

2. Behind retaining walls

Nonwoven PP 200-300 gsm between drainage gravel and retained soil. Prevents fines migration into the drainage layer. AOS sized to retained soil grading. Pairs with weep holes or perforated pipe drain at wall base.

3. Under rip-rap and gabion mattress

Nonwoven PP 300-400 gsm between rock armour and underlying soil. Prevents wave or flow action from washing fines out from under the armour. Common on coastal protection, river training (JPS / DID), bridge scour countermeasures.

4. Sub-pavement separation

Nonwoven PP 200-300 gsm between subbase aggregate and weak subgrade where both separation and filtration are needed. For pure separation under heavy load with no filtration concern, slit-film woven PP is cheaper.

5. Geomembrane protection cushion

Heavy nonwoven PP 400-800 gsm above HDPE geomembrane in landfill basal liner systems, pond liner systems, secondary containment. Protects geomembrane from puncture by angular gravel above. GRI-GT12 specification.

6. Leachate collection

Nonwoven PP 300-400 gsm above geomembrane plus drainage layer in landfill cells per DOE Garis Panduan. Acts as filter between drainage aggregate and the waste mass above. Resistance to chemical attack from leachate verified per ASTM D5322.

07 / FAQ

Buyers and consultants usually ask:

What gsm do I need for X application? +
French drain wrap: 150-200. Behind retaining wall: 200-300. Under rip-rap: 300-400. Sub-pavement separation: 200-300. Geomembrane protection cushion: 600-800. AOS sized to protected soil grading independently. Check filter compatibility (gradient ratio less than 3) for high-fines residual soils.
Lead time for common SKUs? +
150-400 gsm: same-day pickup from Klang Valley stockyard, next-day Klang Valley delivery, 2-4 days nationwide West Malaysia, 5-7 days East Malaysia via Port Klang sea freight. 500-800 gsm heavy grades: 3-6 weeks direct mill order.
Filter compatibility check on Malaysian residual soils? +
High-fines Malaysian residual soils (silt and clay greater than 30 percent) need gradient ratio testing (ASTM D5101) on the actual project soil-geotextile combination, not just AOS and permittivity. For dispersive clay (rare but possible in some regions), pinhole test (ASTM D4647) on the soil first. Use stricter end of AOS range (O95 around 0.10-0.15 mm) for high-fines residual soil.
Do you supply for DOE landfill projects? +
Yes. Nonwoven PP 300-600 gsm for landfill leachate collection and protection cushion per DOE Garis Panduan plus GRI-GT12 specification. Compatible with HDPE geomembrane systems. Chemical resistance verified for leachate exposure.
