Use cases

Where rock bolting typically appears.

Rock bolting stabilizes rock masses across many development types. Wherever a rock mass needs reinforcement against wedge or block failure, bolting is the standard intervention. The five typical scenarios:

Utility infrastructure

TNB tunnel portal stabilization, hydropower tunnel rock bolting, water tunnel and intake stabilization, dam abutment anchoring. Underground utility infrastructure relies on bolted rock support.

Residential development

Hillside development rock cut stabilization, gated community access through rock cuts, hillside lot rock face reinforcement. Residential rock cuts above units demand zero-tolerance stabilization.

Hillside and special terrain

Foundation rock anchoring for slope structures, post-rockfall stabilization, dam abutment rock bolting, highland resort rock cut stabilization. Engineered to BS 8081 and BS EN 1537.

Commercial and industrial

Quarry bench stability rock bolting, mining access rock bolting, hilltop industrial site rock cuts. Operational rock faces in active production environments.

Public infrastructure

Highway tunnel portal rock bolts, rail tunnel rock bolting, road rock cut stabilization, bridge foundation rock anchoring. Live-traffic and live-rail phasing standard.
