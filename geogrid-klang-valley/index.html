01 / Klang Valley geogrid applications

Hillside MSE walls, urban platforms, federal road base.

  • Hillside platform MSE walls (Damansara Heights, Cheras, Hulu Selangor, Semenyih): StrataGrid PET 80-200 kN/m with MSE wall facing (StrataBlock modular or StrataWall precast panel). Design per BS 8006 coherent gravity method; see MSE wall design guide.
  • Reinforced soil slopes (RSS) on hillside developments with right-of-way available: StrataGrid PET with vegetated facing (wrap-around fabric or StrataWeb geocell overlay). See RSS design guide.
  • Highway and federal road embankments: ELITE, EKVE, KESAS approach embankments with StrataTex HSR + StrataGrid Biaxial combined system over alluvium near port and federal corridor.
  • Urban basement perimeter (KL CBD, Mont Kiara, Bangsar): combined with StrataDrain geocomposite for drainage.
  • Industrial and logistics platforms (Klang, Pulau Indah, Subang Industrial): StrataGrid Biaxial + geocell + nonwoven separator over weak subgrade.
  • MRT/LRT corridor embankments: federal infrastructure with StrataGrid as primary reinforcement in MSE walls.
02 / Authority spec for Klang Valley

Local authorities and federal references.

  • DBKL hill land controls: hillside developments require JKR Class I-IV slope categorisation and corresponding engineering response.
  • MBPJ, MBSA, MBSJ, MPSJ, MPS: each with local hill land specifications and slope works requirements.
  • JKR Cawangan Jalan / Cerun: JKR-SPJ Section 7 for federal works.
  • Putrajaya Corporation: Putrajaya area projects.
