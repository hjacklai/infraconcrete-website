Engineering team and credentials.

Both companies operate under Panthera Group from Petaling Jaya, Selangor. The combined team includes registered engineers, project managers, geotechnical specialists, and field crews with cumulative experience on 100+ Malaysian geotechnical, slope, and geosynthetic projects.

Two companies, one accountability point.

CompanyRoleWhy this matters to project owners
Infraconcrete Construction Sdn BhdCIDB G7 contractor for slope stabilization, MSE walls, geosynthetics installation, earthworks, and ground improvementSingle signature on the main contract. ISO 9001:2015 QA on every site.
Starwall Sdn BhdAppointed STRATA Geosystems Malaysia distributor (StrataGrid, StrataTex HSR, StrataWeb, StrataDrain, StrataBlock, StrataWall, StrataSlope, Sleeve-It)Direct supply from STRATA, no broker mark-up. Factory warranty intact.
Panthera GroupParent holding company. Owns Infraconcrete + Starwall plus other construction and engineering businesses.Capital, governance, and group warranty stand behind both subsidiaries.

This structure is the answer to the most common project-owner question on geosynthetics work: "If the material fails, who is responsible — supplier or installer?" Under our group model both are the same ownership. One root cause investigation, one remedy, no inter-party finger pointing.

03 / Engineering competencies

Standards we work to every day.

British / European

BS 8006-1 and -2 (reinforced soil), BS 8081 (ground anchorages), BS 6031 (earthworks), BS EN 1537 (anchors), BS EN 14487 (sprayed concrete), Eurocode 7 / BS EN 1997 (geotechnical design with partial factors).

American / FHWA / AASHTO

FHWA-NHI-10-024 (MSE walls), FHWA-NHI-07-092 (geotextile design), FHWA-NHI-14-007 (drilled shafts), FHWA-HEC-15 (channel design with geocell), AASHTO LRFD Bridge Design Specifications, AASHTO M288 (geotextile material spec).

ASTM / ISO test methods

ASTM D6637, D6638, D4595, D4751, D4491, D5101, D5321, D5397, D5994, D6244, D6706, D7007, D7466, D7864 plus ISO 10319, 11058, 12956, 13431, 13426 (test methods underpinning every geosynthetic property in our specs).

Malaysian / regional

JKR-SPJ Section 7 (earthworks and slopes), JKR Standard Specifications for Building / Roadworks, DOE Malaysia landfill guideline, MBPP Hill Slope Development Guidelines, LLM expressway specifications.

Design software in routine use

Plaxis 2D / 3D, Slide / Slide2, MSEW, ReSSA, GeoStudio (SLOPE/W, SEEP/W), AutoCAD Civil 3D for grading and earthworks; in-house Excel sheets for BS 8006 and FHWA-NHI hand checks before software.

Industry sectors served

Federal highways, state roads, MRT and rail, port and coastal reclamation, hillside residential, hillside commercial, mining haul roads and TSF, landfill, plantation drainage, oil and gas industrial.
