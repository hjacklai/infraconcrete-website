01 / Johor applications

Port reclamation, Iskandar, Pasir Gudang, NSE corridor.

  • Port of Tanjung Pelepas / Pasir Gudang Port reclamation: StrataTex HSR 400-800 kN/m basal mats on soft marine alluvium and peat. Combined with biaxial geogrid for confinement.
  • Iskandar Puteri / Medini development: nonwoven PP separator and filter for cut-platform drainage.
  • Pasir Gudang petrochemical zone: nonwoven PP 400-800 gsm cushion above HDPE geomembrane in secondary containment per GRI-GT12.
  • NSE, Senai-Desaru, ECRL Section 3 embankments: nonwoven separator + StrataTex HSR basal mat where alluvium is soft.
  • Pontian / Batu Pahat / Muar coastal road embankments: high-strength HSR for soft marine clay.
