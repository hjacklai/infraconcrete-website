01 / Klang Valley geotextile applications

Basements, road platforms, MSE wall back drainage, landfill cushion.

  • Urban basement perimeters (KL CBD, Mont Kiara, Bangsar, Damansara): nonwoven PP 200-300 gsm filter wrap on perforated drain pipes; protection of waterproofing membranes.
  • Highway and federal road embankments: StrataTex HSR basal mats over alluvium near Port Klang and federal corridor (EKVE, KESAS, ELITE approaches).
  • MSE wall back drainage: nonwoven PP filter on retained-fill side of geocomposite or gravel chimney behind MSE walls on hillside developments.
  • Pavement separation: nonwoven PP 200-400 gsm between weak subgrade and sub-base aggregate on plantation, industrial, container yard projects.
  • Erosion control underlay under riprap: nonwoven PP 400-600 gsm on stormwater channels and river training.
  • Industrial landfill containment: nonwoven PP 600-800 gsm cushion above HDPE geomembrane (GRI-GT12).

See the geotextile design guide for filter criteria (Terzaghi-Cistin, gradient ratio) and the slope design with geotextile guide for slope-specific applications.
