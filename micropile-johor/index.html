01 / Johor applications

Core micropile use cases.

  • Iskandar Puteri commercial foundations: 200-250 mm micropile for high-rise and commercial developments on reclaimed and variable ground conditions.
  • Pengerang petrochemical foundations: micropile for process equipment pads, pipe-rack supports, and tank farm foundations within the RAPID complex and adjacent industrial zones. Design to BS EN 1997 and client (Petronas) spec.
  • Pasir Gudang industrial underpinning: underpinning for industrial facility expansion or equipment load upgrade.
  • Johor coastal underpinning: foundation retrofit on coastal buildings with scour or settlement issues; micropile transfers load to deeper competent stratum.
02 / Johor ground conditions

Why micropile fits here.

Johor geology mixed: granite in central spine (common with Iskandar hillside developments), sedimentary and alluvial in coastal zones (Pasir Gudang, Pontian, Batu Pahat), reclaimed and dredged fill in port areas (Pasir Gudang, Pengerang reclamation). Micropile is often the only cost-effective way to transfer load through variable fill or soft alluvium to the deeper competent stratum.
