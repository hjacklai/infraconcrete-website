01 / Specialist services

The six in-house geotechnical systems.

Infraconcrete designs and installs every geotechnical scope in-house, no subcontracting on the specialist works. Click through to the technical guide for each.

Soil Reinforcement

Soil Nailing →

High-yield steel bars drilled and grouted at engineered angles. BS 8006-2, FHWA-NHI-14-007, JKR. Cut slopes, retained excavations, distressed slopes.

Surface Protection

Guniting & Shotcrete →

Wet-mix and dry-mix sprayed concrete on slope/rock faces, tunnels, structural repair. ACI 506, EN 14487. The structural skin on soil-nailed slopes.

Read guide
Rock Anchorage

Rock Bolting →

Tensioned and grouted rock anchors for cut rock faces, tunnel portals, quarry benches. BS 8081, EN 1537, AASHTO.

Read guide
Rockfall Defence

Rockfall Barriers →

Energy-rated flexible barriers (100 kJ to 5000 kJ), drape mesh, dynamic netting. ETAG 027 / EAD 340059. Highway, rail, mining benches.

Read guide

Retaining Walls →

RE/MSE walls, modular block, RC cantilever, sheet pile, soldier pile, gabion. BS 8002, BS 8004, Eurocode 7.

Read guide
Subsurface Drainage

Horizontal Drains →

Drilled subsurface drains lowering the groundwater table by gravity. BS 6031, JKR. Single most effective fix when groundwater drives failure.
