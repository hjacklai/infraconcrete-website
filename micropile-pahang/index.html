01 / Pahang applications

Core micropile use cases.

  • Genting and Cameron Highlands resort foundations: hillside foundations for resort and hospitality developments on highly variable weathered granite over bedrock.
  • Central Spine Road and East Coast Expressway bridge retrofit: micropile for bridge pier scour countermeasures and seismic retrofit along the federal road network.
  • Kuantan industrial underpinning: foundation underpinning for industrial facilities in the Gebeng industrial zone and port area.
  • Hillside slope remediation: micropile wall systems on active hillside instability along the Karak Highway, Genting access roads, and Cameron tea plantation zones.
02 / Pahang ground conditions

Why micropile fits here.

Pahang hill country geology is weathered granite dominant in the Genting / Cameron and central zones; coastal Kuantan is alluvial and sedimentary. Highland sites typically have thick residual soil over variable bedrock depth. Micropile is a core tool for highland hospitality and infrastructure where access and rock-depth variability make bored pile uneconomic.
