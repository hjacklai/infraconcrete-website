01 / Sabah applications

Core micropile use cases.

  • Pan Borneo Highway bridge foundations: micropile for bridge pier and abutment foundations where soft alluvium or variable bedrock makes bored pile difficult.
  • Kota Kinabalu hillside residential: hillside foundation for KK residential and commercial on variable weathered rock over bedrock.
  • Palm oil mill foundations: micropile for mill equipment pads and tank foundations on soft alluvium and peat sites.
  • Coastal structure retrofit: foundation retrofit for coastal and port structures with scour or settlement issues along the Sabah coast.
02 / Sabah ground conditions

Why micropile fits here.

Sabah geology ranges from recent alluvium (coastal plains), peat (parts of Beaufort / Klias), weathered sedimentary and metamorphic (interior), to volcanic (Ranau / Kundasang). Depth to competent bearing stratum highly variable. Micropile is a core tool across this range.

03 / Logistics

Rig mobilisation to Sabah.

Equipment and crew mobilised by air and sea. Project minimum stay 4-8 weeks typical for sustained delivery. Staging base in Kota Kinabalu for Sabah mainland work; Tawau / Sandakan for eastern Sabah.
