02 / Common Penang project types

Four key sectors.

  • George Town UNESCO heritage work: careful scaffolding on heritage shophouses, museums, religious buildings. Low vibration, heritage-sympathetic access, aligns with our underpinning capability base.
  • Bayan Lepas industrial M&E: factory and electronics manufacturing M&E, chiller plant, HVAC, substation. GI tubular suits irregular equipment layouts.
  • Hillside residential and commercial: condominium facade work, hillside building maintenance (MBPP Hill Slope Guideline area awareness).
  • Mall and commercial fit-out: Gurney Plaza, Queensbay Mall, Komtar, 1st Avenue, Prangin Mall tenancy refit.
03 / Logistics from Klang Valley

Delivery via NSE.

Delivery from our Klang Valley yard is typically 4-6 hours via NSE. For sustained Penang projects we stage equipment and crew locally to avoid repeated mobilisation; typical minimum stay 2 weeks between re-mobilisations. For heritage work we coordinate with Penang Island City Council (MBPP) and Seberang Perai City Council (MBSP) for permits and site conditions.
