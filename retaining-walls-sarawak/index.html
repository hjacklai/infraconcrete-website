Use cases

Where retaining walls typically appears.

Retaining structures appear in almost every land development. Whenever ground level changes, a retaining solution is part of the design. The five typical scenarios:

Utility infrastructure

TNB substation and pylon platform terraces, Air Selangor reservoir and pump station retaining, IWK treatment plant compound retaining, telco tower platform terraces. Utility platforms almost always sit on cut-and-fill terraces.

Residential development

Developer housing platform terraces, gated community boundary and lot retaining, high-density podium retaining, hillside lot retaining behind units. Cluster, terrace, and bungalow lots all involve level changes.

Hillside and special terrain

Highland resort terraces, post-landslide retaining replacement, river-bank retaining, coastal retaining and reclamation. Hillside and waterfront retaining each have their own design framework.

Commercial and industrial

Warehouse and logistics pad retaining, commercial podium and parking deck level changes, data center compound retaining, retail park terraced platforms. Heavy floor loads dictate wall and foundation engineering.

Public infrastructure

Highway approach embankments, bridge abutment retaining, rail corridor retaining, road widening retaining. Authority submission and live-traffic phasing are routine.
