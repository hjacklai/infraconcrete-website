04 / FAQ

Buyers and QS usually ask:

What is the cheapest retaining wall option? +
For vertical face: gabion wall at RM 380-720 per m cubed for heights 1-8 m, or modular block SRW at RM 380-700 per m squared. For steepened-slope geometry: RSS at RM 220-450 per m squared is cheapest engineered option but requires base footprint.
What's the cost difference between MSE wall and RC cantilever? +
MSE wall (RM 450-900 per m squared) is typically 25-50 percent cheaper than RC cantilever (RM 600-1200) for heights 4-12 m. RC wins on tight urban sites with restricted footprint. MSE wins on tall heights, fast construction, and where moderate settlement is tolerable.
