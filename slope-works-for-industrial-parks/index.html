01 / Why industrial parks need a specialist

Five drivers specific to industrial estate development.

  • Heavy-load platform requirement: manufacturing buildings, warehouses, and process plants impose high static and dynamic loads on the platform. Geotechnical design has to satisfy higher bearing capacity and lower settlement targets than commercial / residential.
  • Salt-spray and chemical exposure: coastal industrial zones (Pasir Gudang, Port Klang, Pengerang, Bayan Lepas, Bintulu) and petrochemical sites have aggressive ground chemistry that drives Double Corrosion Protection (DCP) on permanent slope and retaining works.
  • Large plot footprints: 1 to 20 hectare factory plots are typical. Perimeter retaining wall length is substantial. Site grading and drainage capacity scales with plot size.
  • Heavy access traffic: 40-ton container trucks, mobile cranes, heavy plant. Access road slopes need higher factor of safety, drainage capacity, and retaining wall load capacity than commercial sites.
  • Operational continuity for expansion: phase 2 / phase 3 expansion on operational manufacturing sites requires vibration-controlled adjacent work, noise management, and traffic coordination with the operator's production schedule.
02 / Scope we deliver for industrial park projects

Five common industrial slope scopes.

Heavy-load platform creation

Cut and fill to create factory pads with high bearing capacity. Controlled engineered backfill, ground improvement where founding is soft (preloading + vertical drains, deep mixing, stone columns), MSE walls (StrataGrid via Starwall) or RC cantilever walls around the platform perimeter. Drainage integration with the platform grading scheme. See land creation, ground improvement.

Perimeter retaining walls between plots

Tall retaining walls (3 to 12 m height typical) between adjacent factory plots, between plots and access roads, between plots and external infrastructure (drains, power easements, gas pipeline rights-of-way). Designed for industrial-loading conditions (heavy truck surcharge, dynamic loading).

Industrial access road slope

Cut and fill stabilisation on internal estate roads carrying heavy plant. Soil nailing on cut slopes, MSE retaining on fill embankments, drainage to handle stormwater plus run-off from adjacent factory pads. Coordination with estate-level drainage scheme.

Drainage management for chemical / petrochemical sites

Process drainage separated from storm drainage (per DOE Environmental Quality Act). Chemical-spill containment integration with slope drainage. Sub-horizontal drains for slope groundwater control behind process plant retaining walls. Stainless steel hardware where chloride or process-chemical exposure.

Post-occupancy distress remediation

Slope movement, retaining wall cracks, settlement under heavy plant loading. Emergency mobilisation 2 to 5 days. Vibration-controlled work near operational machinery. Coordination with operator's HSE and production teams.

Phase 2 / phase 3 expansion adjacent to operational

New retaining and slope works on the expansion footprint, with vibration-controlled and noise-managed phasing to protect operational manufacturing on the adjacent phase 1.

03 / Industrial-park-specific delivery framework

What changes on industrial sites.

  • 50+ year design life: permanent industrial buildings have longer design life than commercial. Double Corrosion Protection (DCP) on permanent soil nails and ground anchors where aggressivity requires. Hot-dip galvanised + epoxy or DCP on hardware. Stainless steel where chloride-aggressive.
  • Soil aggressivity testing: BS 8006-2 Annex C panel of tests (pH, resistivity, chloride, sulphate, redox) on representative soil samples. Drives corrosion protection class for permanent works. Standard on coastal and petrochemical sites.
  • Heavy-load design: retaining wall design includes industrial-truck surcharge loading (40-ton container truck plus dynamic factor), heavy plant point loading where mobile cranes operate adjacent to the wall, and seismic loading where applicable.
  • Phased delivery: industrial parks typically build out in phases. Slope and retaining scope phased to match infrastructure delivery (roads first, then plot perimeter retaining, then factory pads).
  • DOE / environmental compliance: environmental controls during construction (silt traps, sediment ponds, dust suppression). Coordination with developer's DOE consultant on environmental management plan (EMP) compliance.
  • Operator-spec for petrochemical: Pengerang PIPC, Kerteh, Kemaman zones have operator-specific specifications (process plant design code, chemical containment, fire-safety, blast loading). We work to operator briefs where applicable.

05 / FAQ

Industrial project questions.

What scopes do industrial parks need? +
Five common: heavy-load platform creation, perimeter retaining walls between plots, industrial access road slope, chemical / petrochemical drainage management, post-occupancy distress remediation, phase 2 / 3 expansion adjacent to operational manufacturing.
Pengerang / petrochemical site work? +
Yes. Pengerang Integrated Petroleum Complex (PIPC) is Malaysia's largest petrochemical zone. Operator-specific specifications, chemical-exposure-rated detailing, fire-safety coordination, blast-loading where applicable. DCP corrosion protection on permanent works due to aggressive ground chemistry.
Coastal salt-spray sites (Pasir Gudang, Port Klang)? +
Standard scope. Soil aggressivity testing drives DCP corrosion protection per BS 8006-2 Annex C. Stainless steel hardware where chloride exposure. Galfan coating on rockfall mesh and similar.
Bintulu SCORE / Sarawak industrial? +
Project-specific East Malaysia mobilisation 3 to 7 days. Existing Sarawak / Pan Borneo footprint. Samalaju, Tanjung Manis, Bintulu Port industrial scope all within mobilisation framework.
Heavy truck surcharge design? +
Industrial retaining walls designed for 40-ton container truck surcharge plus dynamic factor per BS 8006 / AASHTO LRFD. Higher surcharge for mobile crane operations adjacent to walls. Discussed with project structural engineer.

Industrial park slope scope?

Factory pad creation, perimeter retaining, access road, petrochemical drainage. Coastal salt-spray sites and Pengerang scope covered.
