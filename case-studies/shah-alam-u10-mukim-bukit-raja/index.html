Shah Alam U10, Mukim Bukit Raja.

Shah Alam Section U10 (Mukim Bukit Raja) is a township and platform development area in the Shah Alam district of Selangor. Our group delivered geotechnical and geosynthetic-related scope on platform development in this area, including earthworks, retaining and platform geometry, and drainage works typical of Selangor township platform construction.

01 / Project context

Township platform in the Klang Valley.

Township platform construction in Mukim Bukit Raja typically involves bulk cut and fill earthworks to establish platform levels for subsequent residential or commercial construction, retaining systems at the boundary with adjacent properties, drainage to manage surface runoff in the wet equatorial climate, and erosion control during the platform handover phase. The Selangor Town and Country Planning Department guidelines and Shah Alam city council requirements apply.

02 / Scope delivered

Geotechnical and retaining works.

  • Bulk earthworks and platform formation
  • Retaining systems at platform boundaries, including MSE walls with geogrid reinforcement where vertical retention served the platform geometry
  • Drainage works including sub-soil drainage and surface drainage
  • Erosion-control measures on exposed fill slopes during construction phase
03 / Engineering themes

What township platforms typically demand.

Township-scale platforms in Selangor face a familiar set of engineering considerations: residual soil with variable weathering, water management under monsoon rainfall, retaining geometry to maximise developable area within plot boundaries, and a tight programme to enable downstream residential or commercial construction. Geosynthetic-based retaining systems (MSE wall with modular block facing, RSS with vegetated facing) are often more cost-effective than reinforced-concrete cantilever walls at the heights and geometries typical of township platforms.
