Mukim Bandar Batu 18, Semenyih.

Mukim Bandar Batu 18 in Semenyih (Hulu Langat district, Selangor) is a residential development corridor in the southern Klang Valley. Our group delivered geotechnical and geosynthetic-related scope on platform development in this area, including bulk earthworks, retaining systems, and drainage works typical of Selangor residential platform construction.

01 / Project context

Residential platform in southern Klang Valley.

The Semenyih and wider Hulu Langat residential development belt has been a sustained growth corridor in southern Selangor over multiple development cycles. Platform development in the area typically involves earthworks to establish residential plot levels from undulating residual-soil terrain, retaining systems at boundary geometries, and drainage management. Subgrade conditions in the area include residual soil derived from weathered granite and meta-sediment bedrock, with variable depth to refusal.

02 / Scope delivered

Geotechnical and retaining works.

  • Bulk earthworks and platform formation for residential plot development
  • Retaining systems at platform boundaries (MSE wall with geogrid where vertical retention served the platform geometry)
  • Drainage works to manage surface runoff under monsoon rainfall
  • Erosion-control measures during construction phase
03 / Engineering observations

What residential platforms typically need.

Residential platforms in Semenyih typically require careful management of post-construction settlement (to avoid downstream damage to subsequent house foundations), drainage management to handle runoff onto adjacent plots, and retaining geometry to maximise developable area within boundary constraints. Geosynthetic-based MSE wall systems at modest heights (typically 2-6 m) are common and cost-effective compared to reinforced-concrete cantilever walls.
