The technical descriptions on these pages are based on publicly available facts; project-specific commercial details and confidential design data remain under NDA where applicable.

About these case studies

Our editorial policy.

Every project listed here was either delivered by our group or one in which our group held a defined supply or installation role. We list a project as a case study only when (1) we have client-side authorisation to do so and (2) the technical scope can be described from publicly available information. Commercial terms, named consultants, and design data remain under any applicable NDA. Where a project is omitted that you expect to see, this is intentional: we do not publish projects we have not actually been involved in.

For project-specific case study material under NDA (named consultant references, instrumentation data, settlement plots), contact our engineering desk with the relevant tender or due-diligence requirements.
