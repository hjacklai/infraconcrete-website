Use cases

Where retaining structures typically appears.

Retaining structures appear in almost every land development. Whenever ground level changes, a retaining solution is part of the design. Six typical scenarios:

Utility infrastructure

TNB substation and pylon platform terraces, Air Selangor reservoir and pump station retaining, IWK treatment plant compound retaining, telco tower platform terraces. Heights typically 3 to 8 metres.

Residential development

Developer housing platform terraces, gated community boundary and lot retaining, high-density podium retaining behind apartment towers, hillside lot retaining behind units.

Commercial development

Commercial podium and parking deck level changes, retail park terraced platforms, office tower basement retaining, mixed-use development terraces. Architectural facing options drive wall-type selection.

Industrial development

Industrial estate platform retaining, free trade zone terraces, warehouse and logistics pad retaining, factory compound retaining, data center compound retaining. Heavy floor loads dictate wall and foundation engineering. Heights up to 12 metres.

Public infrastructure

Highway approach embankments, bridge abutment retaining, rail corridor retaining, road widening retaining. Wall types selected by economics: MSE for tall, RC cantilever for short, anchored systems where settlement and footprint are constrained.

Hillside and special terrain

Highland resort terraces, post-landslide retaining replacement, river-bank retaining, coastal retaining and reclamation. Hillside and waterfront retaining each have their own design framework.
