Use cases

Where rock netting drape typically appears.

Rock netting drape protects ground below rock cut faces from spalling and ravelling. The drape catches loose rock without trying to hold the whole mass. The five typical scenarios:

Utility infrastructure

TNB transmission corridor rock face drape, water utility access road rock drape, telco tower access drape. Used where minor rock fall is the main hazard rather than wedge failure.

Residential development

Hillside housing rock cut drape, gated community road drape, hillside lot rock face drape. Lower-cost alternative to barriers where energy levels are modest.

Hillside and special terrain

Highland resort access drape, tourist viewpoint cliff drape, post-rockfall drape, decorative rock face drape. Vegetation can re-establish through the mesh over time.

Commercial and industrial

Quarry rim drape, industrial estate access drape, hilltop development rock face drape. Often pairs with rock bolting on the same face.

Public infrastructure

Highway rock cut drape, rail corridor rock drape, mountain road drape. Standard part of integrated rock face treatment.
