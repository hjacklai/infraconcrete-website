01 / Federal credentials

The grade required for federal slope works.

CIDB G7 (Reg. 0120220822-WP104845), the highest grade. Categories: B04, CE01/06/08/21/36, M15. Eligible for all tiers of public and private project in Malaysia.

ISO 9001:2015, certified by ARS Assessment under UAF/IAF accreditation. Quality management documentation throughout.

Standards native: JKR Standard Specifications for Highway and Government Works (slope, drainage, earthworks divisions), plus BS 6031, BS 8006, BS 8081, EN 1537, EN 14487, Eurocode 7, FHWA reference manuals.

03 / Procurement

Tender, e-tender, direct appointment.

We respond to JKR/LLM/MOW e-tenders via MyEPerolehan, traditional sealed-tender procurement, and direct appointments under emergency works clauses. Documentation, method statements, and HSE submissions per the federal spec.
