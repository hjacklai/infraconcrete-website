01 / The group structure

Sister companies, one ownership, one accountability.

Starwall Sdn Bhd is the appointed STRATA Geosystems distributor for Malaysia. Starwall handles the STRATA supply chain: material orders, logistics, factory liaison with STRATA Geosystems, manufacturer certificates of conformance, batch traceability, technical datasheets, and project-specific stock holding. Starwall maintains the formal distributor relationship with STRATA.

Infraconcrete Construction Sdn Bhd is the CIDB G7 installer, ISO 9001:2015 quality system. Infraconcrete provides the design support to consulting engineers (BS 8006, FHWA-NHI-10-024, AASHTO LRFD, NCMA SRW design coordination), in-house installation crews (no subcontracting on the specialist scope), and project execution (site mobilisation, supervision, documentation, handover).

Both companies sit under the same ownership and parent group, Panthera Group. The two operate as a coordinated group: Starwall on the procurement and supply side, Infraconcrete on the engineering and execution side. For project owners and consulting engineers, the result is single-point procurement covering both the material and the workmanship, with one accountability chain.

For projects that prefer supply-only (with installation tendered separately) or installation-only (where material is procured by another contractor through Starwall), both routes are workable. We route accordingly at brief stage based on the project's procurement model.

02 / STRATA Geosystems manufacturer profile

One of the world's largest geosynthetics manufacturers.

STRATA Geosystems is an international geosynthetics manufacturer with operations across Asia, Africa, the Middle East, and the Americas. Manufacturing is ISO 9001:2015 certified. Globally, approximately 65 million m² of StrataGrid uniaxial PET geogrid is installed across 50+ countries, in applications including MSE walls and reinforced soil slopes for federal highway and rail infrastructure, basal reinforcement on soft ground for port reclamation, and base reinforcement on plantation and mining haul roads.
