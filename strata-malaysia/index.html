01 / Why a sole-distributor relationship matters

Three reasons it changes the equation.

Authentic STRATA material

Every roll arrives with manufacturer's certificate of conformance, batch traceability, and warranty backed by STRATA itself. No grey-market product, no rebranded look-alikes, no incompatible reduction-factor data on file.

Manufacturer's engineering team

Design support, long-term allowable strength derivation per ISO 13431, and field troubleshooting come directly from STRATA's technical group — channelled through us. Most local suppliers stop at a spec sheet; we bring you the manufacturer's engineer when a non-standard project demands it.

Single ownership accountability

If there's a supply issue (Starwall), design question, or installation defect (Infraconcrete), one group owns the answer. No fingerpointing between supplier, consultant, and installer because Starwall and Infraconcrete are sister companies under the same ownership. CIDB G7 contractor + sole STRATA distributor + in-house geotechnical engineering, all in the same corporate group.

04 / How we work with consultants

From design intent to commissioned wall.

01

Concept consultation

Send the project brief, geometry, soil report (if available). We respond with product recommendation, indicative grade selection, and budget estimate. No charge, no obligation.

02

Design support

For non-standard or large projects, STRATA's manufacturer engineering team — channelled through Infraconcrete — will issue full design calculations: stability check, allowable strength derivation per ISO 13431, FoS verification per BS 8006 / FHWA-NHI-10-024.

03

Procurement & delivery

Order placed, manufacturer certificate of conformance issued. Standard grades shipped from local stock; specialised grades drawn ex-factory. Delivery direct to site or staged storage in our Selangor facility.

04

Installation

Our CIDB G7 crews install per the consultant's drawing or our design-build scope. Continuous QA — geogrid placement, layer compaction, geotextile overlap, geocell tendon connection, geomembrane field welding with NDT.

05

Commissioning & documentation

As-built drawings, batch-traceable material records, NDT reports, manufacturer warranty letter. Submitted to consultant + authority for sign-off. Defect liability period commences.
