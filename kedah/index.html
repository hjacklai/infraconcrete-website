01 / Kedah's slope context

Hi-tech industrial belt plus island tourism.

Kedah's terrain is mostly flat paddy in the central plains, but the development pressure concentrates on three slope-relevant fronts: the Kulim Hi-Tech Park and Sungai Petani industrial corridor inland, Langkawi's hillside tourism and hospitality on the island, and the North-South Expressway slopes traversing the state.

Three project patterns dominate:

KHTP and Sungai Petani industrial platforms. High-value manufacturing investments cut platforms into the gentle terrain at Kulim and Sungai Petani's edges. Vibration-controlled construction near sensitive equipment, RE walls, RC retaining, drainage. Same playbook as our Bandar Serendah reference (auto industry, 540,000 m³ backfill, 7 retaining structures).

Langkawi tourism and hillside hospitality. Resort, villa, and hotel developments cut into Langkawi's interior hills. Soil nailing, retaining walls, slope drainage, tropical exposure spec. Island logistics extend mobilization windows.

NSE and federal corridor. Slope maintenance and post-monsoon remediation along the NSE through Kedah. Sub-surface drainage where pore-pressure is the failure driver.
