01 / What the single contract carries

Five scope elements, one accountable party.

Earthworks on a Malaysian hillside or township platform is rarely a single trade. It combines forming the platform, retaining the levels, treating the cut slopes, and draining the whole site. In design and build, all of it sits under one contract so the design of each element is set against the others rather than tendered in silos.

Geotechnical and earthworks design

Ground investigation interpretation, cut and fill balance, platform levels, mass-haul plan, slope geometry, and temporary works. Design to BS 6031 and BS EN 1997 (Eurocode 7), with BS 5930 governing the ground investigation and BS 1377 the soil testing.

Bulk earthworks execution

Topsoil strip and stockpile, bulk cut, fill placement and compaction in 200 to 300 mm lifts, subgrade preparation and final trim. Compaction to 95 percent MDD general fill and 98 percent MDD structural backfill per JKR-SPJ Section 2 and MS 1227.

Retaining structures

Reinforced earth (RE) walls, gravity, cantilever, and gabion. The wall type is chosen to suit the ground, the fill available on site, and the platform geometry, rather than fixed before the earthworks design is settled. Facing detail integrated with the drainage.

Slope stabilization

Soil nailing, shotcrete facing, and horizontal drains applied to the cut slopes the earthworks form. Designed and built as part of the same package so reinforcement, facing, and groundwater control are coordinated, not split across separate subcontracts.

Site drainage and erosion control

Surface drains, interceptor drains at cut crests, cascades and chutes, silt traps, and detention ponds. Erosion and Sediment Control Plan (ESCP) measures for DOE compliance, staged so the platform stays drained through the monsoon.

02 / Why the design and build route

Where single-contract delivery earns its keep.

  1. Single accountability. One entity is responsible for both the earthworks design and its construction. When platform levels, settlement, or slope performance are questioned, there is no design-versus-build blame gap and one party stands behind the outcome, backed by project-specific PI insurance on the design.
  2. Faster interface resolution. Ground rarely matches the SI report exactly. When a soft pocket, a hidden boulder, or a higher water table appears, the design team sits on the contractor side, so a revised cut line, a changed retaining type, or an added drain is redesigned in days rather than through the weeks a separate consultant loop can take.
  3. Buildability-led design. The cut and fill scheme is set around what the plant fleet and crew can actually build economically. Mass-haul is optimised for haul distance to cut external cart-away and import, and platform sequencing is set to keep working areas drained and trafficable.
  4. Compressed programme. Design and enabling works overlap under one programme. Site clearing, access, and ESCP silt-control works can start while platform design and authority submissions are finalised, because the designer and builder are one team.

08 / FAQ

Developers, consultants and main contractors usually ask:

What is earthworks design and build (turnkey EPC)? +
A procurement route where one contractor carries both the geotechnical and earthworks design responsibility and the construction. Instead of a consultant designing the platform, cut and fill scheme, retaining walls, slope treatment, and drainage, then tendering it to a separate earthworks contractor, the design-and-build contractor owns the whole chain from SI interpretation to as-built handover. It delivers a single accountable package for platform performance, under one CIDB G7 contract with project-specific PI insurance.
What does the single design and build contract cover? +
Five elements under one contract: (1) geotechnical and earthworks design (SI interpretation, cut and fill balance, platform levels, mass haul, slope geometry, temporary works); (2) bulk earthworks execution (strip, cut, fill and compaction, subgrade); (3) retaining structures (RE, gravity, cantilever, gabion); (4) slope stabilization (soil nailing, shotcrete, horizontal drains) on the cut slopes; and (5) site drainage and erosion control including ESCP for DOE compliance.
Why design and build rather than design-bid-build for earthworks? +
Three reasons. Single accountability: one party stands behind platform levels, settlement, and slope performance, so there is no design-versus-build blame gap. Faster interface resolution: when the ground differs from the SI report, the design team is on the contractor side, so redesign happens in days not weeks. Buildability-led design: the cut and fill scheme is set around what the plant and crew can build economically, with mass-haul optimised for haul distance. Design-bid-build still suits projects where a separate consultant must design for multiple tendering contractors.
What CIDB grade and categories do you hold? +
CIDB Grade G7 (highest contractor grade, no upper tender value limit). Categories CE01 (earthworks), CE08 (slope works), CE21 (geotechnical), B04 (specialised building works), M15 (mechanical). This combination lets one contract carry earthworks, retaining, and slope scope without splitting categories. PPK Registration 0120220822-WP104845, valid 22 August 2022 to 13 December 2027. ISO 9001:2015 certified, verifiable at cidb.gov.my.
What compaction and testing standard do you build to? +
95 percent MDD for general fill and 98 percent MDD for structural backfill, with maximum dry density set from the Proctor test per BS 1377. Field density confirmed by nuclear density gauge, sand-replacement, or DCP; CBR tested per specification. Execution follows BS 6031 and JKR-SPJ Section 2, with soil compaction to MS 1227. Records held under ISO 9001:2015 and submitted for sign-off before the layer above proceeds.
Do you carry PI insurance for the design responsibility? +
Yes. For design and build, project-specific Professional Indemnity (PI) insurance covers the design negligence exposure the contractor takes on by carrying the geotechnical and earthworks design. It sits alongside public liability, workmen's compensation, Contractor's All Risk (CAR), and Erection All Risk (EAR). Cover limits are scaled to project value, with certificate copies provided in the contract documentation.
Can you do earthworks design and build on a JKR or federal project? +
Yes. CIDB G7 with CE01, CE08, and CE21 means we are eligible for JKR submissions with no upper tender value limit, and we have delivered federal corridor scope on projects such as EKVE and ECRL Section 3. Submissions go through the state JKR office and the project consulting engineer, aligned to JKR-SPJ Sections 2 and 7, BS 6031, and Eurocode 7. ESCP and DOE compliance are handled in the same package. This is project experience and eligibility, not panel registration.
Which integrated earthworks projects have you delivered? +
Integrated packages combining earthworks with retaining, slope, and drainage include Bandar Serendah in Hulu Selangor (540,000 m3 backfill, 7 retaining structures, including an industrial facility platform for a national automotive-industry account), Eco Terraces Phase 2 in Semenyih, Mukim Bandar Batu 18 (120,000 m3 earthworks plus 11,000 m2 retaining wall with soil nailing), and Alam Impian Township in Shah Alam (earthworks, soil improvement, soil nailing, retaining walls, drainage, and a flood detention pond across Precincts 6 to 10). Further platforms include Mukim Sentul, Bandar Ulu Kelang Spine Road, and Welbuilder Phase 5B. Client names are not disclosed.
