Soil Nail Contractor in Mont Kiara.

Specialist soil nail contractor for Mont Kiara (Mont Kiara KL), Wilayah Persekutuan Kuala Lumpur. High-strength steel bars drilled into the slope at engineered angles, grouted in place, and faced with shotcrete or panels. Locks the soil mass against sliding. Coverage across Sri Hartamas, Solaris Mont Kiara, Kiara Hills, Plaza Mont Kiara, Hartamas Heights, Bukit Tunku, Damansara Heights, Sri Hartamas, Desa Park City. DBKL local council compliant. Designed and installed to BS 8006-2, FHWA-NHI-14-007, Eurocode 7, JKR. CIDB G7 (highest grade), ISO 9001:2015 certified. 100+ delivered projects, 5 million m² stabilized across Malaysia. Same-day site response from our office in Petaling Jaya, Selangor.
