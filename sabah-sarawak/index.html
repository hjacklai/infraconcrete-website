01 / East Malaysia context

Project-specific mobilization, specialist capability.

We don't maintain a permanent East Malaysia base, we mobilize for projects that justify the shipping and logistics. The proposition: bring Peninsular-grade specialist capability (six in-house slope systems, CIDB G7, ISO 9001:2015) to East Malaysian projects where local specialist coverage is thin.

Three project patterns make sense:

Hill-station and resort development. Mount Kinabalu access roads, Sabah highlands tourism platforms, Sarawak interior resort cuts. Specialist soil nailing and retaining systems on steep terrain.

Plantation road and estate access. Oil palm estate access through interior Sarawak and Sabah where slope works on plantation haul roads need durable engineering rather than recurrent re-grading.

Pan Borneo Highway and hydropower corridor. Federal-grade infrastructure where the slope works need to hold for design life through East Malaysia's heavy rainfall and high-relief terrain.

03 / Why us in East Malaysia

Bringing Peninsular grade across the South China Sea.

Six in-house systems, mobilized as a package

Soil nailing, guniting, rock bolting, rockfall barriers, retaining walls, horizontal drains, all Infraconcrete crews, shipped as one specialist package rather than scrambled local subcontracting.

CIDB G7 + ISO 9001:2015

Eligible for federal-level work and registered for cross-border project-specific works. Coordinated with JKR Sabah / JKR Sarawak at engagement.

Project-size threshold honest

Mobilization carries shipping, accommodation, and logistics costs. Practical minimum: ~5,000 m² slope works or 1,000 m² retaining walls. Below that, we'd direct you to local Sabah/Sarawak specialist contractors. Above that, we deliver.

Logistics planning at proposal stage

Crew shipping via Klang/Westport. Equipment to KK, Sepanggar, Kuching, Bintulu, Miri ports. Accommodation and rotation schedule built into the programme.
