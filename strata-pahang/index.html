01 / Pahang context

Hill-station slope works, federal corridor, monsoon-resilient design.

Pahang's project mix divides into hill-station and coastal-corridor zones. The hill stations (Genting, Cameron Highlands, Bukit Tinggi, Fraser's Hill) sit on weathered granite with significant slope angles, prone to rainfall-triggered shallow failures during the north-east monsoon. JKR slope classification typically III or IV for these elevations; STRATA products are extensively specified for slope reinforcement (StrataGrid PET for MSE walls and RSS), vegetated facings (StrataWeb HDPE), and drainage (StrataDrain geocomposite).

Coastal Pahang (Kuantan, Pekan) sits on alluvial deposits with marine clay zones near the coast; ECRL Section 3 passes through this corridor with extensive geosynthetic-reinforced embankments. Central Spine Road (CSR) connects Pahang to Kelantan through interior hills, with multiple slope stabilisation and reinforced-soil structures.

STRATA products on Pahang projects:

  • Genting and Cameron Highlands hillside platforms with MSE walls (StrataGrid + StrataWall or StrataBlock) and StrataWeb vegetated cut-slope facing.
  • Central Spine Road corridor with comprehensive geosynthetic-reinforced soil structures (federal-grade BS 8006 / FHWA-NHI-10-024 designs).
  • ECRL Section 3 embankments through Pahang with StrataTex HSR basal mats on alluvium.
  • Resort and theme-park hillside developments using full STRATA range.

03 / Logistics + design support

Highland mobilisation from PJ HQ.

  • Delivery to Genting / Bentong / Bukit Tinggi: 2-3 hours from PJ HQ via Karak Highway.
  • Delivery to Cameron Highlands / Fraser's Hill: 3-5 hours via federal road or B40 / B59 routes.
  • Delivery to Kuantan: 3-4 hours via federal road.

  • Design support: JKR Cawangan Cerun submissions, MBKT and local authority for resort developments.
