SGA-C asphalt reinforcement in Malaysia.

SGA-C is the StrataGrid Asphalt-Composite engineered geosynthetic for asphalt pavement reinforcement. High-tenacity polyester geogrid bonded with thermally stable non-woven fabric, coated with proprietary bituminous coating. 50 and 100 kN/m tensile strength in both machine and cross-machine direction. Roll widths to 3.75 m. Mitigates reflective cracking, enhances fatigue life, and reduces rutting. What it does

Mitigate reflective cracking + extend fatigue life.

SGA-C is engineered for two principal pavement performance benefits.

Performance benefitHow it works
Mitigates reflective crackingThe geogrid arrests upward propagation of cracks from underlying joints, distressed sub-layers, or pre-existing cracks in the substrate pavement. The strain energy that would propagate the crack is absorbed and redistributed in the reinforcement plane.
Enhances fatigue lifeHigh modulus and low elongation improve interlayer shear resistance and bond strength. Industry-leading Fatigue Improvement Factor (FIF) and Rut Improvement Factor (RIF) per STRATA technical data.
Reduces ruttingConfines lateral spreading of the upper asphalt layer under heavy axle loading; reduces surface deformation in high-traffic sections.
Improves trafficabilityDuring greenfield pavement construction phasing, the reinforced layer supports traffic at lower asphalt thickness with improved fatigue tolerance.

Installation

Eight-step site sequence.

  1. Pavement preparation. Mill or pre-clean per substrate condition. Address localised distress (potholes, deep cracks, settlement) per project specification before SGA-C placement.
  2. Tack coat application. Apply tack coat at design rate (typically 0.4 to 0.7 kg per square metre for SGA-C with bituminous coating). The proprietary coating bonds with the tack coat to achieve interlayer shear strength.
  3. Roll placement. Lay in longitudinal direction of traffic. Maintain alignment.
  4. Overlaps. Longitudinal overlap typically 100-150 mm per project specification; transverse overlap at roll ends per specification.
  5. Tension and alignment. Maintain straight rolls. Correct any wrinkling or creasing before paving.
  6. Anchorage. Edges and joints anchored per detail.
  7. Asphalt overlay placement. Direct overlay at typical paving temperatures (140-160 degrees C); the bituminous coating activates the bond on contact.
  8. Compaction and opening. Standard asphalt compaction practice. Cool and open to traffic per project specification.

Plant and crew. Standard asphalt paving plant. Standard paving crew with brief STRATA installation orientation. Production rate compatible with standard pavement programme.
