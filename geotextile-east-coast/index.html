01 / East Coast applications

ECRL corridor, Petronas Kerteh, coastal protection.

  • ECRL Section 3 embankments through Kelantan, Terengganu, East Pahang: StrataTex HSR basal mats on alluvium plus nonwoven PP separator under sub-base.
  • Petronas Kerteh O&G secondary containment: nonwoven PP 600-800 gsm cushion above HDPE geomembrane per GRI-GT12.
  • Coastal protection at Kemaman, Setiu, Besut: nonwoven PP underlay under rock revetment armor.
  • State road embankments through alluvial floodplains: nonwoven PP separator + HSR basal mat where alluvium is soft.
  • Interior hill-station slope drainage: nonwoven filter behind cut-slope drainage works.
