01 / Klang Valley applications

Core micropile use cases.

  • Hillside residential foundations: 200 mm micropile with 4-6 m rock socket into grade II/III weathered granite for condos and bungalows in Damansara, Ampang, Hulu Kelang.
  • Foundation underpinning: KL and PJ older commercial buildings adding floors or changing use; micropile transfers new load to deeper competent ground without disruption.
  • Basement extension underpinning: underpinning existing shallow footings in KL inner city for basement conversion; low-headroom rigs required.
  • Hillside slope stabilization: micropile wall + soil nailing combined systems on active landslide remediation in Ampang, Hulu Kelang, Hulu Langat.
  • Transmission tower foundations: micropile group under tower base for uplift and overturning resistance along the Klang Valley power corridor.
02 / Klang Valley ground conditions

Why micropile fits here.

Klang Valley geology is dominated by weathered granite (Kuala Lumpur Limestone outcrops in isolated zones). Residual soil + grade V/VI typically 5-15 m deep over grade III/II bedrock at variable depth. Micropile with rock socket is the standard foundation for hillside developments where bored pile would be uneconomic across variable rock depth.
