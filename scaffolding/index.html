03 / Why GI tubular

Material focus is deliberate.

GI tubular scaffolding (hot-dip galvanised steel tube with cup-head couplers) is the oldest and most versatile scaffolding system in the Malaysian market. It excels where system scaffolding (Cuplock, Ringlock, Kwikstage, Layher) struggles: irregular geometry, access scaffolding around existing structures, bridge and tunnel access, formwork support (shoring), independent and putlog scaffolds for facade and painting, chimney scaffolding, repair and renovation work.

Our focus on GI tubular is deliberate: it keeps inventory simple, lets us serve the widest range of job types, and keeps the material in high stock-turn circulation. For system scaffolding requirements (fast-build repetitive jobs on tall uniform structures), we refer to specialist system scaffolding contractors.

For the technical detail on GI tubular pipe specifications, couplers, jacks, and accessories, see our GI tubular scaffolding reference page.

05 / The three regulatory roles

PE, Competent Person, Competent Scaffolder: all three under one roof.

Malaysian scaffolding regulation (BOWEC Regulations 1986, OSHA 1994, DOSH Industry Code of Practice for Scaffolding) assigns three distinct roles to three distinct certified persons. For a legally compliant scaffold on a Malaysian site you need all three. Working with Infraconcrete, you get all three under one contract.

RoleCredential requiredWhat they doWhen involved
Professional Engineer (PE)BEM registration (Board of Engineers Malaysia), Civil / Structural specialisation. Form A under Engineers Act 1967.Design the scaffold, perform structural calculations, issue approved drawings, sign the Form of Approval (BOWEC Form), accept professional liability.Before erection starts, and whenever the scaffold is substantially modified. Required by BOWEC above statutory height threshold, by many client specifications regardless of height, and for all unusual configurations (cantilever, suspended, flying).
Competent Person (scaffold inspector)DOSH-recognised scaffold inspector training. Experience-based appointment under BOWEC Regulations.Inspect erected scaffolds, affix green / yellow / red DOSH tag, maintain inspection logbook, raise non-conformance reports.Immediately after erection, weekly thereafter, after modification, after weather event, before any high-load activity.
Competent Scaffolder (erection crew)DOSH-recognised scaffolder training with green card. Covers tube and coupler scaffold or specific system scaffold types.Erect, modify, and dismantle scaffolds under supervision; coordinate material lay-down and sequencing.Throughout erection, during any modification, and during dismantling. Site supervisor on the crew holds additional supervisor-level certification.

This matters because the three roles cannot be done by any single person; a PE is not legally a scaffold inspector, a competent scaffolder is not legally a PE, and so on. Many Malaysian jobs end up with three separate contractors or vendors covering the three roles, which creates coordination friction and gaps in accountability. Our full service bundle collapses all three into a single contract under our CIDB G7 ISO 9001:2015 QA framework, with role-appropriate certification evidence provided to the client or main contractor.
