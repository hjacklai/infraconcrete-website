Soil Nail Contractor in Iskandar Puteri.

Specialist soil nail contractor for Iskandar Puteri (Nusajaya), Johor (Iskandar Malaysia flagship zone). High-strength steel bars drilled into the slope at engineered angles, grouted in place, and faced with shotcrete or panels. Locks the soil mass against sliding. Coverage across Medini, Educity, Kota Iskandar, Puteri Harbour, EduCity, Legoland, Pinewood Iskandar Studios, Horizon Hills, Eco Botanic, Bukit Indah Iskandar, Sunway Iskandar. MBIP (Majlis Bandaraya Iskandar Puteri) local council compliant. Designed and installed to BS 8006-2, FHWA-NHI-14-007, Eurocode 7, JKR. CIDB G7 (highest grade), ISO 9001:2015 certified. 100+ delivered projects, 5 million m² stabilized across Malaysia. Same-day site response from our office in Petaling Jaya, Selangor.
