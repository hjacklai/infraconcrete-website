01 / What we deliver

Bulk earthworks, end to end.

Cut and fill

Bulk cut in residual soil and weathered rock, engineered fill placement in 200 to 300 mm lifts, and cut-to-fill balancing to minimise import and disposal. Level and grade held by GPS and total-station setting-out. Rock excavation by hydraulic breaker where ripping is not viable.

Mass haul and spoil

Haul-road construction, watering and dust control, and mass movement of cut to fill or to stockpile with articulated dump trucks. Import of engineered fill and cart-away of unsuitable material where the site cannot balance. Banksman-controlled haul routes and public-road interface.

Platform and building-pad creation

Development platforms and building pads formed to design level and tolerance for townships, industrial facilities, and hillside lots. Proof-rolling, sub-soil drainage, and geogrid or soil improvement interface where soft ground or fill depth requires it.

Subgrade, roads and drainage

Subgrade preparation and final trim to CBR specification, internal road formation, kerb and drain lines, culverts, and earth drains. Camber and formation held by motor grader, compacted and tested lift by lift for adoptable road standards.

Erosion and sediment control (ESCP)

Silt traps, sediment basins, wash-down bays, cut-off drains, and perimeter bunds installed before bulk works, then maintained and monitored for DOE (JAS) and JPS requirements through to landscaping handover. Temporary turfing and slope protection on exposed faces.

Integrated geotechnical scope

Earthworks that ties directly into retaining walls, reinforced-soil structures, soil nailing, and slope stabilization under one CIDB G7 contract, so the platform, the walls, and the slopes are one programme and one point of responsibility, not three subcontract interfaces.

08 / FAQ

Developers, QS, and main contractors usually ask:

What CIDB grade and categories do you hold for earthworks? +
CIDB Grade G7 (highest contractor grade, no upper tender value limit). Categories B04 (specialised building works), CE01 (earthworks), CE08 (slope works), CE21 (geotechnical), M15 (mechanical). CE01 is the primary category for bulk earthworks, cut and fill, and platform creation. PPK Registration 0120220822-WP104845, valid 22 August 2022 to 13 December 2027. ISO 9001:2015 certified quality management system.
Can I verify your CIDB grade independently? +
Yes. CIDB Malaysia operates a public contractor verification portal at cidb.gov.my. Search by company name (Infraconcrete Construction Sdn Bhd) or PPK Registration Number (0120220822-WP104845). The portal returns current grade, categories held, validity period, and registration status independent of any contractor representation. The ISO 9001:2015 certificate is also verifiable through the issuing certification body.
What earthworks scope do you self-perform? +
Site clearing and topsoil strip, bulk cut and fill, mass haul and spoil disposal, cut-to-fill balancing, import and placement of engineered fill, platform and building-pad formation, subgrade preparation and final trim, internal roads and haul roads, drainage and culverts, and the erosion and sediment control plan (ESCP). We integrate earthworks directly with retaining walls, soil improvement, and slope stabilization under one CIDB G7 contract, which removes the interface risk between separate earthworks and geotechnical packages.
How do you handle ESCP and DOE compliance on an earthworks site? +
Every earthworks package is delivered against an approved Erosion and Sediment Control Plan (ESCP), with silt traps, sediment basins, wash-down bays, and perimeter bunds installed before bulk works begin. We maintain the controls and monitoring records for DOE (JAS) and JPS requirements throughout the works. We have project experience working with local authorities including DBKL, MBPJ, MBSA, MBSJ, MPSJ, MPS, state JKR, DOE and JPS. We do not claim registration or approval by any council; compliance is delivered project by project against the approved plans.
What compaction control and testing do you achieve? +
General fill is compacted to 95 percent maximum dry density (MDD) and structural backfill to 98 percent MDD, placed in 200 to 300 mm lifts, per BS 6031, MS 1227 and JKR-SPJ Section 2. Field density is verified by nuclear density gauge, sand-replacement, or dynamic cone penetrometer (DCP), with maximum dry density established by Proctor and CBR to specification. Test records are logged under our ISO 9001:2015 quality system and submitted to the consultant for sign-off before the next lift or layer proceeds.
How fast can you mobilise earthworks plant and crew? +
Klang Valley (Selangor, KL, Putrajaya): same-day site response and daily crew availability. Penang, Kedah, Perlis, Perak, Negeri Sembilan, Melaka, Johor: 1 to 2 day mobilisation. Pahang, Kelantan, Terengganu: 2 to 3 day mobilisation with East-coast monsoon-period scheduling. Sabah, Sarawak, Labuan: project-specific mobilisation 3 to 7 days. The plant fleet includes 20-tonne and 50-tonne excavators, articulated dump trucks, bulldozer, motor grader, and the full compaction train (smooth-drum vibratory, padfoot, and pneumatic-tyre rollers).
How is bulk earthworks priced in Malaysia? +
Earthworks is priced on measured quantities and site conditions rather than a single headline rate. The main drivers are cut and fill volumes (m3), the cut-to-fill balance and whether fill must be imported or spoil carted off site, average haul distance, ground and rock conditions, compaction specification, dewatering and ESCP scope, and site access and permitted working hours. Send the earthworks drawings, cut-and-fill schedule, and soil report and we return a measured quotation with a construction programme. See the cost and programme guide for how earthworks cost and programme are built up.
