Sarawak · Pan Borneo Highway + Kuching + Miri + Kapit

Slope disaster prevention in Sarawak.

Sarawak combines the Pan Borneo Highway federal corridor across the state with hillside development at Kuching, Miri, Kapit, Bau, Padawan, and Mukah. The named-incident record includes the Kampung Baru Cina Kapit landslide of 26 December 2007 (2 deaths, 9 houses destroyed). JKR SHaRp monitors 3,633 Sarawak slopes as of November 2024. Geology is dominated by sedimentary sequences with localised igneous intrusions; weathering profiles produce thick residual soil mantles susceptible to monsoon-driven failure. Infraconcrete Construction Sdn Bhd mobilises to Sarawak within 3-7 days from Klang Valley HQ with documented federal corridor experience on the Pan Borneo Highway programme. CIDB G7 + ISO 9001:2015.
