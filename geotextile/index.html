01 / Two product families

Woven HSR + nonwoven.

StrataTex HSR, Woven (PET)

High-strength reinforcement woven from polyester yarns. Designed for tensile load. Basal mats under embankments on soft ground, fill reinforcement behind retaining walls, paved-road sub-base reinforcement. Grades 20-1000 kN/m. Roll widths typically 5 m, lengths 100-200 m.

Nonwoven (PP)

Needle-punched polypropylene. Designed for filtration & separation, not tensile. Separation between fill and subgrade, filter around perforated drains, drainage protection of geomembrane, cushion under riprap. Mass typically 150-600 g/m². Selected by AOS (apparent opening size) and permittivity.

02 / Where geotextile fits

Six core applications.

Basal reinforcement on soft ground

StrataTex HSR woven laid horizontally below an embankment fill on soft clay or peat. Spreads load, prevents bearing-capacity failure and lateral spread. Common on Malaysian port reclamation, ECRL/highway approaches over alluvial deposits, plantation embankments.

Separation

Nonwoven between sub-base aggregate and weak subgrade. Prevents fines from migrating up into the aggregate, preserves drainage, extends pavement life. Standard on plantation, mining haul, parking platforms.

Filtration

Nonwoven wrap around perforated subsurface drains, French drains, horizontal drains. Lets water pass, holds back fines. AOS sized to soil grading.

Drainage protection

Nonwoven cushion above geomembrane in landfill caps and pond liners. Prevents puncture by overlying drainage aggregate. Mass typically 400-600 g/m² for puncture resistance.

Reinforced fill behind walls

StrataTex HSR woven layered into compacted fill behind RE / MSE walls. Either as primary reinforcement (cheaper grades) or as secondary mat at the wall connection.

Erosion control under riprap

Nonwoven cushion below stone riprap on bridge piers, river training, channel lining. Prevents soil washout while letting water pass.

04 / FAQ

Engineers and developers usually ask:

Woven or nonwoven, which? +
Woven (StrataTex HSR) for tensile reinforcement. Nonwoven for separation/filtration/drainage. If it must take load → woven. If it must filter → nonwoven.
What tensile range? +
HSR woven: 20-1000 kN/m. Nonwoven: not tensile-rated, sized by g/m² and AOS.
Standards? +
BS 8006, Eurocode 7, ASTM D4595/D4751/D4491, ISO 10319/11058, AASHTO M288, JKR.
Basal reinforcement use case? +
Horizontal mat below embankment fill on soft ground (clay, peat, marine alluvium). Takes tensile load from lateral spread, prevents bearing failure.
Lead time? +
Common grades ex-stock or 1-2 weeks. Heavy basal mats 4-10 weeks ex-factory.
Supply only or install too? +
Both. Sole STRATA distributor + CIDB G7 installer = single-point accountability.
