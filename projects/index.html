Selected enabled spaces

What we delivered, where.

Each card below: the location, the condition before, and the scope delivered to enable the space. Client names and commercial details are not disclosed here out of respect for commercial confidentiality.

Selangor

Alam Impian Township

Alam Impian, Shah Alam, Selangor

Before: Five raw township precincts (6, 7, 8, 9, 10) needing full earthworks integration to create the development platforms.

What we did: Earthworks, soil improvement, soil nailing, retaining walls, drainage, and a flood detention pond delivered as a single coordinated multi-discipline scope across the five precincts. The space went from raw land to a fully prepared multi-precinct township platform.

Negeri Sembilan

Felda Bukit Jalor Connector Road

Gemas, Negeri Sembilan

Before: No direct road link between the Felda Bukit Jalor settlement and Gemas, restricting daily access for the community.

What we did: New connector road designed and constructed across the terrain, opening up year-round access between the settlement and the town.

Selangor

Retention Pond Desilting

Ulu Kelang area, Gombak, Selangor

Before: Two retention ponds (Ponds A and B) silted up over time, losing detention capacity and increasing downstream flood risk.

What we did: Site clearance and pond desilting to restore the original detention capacity, protecting downstream properties and infrastructure during monsoon.

Kuala Lumpur

Desa ParkCity Water Tank Substructure

Sector A, Desa ParkCity, Kuala Lumpur

Before: Sector A required an elevated water tank, but the substructure platform was geotechnically demanding (high vertical load, restrictive site footprint, urban-density constraints).

What we did: Piling, pile cap, reinforced earth wall, and earthwork delivered together as one integrated substructure scope for the elevated water tank.

Selangor

IWK Septic Tank Slope Rectification

Puchong Gateway, Puchong, Selangor

Before: Slope failure at an IWK sewage treatment facility, threatening continuous operation of critical sewerage infrastructure for the surrounding community.

What we did: Slope failure rectification works to restore stability and protect the live utility from further movement.

Selangor

Welbuilder Earthworks (Phase 5B)

Mukim Jeram, Kuala Selangor, Selangor

Before: A township development phase covering 10 lots (14214 to 14223) plus part of state land, needing earthworks and retention works to create the platform.

What we did: Earthworks and retaining wall delivered as a single scope across the lots, preparing the platform for development.

Negeri Sembilan

Mini Great Wall Retaining (Setul)

Mukim Setul, Seremban, Negeri Sembilan

Before: A twin business unit development on residual soil cut requiring a stepped retaining wall, both as engineered retention and as a visible architectural feature.

What we did: Stepped retaining wall (locally nicknamed the "Mini Great Wall" for its visible stepped form) delivering both engineered retention and a strong architectural presence at the site.

Kedah

Kampung Badlishah Junjong Road

Badlishah and Ayer Puteh Junjong, Kulim, Kedah

Before: Two kampung areas (Badlishah and Ayer Puteh Junjong) with limited road access, affecting daily community connectivity and access to services.

What we did: New village road construction across both kampung areas, opening up year-round access for residents and local services.

Selangor

Scientex Park Hulu Langat Earthworks

Mukim Hulu Langat, Selangor

Before: Three lots (53655, 53656, and 4955) of undeveloped land in Mukim Hulu Langat earmarked for a Scientex Park mixed development.

What we did: Earthworks across all three lots to create the developable platform for the mixed-development scope.

Kelantan

Lingkaran Tengah Utama (Kelantan)

Kuala Krai to Jambatan Sungai Lakit, Kelantan

Before: A federal road gap on the Lingkaran Tengah Utama alignment, with the section from Kg Laloh to the FT66 junction needing completion to connect the wider central-spine route.

What we did: Federal road works delivered for this central-spine section in Kelantan, contributing to the wider east-coast connectivity programme.

Selangor

Kwasa Damansara Common Infrastructure

Kwasa Damansara, PJU-4, Petaling, Selangor

Before: The Kwasa Damansara township at PJU-4 required Phase 2 of common infrastructure plus a water reservoir to support the township's progression.

What we did: Common infrastructure works plus the water reservoir delivered together as one integrated scope, unlocking the next phase of township development.

Selangor

Mukim Rawang Mixed Development

Rawang, Gombak, Selangor

Before: Multiple lots in Mukim Rawang earmarked for mixed development, each requiring earthworks platforms to enable subsequent construction.

What we did: Earthworks across the lots to deliver the multi-lot platform, ready for the mixed-development build-out.
