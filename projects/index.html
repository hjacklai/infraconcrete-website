Federal expressway · Selangor

EKVE Project

East Klang Valley Expressway, Selangor

Challenge: A federal expressway corridor with cut slopes and rock faces along the alignment, needing slope and geotechnical works under live traffic without halting the network.

Solution: Delivering soil nailing, guniting, rock bolting, rock netting, jersey barriers, and rock-face stabilisation across the alignment, sequencing the works with staged closures and traffic management approved by the Highway Authority.

Federal rail · Pahang · Terengganu · Kelantan

ECRL Section 3

Pahang, Terengganu, and Kelantan corridor

Challenge: Two parallel contract sections on the East Coast Rail Link Section 3 alignment, covering 64 km (CH87+642 to CH151+365) and 42 km (CH88+467 to CH130+718), needing slope and geotechnical works on cut slopes and embankments through varied terrain, in coordination with the rail builder.

Solution: Delivering slope stabilisation and geotechnical scope across both contract sections under live-corridor logistics, contributing to opening up the national east-coast rail link.

Federal road · Kelantan

Lingkaran Tengah Utama (Kelantan)

Kuala Krai to Jambatan Sungai Lakit, Kelantan

Challenge: A federal road gap on the Lingkaran Tengah Utama alignment, with the section from Kg Laloh to the FT66 junction needing completion to close the central-spine connectivity for the east coast region.

Solution: Building federal road works for this central-spine section in Kelantan, contributing to the wider east-coast connectivity programme.

Community road · Kedah

Kampung Badlishah Junjong Road

Badlishah and Ayer Puteh Junjong, Kulim, Kedah

Challenge: Two kampung areas (Badlishah and Ayer Puteh Junjong) with limited road access, affecting daily community connectivity and access to services.

Solution: Constructing a new village road across both kampung areas, opening up year-round access for residents and local services.

State road · Negeri Sembilan

Felda Bukit Jalor Connector Road

Gemas, Negeri Sembilan

Challenge: No direct road link between the Felda Bukit Jalor settlement and Gemas, restricting daily access for the community.

Solution: Constructing a new connector road across the terrain, opening up year-round access between the settlement and the town.

Township · Selangor

Alam Impian Township

Alam Impian, Shah Alam, Selangor

Challenge: Five raw township precincts (6, 7, 8, 9, 10) needing fully integrated multi-discipline scope to become developable platforms ready for residential build-out.

Solution: Delivering earthworks, soil improvement, soil nailing, retaining walls, drainage, and a flood detention pond as a single coordinated multi-discipline scope across the five precincts.

Industrial platform · Hulu Selangor

SAMAMARA Bandar Serendah Industrial Facility

Bandar Serendah, Hulu Selangor

Challenge: A national auto-industry platform at Bandar Serendah needing an integrated platform for a spare parts centre with mezzanine, canteen, electrical pavilion (SSU 11 kV), guard houses, and bus stop.

Solution: Delivering platform earthworks and multi-structure site preparation for the auto-industry platform, enabling all the planned structures to take their positions on the developed site.

Township · Selangor

Kwasa Damansara Common Infrastructure (Phase 2)

Kwasa Damansara, PJU-4, Petaling, Selangor

Challenge: The Kwasa Damansara township development at PJU-4 needing Phase 2 of common infrastructure plus a water reservoir to support the township's continued progression.

Solution: Delivering common infrastructure works plus the water reservoir together as one integrated scope, unlocking the next phase of township development.

Bandar Ulu Kelang Spine Road

Bandar Ulu Kelang, Gombak, Selangor

Solution: Building a 100ft internal spine road plus supporting earthworks, drainage, and retaining wall across the development phases.

Reinforced soil wall + hillside slope · Selangor

Section U10, Shah Alam

Section U10, Mukim Bukit Raja, Shah Alam, Selangor

Challenge: A residential and commercial development on Section U10, Mukim Bukit Raja, needing both engineered retention at the platform edge for a 147-unit residential phase and multi-stage slope stabilisation across cut faces created during platform development.

Solution: Delivering Phase 4A reinforced soil wall (147-unit phase) plus four stages of Centralfields slope stabilisation (Stages 1, 2, 3, and 4) across the development, setting up the slope-stable platform for stage-by-stage build-out of the residential and commercial scope.

Hillside earthworks · Selangor

Eco Terraces Earthworks (Phase 2)

Mukim Bandar Batu 18, Semenyih, Hulu Langat, Selangor

Challenge: Phase 2 of a hillside development at Lot 24738 in Mukim Bandar Batu 18 needing earthworks and retention to extend the developable platform across the hillside.

Solution: Delivering earthworks and retaining wall as one scope for the Phase 2 hillside platform extension.

Retaining wall · Negeri Sembilan

Mini Great Wall Retaining (Setul)

Mukim Setul, Seremban, Negeri Sembilan

Challenge: A twin business unit development on residual soil cut needing a stepped retaining wall, both as engineered retention and as a visible architectural feature on the site.

Solution: Building a stepped retaining wall (locally nicknamed the "Mini Great Wall" for its visible stepped form) that gives both engineered retention and a strong architectural presence at the site.

Slope rectification · Selangor

IWK Septic Tank Slope Rectification

Puchong Gateway, Puchong, Selangor

Challenge: Slope failure at an IWK sewage treatment facility, threatening continuous operation of critical sewerage infrastructure for the surrounding community.

Solution: Carrying out slope failure rectification works to restore stability and protect the live utility from further movement.

Utility substructure · Kuala Lumpur

Desa ParkCity Water Tank Substructure

Sector A, Desa ParkCity, Kuala Lumpur

Challenge: Sector A needing an elevated water tank, with a substructure platform that is geotechnically demanding (high vertical load, restrictive site footprint, urban-density constraints).

Solution: Delivering piling, pile cap, reinforced earth wall, and earthwork together as one integrated substructure scope for the elevated water tank.

Drainage works · Selangor

Retention Pond Desilting (Ponds A and B)

Ulu Kelang area, Gombak, Selangor

Challenge: Two retention ponds (Ponds A and B) silting up over time, losing detention capacity and increasing downstream flood risk during monsoon.

Solution: Carrying out site clearance and pond desilting to restore the original detention capacity, protecting downstream properties and infrastructure during heavy rainfall.
