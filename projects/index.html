Reading the portfolio.

This is a selection of recently delivered and currently active client engagements, 2022 to 2026. It is not exhaustive of every project Infraconcrete has delivered since founding in 2018, and it does not include the federal landmark references covered separately on our federal project case studies page (Pan Borneo Highway, KL to Singapore HSR, MRT2 stations, Highland Towers and Bukit Antarabangsa as historical landslide references).

Client confidentiality

Client names and contract values are not disclosed here. We respect the commercial sensitivity of each client relationship. For tender pre-qualification submissions, full project references including client letters, contract values, and dated certificates of completion are provided directly to the requesting agency or main contractor upon request.

Geographic coverage

Projects across Selangor, Wilayah Persekutuan Kuala Lumpur, Kedah, Pahang, Kelantan, Terengganu, and Negeri Sembilan. Mobilisation for federal infrastructure and tier-1 hillside developments across the Klang Valley is same-day. Project-specific mobilisation for East Malaysia (Sabah, Sarawak, Labuan).

Scope range

Federal expressway and rail slope works, township earthworks and infrastructure, hillside slope stabilisation (soil nailing, MSE wall, retaining wall, guniting), reinforced soil walls, slope rectification, water tank substructures, retention pond works, and road construction. Designed and installed in-house by Infraconcrete to BS, EN, ACI, FHWA, and JKR specifications.
