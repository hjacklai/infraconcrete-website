Five functions

What non-woven geotextile does.

FunctionWhat it achieves
SeparationPrevents migration of fines from soft subgrade into overlying granular layers. Maintains structural integrity of the engineered course above. Standard at the subgrade-to-sub-base interface on weak or variable Malaysian residual soils.
FiltrationPermits free water flow while retaining soil particles. Prevents washout of fines from drainage systems and embankment toes. Wraps drainage gravel in French drains, behind chimney drains, around perforated pipe.
DrainageIn-plane water flow through the geotextile thickness. Supports lateral drainage in landfill caps, behind retaining walls, in blanket drains.
Soil stabilisationTensile reinforcement at the soil-aggregate interface controlling rutting on weak subgrade under construction or operational loading.
ProtectionCushioning layer protecting geomembranes, pipes, and other geosynthetics from puncture by underlying or overlying angular material. Standard above and below HDPE liner in landfill engineering.
Applications

Common Malaysian use cases.

  • Road construction. Separation between subgrade and granular sub-base.
  • Embankment construction. Separation and drainage layer at the base of fill over soft soils.
  • Retaining wall backfill. Vertical filter behind drainage gravel or chimney drain.
  • Slope drainage works. Blanket drain wrap, surface drainage, French drain wrap.
  • Landfill engineering. Protection layer above and below geomembrane liner.
  • Pipe trench wrap. Protection of perforated drainage pipe and gravel filter.
  • Erosion control. Under hard armour, rip-rap, gabion mattress.
  • Coastal and river revetment. Separation between subgrade and rock armour.
ST vs HSR

Non-woven vs woven STRATA geotextile.

PropertyStrataTex ST (Standard Tensile, non-woven)StrataTex HSR (High Strength Reinforcement, woven)
ManufacturingNeedle-punched short polymer fibres into fabric matWoven high-tenacity polyester yarn
Primary functionSeparation, filtration, drainage, protectionHigh-tensile basal reinforcement
Tensile strengthModest, sufficient for separation/protection role50-500 kN/m for reinforcement design
PermittivityHigh (water flow through thickness)Moderate (water flow controlled by weave)
Cost basisCost-efficient area-based pricingHigher unit cost reflecting tensile capacity
Typical specification driverCBR puncture resistance + permittivityLong-term design tensile strength
