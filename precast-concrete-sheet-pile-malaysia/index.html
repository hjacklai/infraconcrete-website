01 / What it is

The permanent wall that does not corrode.

A precast concrete sheet pile is a factory-cast reinforced or prestressed section with an interlocking tongue-and-groove joint. Driven side by side, the sections form a continuous wall that retains soil or water, controls seepage, or armours a river bank or shoreline. Because the body is concrete, the wall carries no corrosion allowance and needs no coating or cathodic protection, which is why it is the default choice for permanent structures that sit in water or aggressive ground.

As a concrete specialist, Infraconcrete treats the sheet pile wall as a concrete structure first: the right grade and cover for the exposure class, crack control designed to Eurocode 2, durable interlocks, and a capping beam that ties the wall into the permanent works. The result is a wall built to outlast the design life with minimal maintenance.

Corrosion-free

No rust, no coating, no cathodic protection. Ideal for rivers, brackish water, coastal and aggressive soils where steel loses thickness.

Long permanent life

Designed to the exposure class with adequate cover and crack control for a long, maintenance-light service life.

Mass and stiffness

High mass and stiffness suit revetment and water-retaining work, resisting flow, wave action and earth pressure.

Finished face

Cast with a clean or patterned face, so the permanent wall needs no cladding for riverfront and public realm work.

03 / Types and profiles

Reinforced and prestressed sections.

Reinforced concrete (RC) sheet pile

Conventional reinforcement for shorter, lower-moment walls. Economical for river bank protection, shallow retaining and drainage channel lining. Typical flat or rectangular section with a tongue-and-groove joint.

Prestressed concrete (PSC) sheet pile

Pre-tensioned strand puts the section into compression, so it resists handling and driving stresses, controls cracking, carries higher bending moment, and reaches longer lengths. Suited to deeper cut-off walls, taller retaining and marine work.

Profiles and joints

Flat, rectangular, W-section and trapezoidal profiles, selected for the bending demand. The tongue-and-groove or grooved joint interlocks adjacent piles into a continuous wall and controls seepage; joints are grouted or sealed where a tight hydraulic cut-off is required.

Sizing

Width, thickness and length are design-led from the retained height, water level, surcharge and ground. Section, embedment and reinforcement are set by the earth and water pressure to Eurocode 2 and Eurocode 7.

05 / Installation

How concrete sheet pile goes in.

Concrete sheet piles are driven to the design toe with the method matched to the ground, with care taken to protect the pile head and interlocks.

  • Impact driving with cushion and helmet: a drop or hydraulic hammer with a cushioned helmet protects the concrete head while delivering penetration in firm ground.
  • Water jetting assist: a jet relieves dense sand ahead of the toe so the pile advances without over-stressing the section. Common for river and coastal sand.
  • Vibratory driving: for granular soils where a vibrator can seat the section quickly, with soft-start to limit vibration near structures.
  • Pre-auger and drive: a pre-bored relief hole for hard ground, boulders or vibration-sensitive sites, then drive or jet the pile through the loosened soil.

Verticality, interlock engagement and toe level are checked continuously, a trial pile confirms drivability and refusal risk from the site investigation, and the wall is finished with a reinforced concrete capping beam. Most concrete sheet pile walls are permanent and are not extracted.

07 / FAQ

Developers, engineers and JPS contractors usually ask:

What is a precast concrete sheet pile? +
A factory-cast reinforced or prestressed concrete section with an interlocking tongue-and-groove joint, driven side by side to form a continuous wall that retains soil or water, controls seepage, or protects a river bank or shoreline. Concrete is used over steel where the wall is permanent and sits in water or aggressive ground, because it does not corrode and needs little maintenance.
Precast concrete or steel sheet pile, which is better? +
It depends on the job. Precast concrete suits permanent works in water or aggressive soil (river bank, coastal, cut-off walls, flood bunds) because it is corrosion-free and needs no coating or cathodic protection. Steel suits temporary works (basement, cofferdam) where sheets are extracted and reused, and deep high-moment walls. For a permanent waterfront or river wall, concrete usually wins on whole-life cost.
Where are concrete sheet piles used in Malaysia? +
River bank and canal protection for flood mitigation and river rehabilitation, coastal and shoreline revetment, seepage cut-off walls for ponds, dams and canals, permanent earth retaining, drainage channel lining, and marine edge walls. The corrosion-free, long-life nature of concrete makes it the default for permanent water-retaining structures.
How are concrete sheet piles installed? +
Driven with an impact hammer using a cushion and helmet to protect the head, often with water jetting to assist in dense sand, or vibrated in granular soil. Pre-augering is used in hard ground or near sensitive structures. The piles interlock through a tongue-and-groove joint, grouted where a tight seepage cut-off is needed. Most are permanent and not extracted.
What is the design life? +
A properly designed precast concrete sheet pile is a permanent, long-life element. With concrete cover, crack control and durability designed to Eurocode 2 and the exposure class, it carries no corrosion allowance in the way steel does, so it suits marine, brackish and aggressive-ground environments.
RC or prestressed concrete sheet pile? +
Reinforced concrete (RC) suits shorter, lower-moment walls and is economical for river bank and shallow retaining. Prestressed concrete (PSC) carries higher bending moment, resists handling and driving stresses, controls cracking and reaches longer lengths, so it suits deeper cut-off walls, taller retaining and marine work. The choice is design-led.
How much does it cost in Malaysia? +
Cost is driven by section size, length, installation method and access, so a firm figure needs the wall height, soil report and site constraints. It is usually quoted per square metre of wall face or per linear metre, supplied and installed. For permanent water works the whole-life cost is often lower than steel once protection and maintenance are counted.
Can you design, supply and install? +
Yes. Install-only to the consultant's design, or design and build where Infraconcrete carries the geotechnical and structural design plus supply and installation under one contract. We provide method statements, lifting plans, RAMS and an inspection and test plan, coordinated with the appointed consultant.
Do they form a watertight wall? +
The tongue-and-groove interlock gives a close joint that controls seepage for river bank, bund and retaining work. Where a tight hydraulic cut-off is required, the joints are grouted or sealed and the wall is designed as a seepage cut-off to the hydraulic gradient, routine for dam, pond and canal cut-offs.
