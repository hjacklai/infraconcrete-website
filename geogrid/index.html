01 / Two product families

Uniaxial PET and biaxial PP.

StrataGrid Uniaxial (PET)

High-tenacity polyester (PET) yarns coated with PVC. One-directional tensile capacity. Use: vertical MSE walls, segmental block walls, reinforced soil slopes, steep embankments, bridge abutments. Tensile: 30, 50, 80, 120, 160, 200, 300, 400 kN/m grades.

StrataGrid Biaxial (PP / SGB)

Punched-and-drawn polypropylene (PP) sheet. Bidirectional tensile + apertures lock aggregate. Use: paved road sub-base reinforcement, unpaved haul road stabilization, basal reinforcement under embankments on soft soil. Lateral aggregate confinement is the primary mechanism.

STRATA also produces specialised welded steel grids and 3D geocell systems for niche heavy-duty work, available on project demand from the manufacturer.

02 / Where geogrid fits

Five core applications.

MSE walls / RE walls

StrataGrid uniaxial PET tied back into compacted reinforced fill, faced by precast concrete panels (StrataWall) or modular blocks (StrataBlock). Federal highway, rail, hillside development. Heights 1-25 m.

Reinforced soil slopes (RSS)

StrataGrid uniaxial PET layered into compacted fill at 30°-70° face angle. Vegetatable face (StrataSlope). Cheaper than vertical wall when ROW allows. Highway widening, hillside platforms, landfill closure.

Basal reinforcement on soft ground

High-strength biaxial or unidirectional StrataGrid laid horizontally below an embankment fill on soft clay or peat. Spreads load, prevents bearing-capacity failure and lateral spread. Port reclamation, ECRL/highway approaches.

Paved & unpaved roads

StrataGrid Biaxial under road sub-base. Reduces aggregate thickness 20-50% on weak subgrade, extends pavement life. Plantation access, mining haul, parking platforms.

Steep embankments & cut platforms

StrataGrid layered into engineered fill on hillside platforms (where excavation cost dominates). Common on Klang Valley hillside developments and federal road widenings.

Bridge abutments (Shored MSE)

Hybrid: soil nail at upper cut + StrataGrid-reinforced fill below. Trestle / bridge deck founded on the reinforced fill. To FHWA-NHI-14-007 / BS 8006.

05 / FAQ

Engineers and developers usually ask:

Uniaxial or biaxial, which? +
Uniaxial PET for vertical/sloped reinforced soil (walls, slopes). Biaxial PP for horizontal under-pavement reinforcement (road basal).
What tensile range? +
PET uniaxial 30-400 kN/m. Biaxial PP 20-40 kN/m (apertures, not tensile, drive performance).
Standards? +
BS 8006, Eurocode 7, ASTM D6637, ISO 10319/13431, AASHTO LRFD, FHWA-NHI-10-024, JKR.
UV / creep / chemical resistance? +
UV-stabilised; short field exposure window. Creep allowed via ISO 13431 reduction factors. PET stable in most soils; PP for saturated/alkaline conditions.
Lead time? +
Common grades ex-stock or 1-2 weeks. Heavy grades 4-8 weeks ex-factory. Specialised projects 8-12 weeks.
Supply only or install too? +
Both. Single-point accountability, supplier + designer + installer.
