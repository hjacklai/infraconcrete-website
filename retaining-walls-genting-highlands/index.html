01 / Genting Highlands context

Why retaining wall matters here.

Genting Highlands is the Pahang highland resort destination at 1,800 m elevation. Resort tower platforms on steep granite slopes, hillside cuts requiring extensive soil nailing + rock netting + rockfall barriers (very high JKR Class IV slopes), tunnel portal works (EKVE Genting Sempah). Cool climate, mossy forest geology.
