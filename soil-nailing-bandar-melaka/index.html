Soil Nailing · Bandar Melaka · Melaka (state capital)

Soil Nail Contractor in Bandar Melaka.

Specialist soil nail contractor for Bandar Melaka (Melaka City), Melaka (state capital). High-strength steel bars drilled into the slope at engineered angles, grouted in place, and faced with shotcrete or panels. Locks the soil mass against sliding. Coverage across Bandar Melaka, Ayer Keroh, Cheng, Kelebang Besar, Bukit Beruang, Tanjung Kling, Hang Tuah Jaya, Alor Gajah border, Universiti Teknikal Malaysia Melaka (UTeM). MBMB (Majlis Bandaraya Melaka Bersejarah) local council compliant. Designed and installed to BS 8006-2, FHWA-NHI-14-007, Eurocode 7, JKR. CIDB G7 (highest grade), ISO 9001:2015 certified. 100+ delivered projects, 5 million m² stabilized across Malaysia. Same-day site response from our office in Petaling Jaya, Selangor.
