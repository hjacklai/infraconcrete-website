01 / Penang context

Hillside developments, coastal alluvium, federal infrastructure.

Penang Island is the steeper terrain with Penang Hill rising to 833 m; Air Itam, Tanjung Bungah, Tanjung Tokong, Balik Pulau host hillside developments on weathered granite (residual soil with high fines). MBPP Hill Slope Guideline categorises slopes by JKR Class I-IV and prescribes acceptable engineering responses; STRATA products feature prominently in the accepted solutions register.

Mainland Penang (Seberang Perai, Butterworth, Bukit Mertajam) sits on alluvial deposits transitioning to marine clay near the coast. Bayan Lepas industrial-zone projects often require StrataTex HSR basal mats on soft ground; Penang Port reclamation uses heavy HSR grades for tall embankments on marine alluvium. Penang Hill developments commonly specify MSE walls (StrataGrid + StrataBlock or StrataWall) for hillside platforms, and StrataWeb vegetated facings for steep cut slopes.

Federal infrastructure includes the Penang Bridge approach corridors, Penang Sentral, and several JKR road projects across the island and mainland.

02 / Authority spec for Penang

MBPP and JKR Cawangan.

  • MBPP Penang Hill Slope Guideline: Class I-IV slope categorisation with corresponding engineering response. STRATA products (StrataGrid for reinforcement, StrataWeb for vegetated facing) are accepted within the prescribed engineering solutions register.
  • MBPP and MBSP submission practice: requires consulting C&S or geotechnical engineer-of-record submission with slope stability analysis, design report, and material certificates.
  • JKR Cawangan Cerun for federal works: JKR-SPJ Section 7 specifications, AASHTO M288, BS 8006.
  • Penang Port Authority (LPP) for port reclamation projects requiring marine-grade specifications.
