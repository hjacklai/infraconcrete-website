Soil Nailing · Keningau · Sabah (interior)

Soil Nail Contractor in Keningau.

Specialist soil nail contractor for Keningau (Keningau Sabah), Sabah (interior). High-strength steel bars drilled into the slope at engineered angles, grouted in place, and faced with shotcrete or panels. Locks the soil mass against sliding. Coverage across Keningau Town, Tambunan, Tenom, Apin-Apin, Sook, Bingkor, Nabawan border, Crocker Range National Park. MDK (Majlis Daerah Keningau) local council compliant. Designed and installed to BS 8006-2, FHWA-NHI-14-007, Eurocode 7, JKR. CIDB G7 (highest grade), ISO 9001:2015 certified. 100+ delivered projects, 5 million m² stabilized across Malaysia. Same-day site response from our office in Petaling Jaya, Selangor.

01 / Keningau context

Why soil nail matters here.

Keningau is the Sabah interior major town in the Crocker Range valley. Palm oil, agriculture, growing administrative centre. Crocker Formation challenges (sandstone-shale interbedded, weak shale partings), hillside cuts on rolling interior terrain.

02 / Coverage

Sub-localities around Keningau.

Same-day site response across the broader Keningau catchment: Keningau Town, Tambunan, Tenom, Apin-Apin, Sook, Bingkor, Nabawan border, Crocker Range National Park. Office in Petaling Jaya, Selangor (cross-causeway / east-coast / north response on project-specific mobilization).
