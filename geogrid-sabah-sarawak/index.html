01 / Borneo geogrid applications

Pan Borneo Highway, peat embankments, mining.

  • Pan Borneo Highway corridor: extensive StrataGrid Biaxial PP under sub-base + StrataTex HSR woven basal mats over peat (Sarawak interior peat deposits 5-15 m thick). See road base reinforcement design guide.
  • Crocker Range hillside developments (Sabah): MSE walls with PET geogrid; StrataWeb vegetated facing on steeper cuts. Sabah moderate seismicity (PGA 0.05-0.10g) requires explicit seismic check.
  • Mining tailings storage facilities (gold, copper across Sabah and Sarawak): StrataGrid PET in embankment reinforcement combined with HDPE geomembrane basal liner.
  • Plantation access roads across oil palm plantation network: biaxial PP geogrid + nonwoven separator over weak peat subgrade.
  • Bintulu SCORE industrial belt: platform construction over alluvial soft ground using combined biaxial geogrid + StrataWeb + nonwoven separator.
02 / Peat embankment specialism

High-strength PET basal reinforcement.

Sarawak peat deposits (5-15 m thick) and Sabah lowland peat present extreme subgrade conditions. Standard engineering response combines StrataTex HSR woven PET basal mat (typically 400-800 kN/m grade) with vertical drains (PVDs) for consolidation. The basal mat handles construction-stage lateral force per Rowe-Soderman analysis; PVDs accelerate long-term consolidation. See geotextile design guide for the design framework.

03 / Authority + logistics

State-level authorities and Borneo logistics.

  • JKR Cawangan Sabah / Sarawak: JKR-SPJ Section 7 adapted for Borneo conditions.
  • State PWD (Jabatan Kerja Raya Sabah / Sarawak): state-level roadworks.
  • DBKK, DBKU: KK and Kuching local authority.
  • Logistics: stock-holding at STRATA depots in Kuching and KK; sea-freight for project-specific heavy grades. Lead times 2-4 weeks (common), 6-12 weeks (heavy basal mat).
  • Mobilisation: Infraconcrete teams mobilise to Borneo for medium-large projects.
