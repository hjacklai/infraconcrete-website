Soil Nail Contractor in Bukit Mertajam.

Specialist soil nail contractor for Bukit Mertajam (BM Penang), Pulau Pinang (mainland - Seberang Perai Tengah). High-strength steel bars drilled into the slope at engineered angles, grouted in place, and faced with shotcrete or panels. Locks the soil mass against sliding. Coverage across Cherok Tokun, Permatang Tinggi, Pekan Bukit Mertajam, Auto City, Sungai Lembu, Berapit, Padang Lalang, Alma. MBSP local council compliant. Designed and installed to BS 8006-2, FHWA-NHI-14-007, Eurocode 7, JKR. CIDB G7 (highest grade), ISO 9001:2015 certified. 100+ delivered projects, 5 million m² stabilized across Malaysia.
