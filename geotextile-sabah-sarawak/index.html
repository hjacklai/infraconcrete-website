01 / Borneo applications

Pan Borneo Highway, mining, plantation roads.

  • Pan Borneo Highway peat embankments: StrataTex HSR 400-800 kN/m basal mats combined with PVDs for consolidation. Rowe-Soderman analysis governs design tensile.
  • Mining tailings storage facilities (TSF): nonwoven PP 600-800 gsm cushion above HDPE geomembrane basal liner per GRI-GT12.
  • Plantation access roads over peat / soft alluvium: nonwoven separator + biaxial geogrid combined system; StrataTex HSR added on very weak subgrades.
  • Bintulu SCORE industrial belt: platform basal mats over alluvial soft ground.
  • Coastal erosion protection: nonwoven PP underlay under rock revetment.
02 / Peat specialism

High-strength HSR woven PET basal mat.

Sarawak peat deposits (5-15 m thick, su 3-10 kPa typical) and Sabah lowland peat present extreme subgrade conditions. Standard engineering response combines StrataTex HSR woven PET 400-800 kN/m basal mat with vertical drains (PVDs) for consolidation. The basal mat handles construction-stage lateral force per Rowe-Soderman analysis; PVDs accelerate long-term consolidation over the design service life. See the geotextile design guide for the analysis framework.
