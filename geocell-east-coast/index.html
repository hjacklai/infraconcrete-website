01 / East Coast applications

ECRL corridor, channel lining, coastal protection.

  • ECRL Section 3 cut-slope vegetated facing: StrataWeb 100-150 mm depth for embankment + cut surface erosion control during establishment.
  • Stormwater channels in Kelantan / Terengganu floodplains: concrete-filled StrataWeb for high-shear protection during monsoon flood events.
  • Coastal erosion protection at Kemaman, Setiu, Besut: StrataWeb 150-200 mm combined with rock revetment armoring above.
  • Interior Kelantan / Hulu Terengganu hillside slope facings: monsoon-resilient vegetated cuts on weathered granite.
  • Petronas Kerteh access road working platforms: load support over alluvial subgrade.
