01 / When PE certification is required

Four trigger conditions.

  1. Height threshold: under the Factories and Machinery (BOWEC) Regulations 1986, scaffolds exceeding certain heights (commonly 40 feet / 12 metres but check current regulation) require PE sign-off.
  2. Unusual configuration: cantilever, suspended, flying, bridge-over, or complex geometry scaffolds often require PE regardless of height.
  3. Client specification: many main contractors and government bodies specify PE sign-off on all scaffolding regardless of statutory threshold. JKR, LLM, and large private developer specs commonly do this.
  4. High live load or hazard: scaffolds carrying heavy loads (shoring, formwork support), scaffolds in high-wind locations, or scaffolds in hazardous environments.
02 / The certification process

Five steps.

  1. Scope definition: you send scaffold geometry, loads, duration, location. We confirm what PE scope you need.
  2. Design review: BEM-registered PE reviews proposed scaffolding against BS EN 12811 and NASC TG20. Loading, bracing pattern, tie design, foundation.
  3. Calculations and drawings: structural calculations issued, approved drawings prepared, specifications tabulated.
  4. Form of Approval: PE signs Form of Approval and submits per BOWEC requirement.
  5. As-built verification: after erection, PE verifies the as-built matches the approved design (via inspection or site visit by the PE or their delegate).
03 / What we need from you

Minimum submission pack.

  • Scaffold footprint drawing and height.
  • Loading schedule (what the scaffold will carry; dead and live load estimate).
  • Project duration and location.
  • Site conditions (ground, wind exposure, nearby structures).
  • Any client specification referencing PE requirement.
