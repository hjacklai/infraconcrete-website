Careers

Build geotechnical engineering in Malaysia.

Infraconcrete is hiring across engineering, site, commercial, safety, and trade functions. Specialist slope and geotechnical contractor, CIDB G7 (highest grade), ISO 9001:2015. Federal references: EKVE, ECRL Section 3, Central Spine Road, Pan Borneo. Smaller in-house teams mean engineers see the full design-to-installation cycle, not just one slice. Office in Damansara Jaya, Petaling Jaya. Site rotations across Klang Valley plus project-specific deployments to Pahang, Penang, Johor, Sabah, Sarawak.

01 / Why work here

What makes Infraconcrete different.

Federal-grade projects

EKVE (450,000 m² of slope protected), ECRL Section 3 (320,000 m of drilled drainage on a 64 km rail alignment), Central Spine Road (65,000 m² of integrated slope works), Pan Borneo Highway. The kind of engineering scope that builds a real CV.

End-to-end visibility

No subcontracting on the geotechnical specialist scope. The same engineer who reviewed the design watches the drill go in, sees the grout cube tests, signs off the pull-out test report. Engineers leave Infraconcrete with full-stack geotechnical experience, not just one trade slice.

Standards-native

BS / EN / Eurocode / FHWA / AASHTO / ACI / JKR fluent on every project. You learn to read and apply the actual standards, not just follow templates.

CIDB G7 + ISO 9001:2015

Highest CIDB grade. Federal panel registered. ISO 9001:2015 quality framework on every project regardless of size. Your CV gets the credibility of a tier-1 specialist contractor.

Group support

Part of the Panthera Group. Sister company Starwall Sdn Bhd is the sole STRATA Geosystems Malaysia distributor. You get exposure to both the contracting and the geosynthetic supply side, useful if your career interest spans both.

Engineering ownership culture

We hire engineers who want to own design intent, not just execute it. Site engineers are encouraged to challenge the consultant's spec where field conditions warrant. Founders are engineers; technical discussion happens at the top.

Klang Valley base, MY-wide deployment

Office-based roles work from PJ. Site-based roles rotate through Klang Valley projects plus periodic 1 to 4 week deployments to ECRL alignments, Pan Borneo corridor, hill-station works (Genting, Cameron), and federal expressway scopes outside KL. Travel allowance, accommodation, and rotation schedule per project.

Professional development sponsored

BEM Graduate Engineer registration, IEM membership, CIDB Green Card, ACI CP-60 nozzleman certification, IRATA rope-access, safety qualifications all supported by the company.

02 / Roles we hire for

Where you might fit.

Engineering (design and site)

  • Graduate engineer trainee: 12-month structured rotation across design office, site engineering, and QS / commercial. For civil / geotechnical / construction management fresh graduates from accredited universities.
  • Site engineer (2-5 years): day-to-day site engineering on slope, retaining, and ground works. Method statement, ITP, HIRARC, site supervision, daily records, weekly progress reporting to the consultant and project manager.
  • Senior site engineer (5-10 years): lead site engineer on tier-1 projects, multi-trade coordination, consultant interface, change control, programme management at site level.
  • Geotechnical design engineer (5+ years): slope stability, MSE wall, soil nail wall, ground anchor, micropile, retaining wall design. Tools: PLAXIS, Slope/W, Slide, Geo5, Settle3D, MSEW, ReSSA. Output: design calculations, drawings, specifications.
  • Project engineer / project manager (8-12+ years): end-to-end project ownership across multiple concurrent projects, client interface, commercial management, programme, P&L responsibility.

Specialist trades (site-based)

  • Drilling operator: rotary / rotary-percussive rig operation for soil nail, ground anchor, micropile, horizontal drain holes. Daily drill log discipline. Manufacturer training provided.
  • Shotcrete nozzleman: wet-mix and dry-mix sprayed concrete application. ACI CP-60 (Nozzleman Certification) sponsored. EFNARC qualification supported.
  • Grouting technician: cement grout mixing (colloidal), tube-a-manchette pressure grouting, daily QA testing (Marsh cone, mud balance, cube strength).
  • Rope-access technician: IRATA-certified rope access for face-installation works (soil nail, rock bolt, mesh, shotcrete on steep faces). IRATA Level 1 sponsored.
  • Foreman / site supervisor: day-to-day crew supervision, equipment coordination, safety enforcement, quality control on the face.

Commercial and support functions

  • Quantity surveyor (3-8 years): tender QS, contract admin, monthly progress claims, variation orders, final account, payment schedule.
  • Contracts manager / senior QS (8+ years): contract risk review, claims management, dispute resolution, commercial strategy.
  • HSE officer / manager: Site safety compliance, HIRARC, weekly safety walk, monthly safety report. CIDB Green Card, NIOSH SHO / Site Safety Supervisor certification supported.
  • Surveyor (land / setting-out): total station, GPS RTK, drone survey for slope topo, weekly setting-out, monthly volume audit, as-built drawings. Licensed Land Surveyor (LJUB-registered) preferred for senior roles.
  • Document controller / project admin: ITP, method statements, material certs, test reports, monthly progress reports, as-built documentation per ISO 9001:2015 framework.

Internships and graduate programme

  • Undergraduate internship (10-24 weeks): civil / geotechnical / construction management students. Rotation across design office, active site, QS / commercial. Industrial placement letter provided for university.
  • Graduate trainee programme (12 months): structured rotation for fresh graduates from accredited universities. Mentor assigned. Path to permanent engineer role on completion.
  • Apprenticeship for tradespeople: on-job training for drilling, grouting, shotcrete, rope-access roles. Combined with formal trade qualifications where applicable.
03 / How to apply

The application process.

1. Send your CV

Email ifrconcrete@gmail.com with subject line containing the role of interest (e.g. "Site Engineer Application") plus a brief cover note (1-2 paragraphs on why you want to work at Infraconcrete and what you can bring). Or WhatsApp +60 16-428 1214.

2. Initial acknowledgement (within 3 working days)

HR / hiring manager acknowledges your application and confirms next steps. If the role is currently open and your CV aligns, you'll be invited to a phone screen.

3. Phone screen (30 min)

Initial conversation about your background, why Infraconcrete, what role you're interested in, salary expectations, notice period. Mostly fit conversation.

4. Technical interview (45-60 min)

Role-specific deep dive. For engineering roles: technical discussion on slope stability, retaining wall design, or construction method (depending on role). For trades: practical capability assessment. For commercial: contract / claims walkthrough. For HSE: HIRARC and safety scenario discussion.

5. Final interview with founders (45 min)

Conversation with the founders. Fit, values, growth direction, expectations on both sides. Salary and package finalised here.

6. Offer letter (within 1 week)

Written offer with role, salary, benefits, reporting line, start date, probation, professional-development sponsorship details. Sign and return.

What to include in your CV: employment history with project names where relevant, qualifications, certifications, language fluency (Bahasa Malaysia / English / Mandarin / Tamil), software skills (CAD, geotechnical software, MS Project, MS Office), any portfolio of project photos or design samples.

04 / What we look for

Across all roles.

Engineering integrity

You take responsibility for what you stamp. You raise concerns when site conditions don't match the design. You read the actual BS / EN / FHWA / JKR standards rather than relying on hearsay. You document your work so a stranger could rebuild it from your records.

Pace and grit

Geotechnical site work is physical, monsoon-affected, schedule-pressured, and sometimes emergency-deployed (post-landslide call-outs). We hire people who can deliver under those conditions without losing engineering rigour.

Communication discipline

Consultant interface, client reporting, authority submission, internal handoff all need clear written and verbal communication. English (technical) is the working language; Bahasa Malaysia is useful site-side. Documentation matters.

Care for safety and quality

Geotechnical works have real safety risks (working at height, drilling, grouting, live traffic). ISO 9001 quality framework requires document discipline. People who treat safety and QA as optional don't last here.

05 / FAQ

Common questions.

Do you currently have openings? +
Yes. We are continuously hiring across engineering (graduate trainee, site engineer, senior engineer, design engineer, project manager), specialist trades (drilling operator, nozzleman, grouting technician, rope-access), commercial (QS, contracts), safety (HSE), survey, and admin roles. Send CV with role of interest to ifrconcrete@gmail.com or WhatsApp +60 16-428 1214 and we'll match you to the most relevant opening.
Do you take fresh graduates? +
Yes. Graduate engineer trainee programme runs annually. Open to civil / geotechnical / construction management graduates from accredited universities. 12-month structured rotation. Mentor assigned. Path to permanent engineer role on successful completion.
What about internships? +
Yes. 10-24 week internships for undergraduates. Rotation across design office, site, and QS / commercial. Industrial placement letter for university requirements. Apply 2-3 months before your intended start date.
Site-based or office-based? +
Depends on role. Design engineers, QS, contracts, document control, HR / admin are office-based at the PJ HQ. Site engineers, supervisors, foremen, drilling operators, nozzleman, grouting techs, rope-access are site-based. Senior engineers and project managers split office and site time roughly 40 / 60. Travel allowance and accommodation provided for non-KV site postings.
Do you sponsor BEM / IEM / CIDB / ACI / IRATA qualifications? +
Yes. BEM Graduate Engineer registration, IEM membership, CIDB Green Card, ACI CP-60 Nozzleman Certification, IRATA Level 1 rope access, NIOSH Site Safety Supervisor (SSS) / Safety and Health Officer (SHO), and other recognised industry certifications are supported financially and with study leave.
What's the typical career path? +
Graduate engineer trainee (year 1) → site engineer (years 2-5) → senior site engineer or design engineer (years 5-10) → project engineer / project manager (years 10+) → senior management. Trades: tradesperson → foreman → site supervisor → trade manager. Commercial: junior QS → QS → senior QS → contracts manager.
Working hours and overtime? +
Standard office hours Mon-Fri 8.30am-5.30pm with Saturday morning 8.30am-12.30pm (some roles). Site hours follow project schedule (typically 7am-5pm or 8am-6pm depending on site). Overtime per Malaysian Employment Act. Federal panel work occasionally requires extended hours during critical activities (e.g. shotcrete pours, possession-window rail works).
Salary range? +
Competitive with market for the Klang Valley specialist geotechnical contractor segment. Discussed at interview based on role, experience, and qualifications. Annual increment plus performance bonus. Health insurance, SOCSO, EPF, public holidays per Malaysian Employment Act.
How long does the application process take? +
Initial acknowledgement within 3 working days. Phone screen within 1-2 weeks. Technical interview within 1-2 weeks of phone screen. Final interview within 1-2 weeks of technical. Decision and offer within 1-2 weeks of final interview. Total: typically 4-8 weeks from CV submission to offer letter, faster for urgent openings.
Do you hire non-Malaysian candidates? +
We hire Malaysian citizens and permanent residents directly. Foreign engineers with specialist geotechnical experience may be considered subject to work permit feasibility (Employment Pass Category I/II via MDEC / Immigration Department). Discuss at first contact.
