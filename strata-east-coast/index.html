01 / East Coast context

ECRL corridor, Petronas Kerteh O&G, monsoon-resilient design.

The East Coast spans three states with distinctive soil and project profiles. Kelantan is largely alluvial floodplain with seasonal flooding; Terengganu coastal areas (Kerteh, Dungun, Paka) host Petronas oil-and-gas operations requiring salt-spray durability spec; Hulu Terengganu, Gua Musang, and interior Kelantan rise into the Main Range with weathered granite slopes. The north-east monsoon (November-March) is the dominant climatic driver for design, with peak rainfall events that can exceed 300 mm/day.

STRATA products on East Coast projects:

  • ECRL Section 3 embankments through Kelantan, Terengganu, and east Pahang corridor with StrataTex HSR basal mats on alluvium and StrataGrid Biaxial under road sub-base.
  • Petronas Kerteh O&G corridor with geomembrane secondary containment (HDPE for hydrocarbon resistance) and StrataGrid for embankment / platform reinforcement.
  • Monsoon-resilient slope works in interior Kelantan / Hulu Terengganu using StrataWeb vegetated facing on cut slopes.
  • Coastal protection at Kemaman, Setiu, Besut with StrataTex HSR + woven geotextile under rock revetment.

03 / Logistics + monsoon design

Cross-state mobilisation and seasonal sequencing.

  • Delivery to Kuala Terengganu / Kuantan: 6-7 hours from PJ HQ via federal road.
  • Delivery to Kota Bharu: 7-9 hours via Karak / Gua Musang or East-West Highway.
  • Delivery to Kerteh / Petronas zone: 6-7 hours.
  • Monsoon planning: dry-window construction (April-October) preferred for major geosynthetic placements. Our project team coordinates programme with monsoon timing.
  • Authority: JKR Cawangan Cerun for federal works, MPKT Kuala Terengganu, MPKB Kota Bharu, state DOE for waste-containment projects.
