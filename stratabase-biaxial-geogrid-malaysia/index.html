StrataBase · Extruded PP rigid biaxial · Road base + railway + airport + embankment

StrataBase biaxial geogrid in Malaysia.

StrataBase is the STRATA Geosystems extruded polypropylene rigid biaxial geogrid for structural reinforcement of paved and unpaved roads, railway ballast, airport runways, and embankments on weak soils. Aperture interlock with granular fill plus high tensile strength in two orthogonal directions reduces granular base thickness 25-50 percent, extends pavement maintenance cycles, and supports trafficking over otherwise unusable weak subgrade. Supplied in Malaysia by Starwall Sdn Bhd (sole STRATA Geosystems Asia distributor). Installed by Infraconcrete Construction Sdn Bhd, CIDB G7 + ISO 9001:2015.

How it works

Two reinforcement mechanisms.

MechanismEffect
Aperture interlock with granular fillAggregate particles partially penetrate the rigid apertures and lock against the ribs, transferring load from the granular layer into the geogrid plane through frictional and mechanical interlock.
Membrane tension reinforcementHigh tensile modulus in two orthogonal directions resists tension under wheel or embankment loading, distributing concentrated load across a larger footprint and reducing stress on the underlying subgrade.
Catenary support at embankment baseWhen placed at the base of an earth embankment over weak foundation, StrataBase sustains catenary tension under embankment self-weight plus loading, allowing construction without prior subgrade improvement.

Project economics

What it saves.

  • Granular base thickness reduction 25-50 percent. Same or higher design CBR achieved with less aggregate. Saves cost of aggregate, transport, placement.
  • Subgrade improvement avoidance. Where the alternative would be soil replacement, lime stabilisation, or PVD + consolidation period, StrataBase plus reduced granular section may eliminate the need entirely for low-criticality projects.
  • Extended maintenance cycle. Reduced stress on subgrade plus reinforced base extends structural pavement life, reducing through-life maintenance.
  • Construction time reduction. Less aggregate to place plus avoidance of subgrade improvement period accelerates programme.
  • Trafficking over otherwise unusable subgrade. Supports construction traffic where unreinforced subgrade would rut and bog.
StrataBase vs StrataGrid

Different products. Different applications.

PropertyStrataBaseStrataGrid (uniaxial PET)
ManufacturingExtruded polypropylene plate with punched aperturesKnitted polyester yarn with proprietary coating
JunctionRigid integral junction (single-piece extrusion)Knitted intersection (flexible)
DirectionBiaxial (two orthogonal directions, equal strength)Uniaxial (single direction primary strength)
Application orientationHorizontal (under roads, under embankments, under hardstanding)Vertical (in MSE walls, in reinforced soil slopes)
Reinforcement mechanismAperture interlock + membrane tensionMembrane tension along reinforcement length
Design lifePer project specification under granular coverOver 100 years per STRATA technical data

Many Malaysian projects use both: StrataBase under the road or platform base, StrataGrid in the retaining wall or RSS behind. See StrataGrid for vertical wall and slope reinforcement.
