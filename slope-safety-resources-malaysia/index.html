By incident

Historical events: engineering analysis.

Highland Towers 1993 →

48 deaths. Block 1 collapse Ulu Klang. The civil-liability precedent (Steven Phoa cases).

Pos Dipang 1996 →

44 deaths. Debris flow through Orang Asli settlement. Catchment-scale risk to vulnerable communities.

Bukit Lanjan 2003 →

35,000 m3 NKVE rockslide. RM 836m cost. Directly formed JKR Cawangan Kejuruteraan Cerun in 2004.

Bukit Antarabangsa 2008 →

4 deaths. Triggered the JKR Slope Engineering Manual 2010.

FELCRA Semungkis 2011 →

16 deaths (15 children) at Madrasah Al-Taqwa orphanage. Vulnerable-occupancy siting precedent.

Tanjung Bungah 2017 →

11 deaths. Penal Code Section 304A criminal liability precedent. Triggered MBPP 2nd Edition 2020.

Batang Kali 2022 →

31 deaths at Father's Organic Farm campsite. Triggered KPKT GPP Tapak Khemah 2023.
