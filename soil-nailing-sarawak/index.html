01 / Sarawak context

Pan Borneo, SCORE manufacturing, oil palm interior.

Largest state in Malaysia by area. Three project patterns drive soil nailing demand: Pan Borneo Highway corridor (~786 km, federal infrastructure with extensive cut slopes through Borneo highlands), Bintulu SCORE industrial platforms (aluminium smelters, polysilicon, manganese alloy plants - vibration-sensitive precision platforms), and Kuching mixed-use + oil palm estate works. Coastal exposure on the Miri-Bintulu corridor demands corrosion-resistant material spec.
