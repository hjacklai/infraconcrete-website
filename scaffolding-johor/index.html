Scaffolding in Johor.

GI tubular scaffolding service across Johor: Johor Bahru, Iskandar Puteri, Pasir Gudang, Senai, Skudai, Kulai, Pontian, Batu Pahat, Muar, Pengerang. Delivery from our Klang Valley yard via the North-South Expressway (4-5 hour delivery). A la carte rental, erection, PE certification, DOSH green tag inspection, or full service bundle. We focus on Johor civil construction and M&E sectors; we do not serve the Pengerang petrochemical sector (specialist operator certifications required). CIDB G7, ISO 9001:2015.

  • Industrial: Pasir Gudang, Senai, Skudai, Kulai, Tanjung Pelepas port area.

02 / Common Johor project types

What we typically see.

  • Iskandar high-rise commercial and residential (hotel, condo, office).
  • Industrial facility M&E in Pasir Gudang and Senai industrial estates (non-petrochemical).
  • Factory painting and maintenance.
  • Mall and commercial fit-out in JB city centre.
  • Residential renovation across Johor Bahru and surrounds.
  • Government facility maintenance (JKR Johor, local councils).
03 / Logistics from Klang Valley

Delivery via NSE.

Delivery from our Klang Valley yard is typically 4-5 hours via the North-South Expressway. Mobilisation 1-2 weeks after contract award.
