01 / Klang Valley context

Why Klang Valley earthworks needs integrated engineering.

The Klang Valley is where most of Malaysia's hillside township and industrial-platform earthworks happen. Steep developer estates, federal expressway cut and fill, and MRT and LRT cut slopes all sit on tropical residual and lateritic soils that need proper compaction control and drainage to carry permanent load. Cut-and-fill on sloping ground here is rarely a standalone job; it comes bundled with slope stabilization, retaining walls, and drainage.

02 / What we deliver

Earthworks plus everything that holds it.

Cut and fill, mass haul

Cutting from high ground, hauling to fill, compaction to 95 to 98 percent MDD. Heavy plant for large volumes; field density testing per layer.

Platform and hillside creation

Sloping ground transformed into buildable platforms. Stabilized cut slopes, retaining walls between platforms, slope protection.

Drainage and erosion control

Perimeter drains, sediment traps, horizontal drains, and DOE-compliant ESCP during the works.

Integrated slope and retaining

Soil nailing, shotcrete and retaining walls delivered in the same sequence as the earthworks, under one contract.
