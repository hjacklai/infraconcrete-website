Soil Nail Contractor in Miri.

Specialist soil nail contractor for Miri (Miri Sarawak), Sarawak (north coast). High-strength steel bars drilled into the slope at engineered angles, grouted in place, and faced with shotcrete or panels. Locks the soil mass against sliding. Coverage across Miri Town, Senadin, Kuala Baram, Lutong, Bandar Baru Permyjaya, Pujut, Tudan, Curtin University Malaysia campus, Miri-Sibuti Coral Reefs. MBM (Majlis Bandaraya Miri) local council compliant. Designed and installed to BS 8006-2, FHWA-NHI-14-007, Eurocode 7, JKR. CIDB G7 (highest grade), ISO 9001:2015 certified. 100+ delivered projects, 5 million m² stabilized across Malaysia. Same-day site response.

Miri is the Sarawak north coastal city, oil & gas industry hub (Petronas, Shell legacy). Curtin University campus, dense urban growth at Senadin / Tudan, hillside developments at Bukit Suai. Northeast Monsoon (Nov-Mar) drainage design critical.
