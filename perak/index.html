01 / Perak's slope context

Limestone state, NSE corridor.

Perak is dominated by two slope-shaping forces: the limestone karst geology of the Lembah Kinta valley around Ipoh, and the North-South Expressway corridor that runs the length of the state. Both generate steady slope work demand. Add active and decommissioned mining, hill-station development around Cameron Highlands' Perak side, and the Sitiawan-Lumut industrial-port belt, and Perak becomes one of Malaysia's most slope-diverse states.

Three project patterns dominate:

Limestone karst stabilization. Ipoh and the Lembah Kinta. Cliffs, caves, and steep cut faces above access roads, residential developments, and active operations. Rock bolting, rock netting, dynamic netting, rockfall barriers as integrated systems. The technical bar is high.

NSE corridor maintenance and remediation. Slopes along the North-South Expressway and parallel federal roads. Post-monsoon emergency remediation, planned upgrades, slope drainage interventions where pore pressure is the failure driver.

Sitiawan / Lumut / Manjung industrial-port belt. Manufacturing and logistics platforms with coastal exposure. RE walls, RC retaining, drainage, durability-class material specification.
