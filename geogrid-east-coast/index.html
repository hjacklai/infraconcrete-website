01 / East Coast geogrid applications

ECRL corridor, Petronas Kerteh, interior slope works.

  • ECRL Section 3 embankments through Kelantan, Terengganu, East Pahang: StrataGrid Biaxial PP under road sub-base + StrataTex HSR basal mats on alluvium. One of Southeast Asia's largest single-corridor geosynthetic deployments.
  • Petronas Kerteh O&G corridor: StrataGrid PET in MSE walls and platform reinforcement; combined with HDPE geomembrane for hydrocarbon secondary containment.
  • Interior Kelantan / Hulu Terengganu slope works: StrataGrid PET in MSE walls + StrataWeb vegetated facing for monsoon-resilient cut slopes.
  • Coastal protection at Kemaman, Setiu, Besut: StrataTex HSR + woven geotextile under rock revetment combined with StrataGrid for embankment reinforcement.
  • State and federal roads: biaxial PP under road sub-base over alluvial subgrade.

03 / Monsoon-resilient design

Programme around dry windows.

Major geogrid placements and MSE wall fill operations are sequenced for dry window (April-October) where possible. North-east monsoon (November-March) peak rainfall events exceed 300 mm/day; exposed geogrid covered within manufacturer UV window. Erosion control mat (coir, TRM) supports vegetation establishment on RSS faces during monsoon transition.
