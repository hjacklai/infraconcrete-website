Soil Nail Contractor in Petaling Jaya.

Specialist soil nail contractor for Petaling Jaya (PJ), Selangor. High-strength steel bars drilled into the slope at engineered angles, grouted in place, and faced with shotcrete or panels. Locks the soil mass against sliding. CIDB G7 (highest grade), ISO 9001:2015 certified. 100+ delivered projects, 5 million m² stabilized across Malaysia. Same-day site response.
