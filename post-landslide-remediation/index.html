01 / Four-phase model

From emergency to durable rebuild.

Phase 1: Emergency stabilisation (days)

Horizontal drains for immediate stability gain. Surface erosion control. Temporary slope-toe protection. Site safety + traffic management. Mobilization 2-5 days from authorisation.

Phase 2: Investigation + design (weeks)

Geotechnical investigation: boreholes, piezometers, inclinometers, slope-stability analysis. Remedial design with the appointed C&S or geotechnical consultant.

Phase 3: Structural remediation (months)

Soil nailing, retaining wall reconstruction, surface protection (guniting), permanent drainage. Phased to maintain access and safety throughout.

Phase 4: Monitoring (ongoing)

Piezometers, inclinometers, surface markers verify the remediation works. Hand-over with monitoring plan; ongoing inspection contracts available.

04 / Four-phase remediation path

From active failure to handover.

01

Emergency stabilization (24-72 hr)

Halt the active mechanism. Tarp the head scarp to stop infiltration. Install temporary horizontal drains to relieve pore pressure. Cordon and signage. Photo record + drone survey for documentation.

02

Diagnosis (1-2 weeks)

Inclinometer + piezometer to confirm slip surface depth and groundwater condition. Topographic survey of the failure mass. Geotechnical investigation if not on file. Geotechnical consultant interprets root cause.

03

Re-design

Consultant produces remedial design. Typical components: regrade head scarp, soil nail / rock bolt the new geometry, install permanent horizontal drains, surface protection (shotcrete + erosion control), drainage at slope toe. We input on buildability.

04

Build & verify

Construction per design. Continuous monitoring during works. Post-completion monitoring (12-24 months) to verify FoS recovery. Defects period instrumentation.

05 / Common failure modes & remedies

What we usually find and fix.

Rainfall-induced shallow slide

Saturated soil mantle (1-3m depth) detached on a translational plane. Remedy: regrade, soil nail, surface protection, drainage. Most common Malaysian failure.

Deep-seated rotational

Failure surface 5-15m deep, often on weathered rock contact. Remedy: long soil nail / ground anchor, deep horizontal drains, possibly ground replacement. Larger scope.

Wedge / planar in rock

Discontinuity-controlled rock slide. Remedy: rock bolts to discontinuities, mesh drape, shotcrete to seal, scaling. Common in Pahang / Penang granite.

Retaining wall failure

RE wall, gabion, or RC wall has translated or rotated. Remedy: re-anchor (tieback or soil nail), re-key, repair facing, drain behind wall.
