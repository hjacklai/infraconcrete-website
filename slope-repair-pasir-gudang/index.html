Slope Repair / Rectification Contractor in Pasir Gudang.

CIDB G7 (highest grade), ISO 9001:2015 certified. 100+ delivered projects, 5 million m² stabilized across Malaysia. Same-day site response from our office in Petaling Jaya, Selangor.
