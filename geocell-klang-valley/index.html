01 / Klang Valley applications

Hillside vegetated cuts, urban channels, working platforms.

  • Hillside vegetated slope facing (Damansara Heights, Cheras, Hulu Selangor, Semenyih, Cyberjaya hillside developments): StrataWeb 100-150 mm depth with topsoil and hydroseed; DBKL hill land controls and MBPJ submissions.
  • Urban stormwater channels: concrete-filled StrataWeb 100-150 mm for hydraulic shear up to 800 Pa per FHWA-HEC-15.
  • Working platforms over weak subgrade (Klang industrial, Pulau Indah, Selangor logistics zones): StrataWeb 100-200 mm filled with crushed aggregate.
  • Gravity retaining walls: stacked StrataWeb for low retaining structures with vegetated face.
  • Channel toe protection on storm drainage outlets.

See geocell design guide for slope-facing anchorage detail by angle and K-factor framework for load support.
