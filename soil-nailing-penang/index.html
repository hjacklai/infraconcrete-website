01 / Penang context

Granite hillside, MBPP submission, industrial growth.

Three drivers: Penang Hill / Air Itam / Tanjung Tokong / Tanjung Bungah hillside developments cut into granite - soil nailing is the dominant slope reinforcement when rock conditions don't justify rock bolting; Bayan Lepas industrial parks (Free Trade Zone, semiconductor / E&E manufacturing platforms); and Seberang Perai infrastructure and industrial growth corridor. MBPP requires the Penang Hill Slope Guideline submission for projects above 76m (250 ft) elevation - we author and stamp these as part of the design-build package.
