06 / Engagement Process

Investigation to handover, five stages.

Step 01

Investigation

Geotechnical site investigation, slope mapping, and risk profiling. We start with the ground, not with a fixed solution in mind.

Step 02

Design

Site specific solution design in collaboration with the consulting engineer. We optimize for buildability, durability, and cost.

Step 03

Engineering

Technical drawings, method statements, and specification finalised. Submitted for consultant and authority approval.

Step 04

Construction

Our in-house crew and fleet build to spec under CIDB G7 certification, with HSE audits weekly on site.

Step 05

Monitoring

Post construction load testing and performance monitoring. Defect liability and long term maintenance options.

Soil Nailing

Reinforcing existing slopes with high strength steel.

Slender steel nails drilled at engineered angles, then grouted to lock the soil mass against sliding.

  • 02Drilling at engineered angles

Guniting · Shotcreting

Spraying high-strength concrete on slope and rock faces.

A protective skin sprayed at high velocity, resisting tropical rainfall, surface erosion, and weathering.

View method
  • 01Surface preparation
  • 02Mix design to spec
  • 03High velocity application
  • 04Curing and integrity check
+images/capabilities/cap-rock-bolting.jpgAwaiting photo
Rock Bolting

Anchoring unstable rock to competent strata.

Tensioned steel bolts that pin loose rock back into the mass, eliminating rockfall on cut slopes and tunnels.

View method
  • 01Geological mapping
  • 02Drilling to depth
  • 03Bolt installation and anchoring
  • 04Tensioning and inspection
+images/capabilities/cap-rock-netting.jpgAwaiting photo
Rock Netting

Containing falling rocks on steep slopes.

High-tensile steel mesh anchored over the rock face, catching loose debris before it reaches the road below.

View method
  • 01Site evaluation
  • 02Anchor installation
  • 03Netting deployment
  • 04Load inspection

Slope Stabilization

Layering multiple techniques on complex slopes.

Soil nailing, guniting, bolting, and netting designed as one integrated system for slopes that need more than one approach.
