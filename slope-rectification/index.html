01 / Slope rectification - the JKR / government term

What "slope rectification" means in Malaysian tender language.

Slope rectification is the term used in JKR (Public Works Department), MOW (Ministry of Works), and most Malaysian government tender documents to describe engineered remedial works on a distressed or non-compliant slope. It encompasses the same scope a private developer might call "slope repair" or "slope reinforcement", but used in a regulatory context.

If you've received a slope rectification directive from JKR Slope Engineering Branch, a local authority (DBKL, MBPP, MBPJ, MBSA, etc.), or a federal agency after annual inspection, that directive will specify: (1) the slope ID and location, (2) the JKR slope class (I-IV), (3) the remedial requirements (e.g., "install soil nailing to FoS ≥ 1.4" or "reinstate horizontal drains and shotcrete facing"), (4) the submission timeline, and (5) the documentation expected (Method Statement, design calculations stamped by registered P.Eng., as-built drawings, test certificates).

We are CIDB G7 panel-registered and have delivered slope rectification packages for federal expressways under live traffic, federal rail corridors, and state-road authorities. The documentation standard is the same regardless of project size - same QA/QC, same submission format.

02 / Slope rectification scope

Standard solution package for JKR-spec rectification.

Investigation phase

Topographic survey, borehole programme (rotary core to 30 m typical), in-situ tests (SPT, vane shear), lab classification (Atterberg, sieve, triaxial), groundwater observation wells. SI report deliverable in JKR / Eurocode format.

Design phase

Slope-stability analysis (limit equilibrium / FE per JKR option), reinforcement sizing (soil nail length, spacing, capacity to JKR / FHWA), drainage layout, durability spec. Drawings + calcs stamped by registered P.Eng.

Construction phase

Drainage installation first (horizontal drains, surface channels), then structural reinforcement (soil nailing / rock bolting / ground anchors), then surface protection (guniting / rock netting / re-vegetation). Live-traffic safe-work practices when applicable.

Handover phase

As-built drawings, pull-out test certificates (load-tested per JKR), grout test certs, drainage flow logs, photographic record, maintenance schedule. Defect liability per CIDB / consultant spec, typically 12-24 months.
