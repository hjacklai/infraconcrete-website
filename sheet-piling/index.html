01 / What we install

Steel sheet pile + soldier piling.

Steel sheet piles in U-section, Z-section, and combined wall systems, Larssen / Arcelor type AU and AZ profiles for Malaysian basement and marine work, lighter LX series for shallow temporary excavation support. Plus soldier piling (driven H-piles or bored piles with timber / precast concrete lagging) for granular soils where sheet pile drivability is poor.

Three installation methods, selected per ground conditions and constraints:

Driven sheet pile

Diesel or hydraulic impact hammers for hard ground. Fast, cost-effective. Used where vibration is acceptable, open sites, away from sensitive structures.

Vibrated sheet pile

Hydraulic vibratory hammers for granular soils. Faster than driving, less noisy. Standard for most Malaysian basement and excavation works.

Pre-augered / silent

Pre-augered holes, water-jet assist, or press-in methods (Giken-style) for sensitive urban sites with vibration limits or close proximity to existing structures.

Soldier piling

H-piles or bored piles with timber/precast lagging, for granular ground, cohesionless fill, or where sheet pile sections cannot be driven.

02 / Use cases

Where sheet piling fits.

Basement excavation in urban sites

KL, PJ, JB urban basement walls, temporary support during excavation, extracted at backfill. Vibration limits and proximity to existing structures dictate method.

Cofferdam for bridge piers, structures

Temporary water-retaining structure for in-river or in-sea construction. Designed to withstand water pressure plus tidal range.

Marine and riverside permanent

Permanent retaining at port jetties, riverbanks, marina walls. Coated or sacrificial-thickness for design life. Common in Pasir Gudang, Klang, Penang ports.

Slope toe and tieback retention

Sheet pile at the toe of a slope, often combined with tied-back upper retaining systems. For sites where slope geometry alone won't hold.

04 / Sections, Steel Grades, and Installation Methods

Profiles, materials, drive techniques.

Sheet pile specification is design-led across three independent dimensions: section profile (Z, U, AU, AZ, combi-wall), steel grade (S 240 GP through S 460 GP), and installation method (driven, vibrated, pressed, pre-augered).

Profile families

Z-section sheet piles (Arcelor AZ, larsen LZ): the modern Malaysian default for high-bending-moment applications. Asymmetric profile with the section modulus maximised. Driven or vibrated. Common pairs: AZ 18 to AZ 50, with section modulus 1840 to 5015 cm³/m.

U-section sheet piles (Larsen LX, Arcelor PU): symmetric profile with the interlock on the neutral axis. Better hydraulic seal than Z, slightly lower section modulus per kg of steel. Common pairs: LX 8 to LX 32 (lightweight to medium-duty), PU 12 to PU 32.

Combi-walls and tubular pile walls: alternating tubular piles (king piles) with infill sheet piles. Used for heavy port retaining, deep cofferdams (12 to 30 m), and where the bending demand exceeds standard Z-section capacity.

Soldier pile and lagging: H-section king piles (HE-A, HE-B, IPE) installed at spacing, with timber, precast concrete, or sprayed-concrete lagging spanning between piles. Used for shallower temporary excavation support in granular soils, where the lagging can be installed as excavation proceeds.

Steel grades

Sheet pile steel per BS EN 10248: S 240 GP (yield 240 N/mm², common for shallow temporary work), S 270 GP, S 320 GP, S 355 GP (the federal-grade default), S 390 GP, S 430 GP, S 460 GP (high-yield for heavy applications). Higher grade lets you achieve the required moment capacity with a lighter section, reducing crane and barge requirements.

Installation methods (with vibration profile)

  • Impact driving (drop hammer or diesel hammer): hardest ground penetration capability. High vibration. Used in open sites away from sensitive structures, or for the final seating drive in mixed ground.
  • Vibrating (resonance-free, high-frequency): the Malaysian default. Variable-moment vibrators (PTC, ICE) allow soft-start to limit vibration near sensitive structures. Production rate typically 4 to 12 m per hour per pair in standard residual soil.
  • Static pressing (silent piler): hydraulic jacking using already-installed sheets as the reaction. Near-zero vibration, low noise. Premium method for urban tight-site work (basement excavation near existing buildings, MRT cut-and-cover near operational structures).
  • Pre-auger plus drive: pre-bore a relief hole, then drive or vibrate the sheet through the loosened soil. Standard technique for hard ground, boulder zones, and vibration-sensitive sites. Common above weathered rock or in karst limestone.

Toe and refusal management

Refusal is the unwanted event when the sheet stops penetrating before reaching design toe. Common causes: boulders, hard rock, dense gravel band, residual rock outcrops. Mitigation: SI review at proposal stage to flag refusal risk, pre-auger relief hole, weld-on toe shoe for cutting, or revised toe level approved by consultant. Refusal early in the production run is the right time to revise method.

05 / Method statement

How we install safely and predictably.

01

Site investigation review

SI report drives the choice of section, install method, and toe depth. We flag refusal risk, boulders, hard rock, and recommend pre-bore where needed.

02

Method selection

Driven, vibrated, or pre-augered. Vibration monitoring required near sensitive structures. Pre-bore for hard ground or vibration-sensitive sites.

03

Mobilisation & trial pile

Trial drive to confirm penetration, drivability, and final toe level. Adjust method if refusal encountered.

04

Production drive

Pitch, drive, interlock, repeat. Continuous QA/QC on verticality, interlock engagement, and toe level. Whaler/strut installation as excavation proceeds.

05

Excavation support / extraction

Permanent sheets stay in place. Temporary sheets are extracted at backfill, recovery rate typically 70-95% depending on ground.

07 / FAQ

What sheet pile types? +
Steel U-section, Z-section, AU/AZ profiles (Larssen/Arcelor), LX series for shallow. Soldier piling with timber/precast lagging for granular soils.
Driven, vibrated, or silent? +
Driven for hard ground and open sites. Vibrated for most Malaysian work. Pre-augered/silent for sensitive urban sites with vibration limits.
Standards? +
BS EN 12063, BS 8002, Eurocode 7, JKR specifications.
Temporary or permanent? +
Both. Permanent with coating/sacrificial-thickness sized for design life.
Common Malaysian use cases? +
