01 / Sabah context

Hillside, oil palm, post-monsoon emergency.

Sabah slopes typically require integrated systems - single-system fixes (soil nail only / guniting only) often fail in tropical rainfall. Our Sabah default: drainage first (horizontal drains lower the groundwater table, the actual failure driver), soil nailing reinforcement, guniting facing, and rock netting on cliff exposures. Three project patterns: Crocker Range residential hillsides, oil palm estate platforms, and post-monsoon road remediation on the Sandakan-Lahad Datu-Tawau corridor.
