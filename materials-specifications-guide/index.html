Materials & specifications.

Practical materials reference for Malaysian geotechnical works. Steel reinforcement (Grade 460 Y-bar, T-bar, deformed threadbar Grade 500/670/950, mild steel mesh A6/A8/A10/A12, BRC). Sheet pile steel (Grade S270GP/S320GP/S355GP/S390GP/S430GP). Prestressing strand (7-wire 15.2/15.7 mm low-relaxation, Grade Y1860, BS 5896 / EN 10138). Grout mixes (cement-water ratio, plasticiser, target cube strength). Concrete grades and shotcrete (wet-mix, dry-mix, ACI 506). Geosynthetics (geogrid, geotextile, geomembrane, geocell, geocomposite). Drainage materials. Aligned with BS / EN, MS standards, AASHTO, JKR/SPJ. 02 / threadbar Threadbar (Soil Nail / Tieback)

Continuous-thread high-tensile steel.

threadbar is a continuous coarse-pitch thread on a hot-rolled steel bar - patented by DSI (Dywidag Systems International) and produced by DSI, Williams Form Engineering, specialty threadbar manufacturer, and licensed manufacturers. The continuous thread allows in-situ coupling and mechanical end fittings without thread cutting. Used worldwide for soil nails, tiebacks, micropiles, ground anchors (where threadbar is preferred over strand).

GradeYield (N/mm^2)UTS (N/mm^2)Common dia (mm)Use
Grade 500 (B500B equivalent)500550-60016, 20, 25, 32, 40, 50Soil nails, micropiles, lower stress applications
Grade 670 (most common Malaysia)67080025, 28, 32, 36, 40, 50, 57, 63, 75Standard soil nail / tieback / threadbar anchor
Grade 835 / 950835 / 9501030 / 105025, 32, 36, 40, 47, 57, 63, 75High-load anchors / micropiles
Grade 1050 / 1080 / 12301050+1230+25, 32, 47, 65, 75Permanent prestressed anchors (alternative to strand)

Common Malaysian soil nail bar

32 mm threadbar Grade 670 - working load 280-330 kN, ultimate ~530 kN. Use for permanent and temporary soil nails. Hot-dip galvanised (HDG) or epoxy-coated for permanent works.

25 mm threadbar Grade 670 - working load 170-200 kN. Use for shorter / lighter nails.

40 mm threadbar Grade 670 - working load 430-510 kN. Use for high-load nails / temporary tiebacks.

Accessories

  • Couplers - mechanical, 1.5-2x bar diameter outside, screw-on engagement
  • End plates - 200 x 200 x 25 mm steel plate or 300 x 300 x 30 mm for high load
  • Hex nuts - galvanised, matching grade
  • Centralisers - plastic, every 1.5-2 m, ensures 25 mm minimum grout cover
  • Spacers / chairs in dual-corrosion-protected systems
specialty threadbar manufacturer / Williams alternative. specialty threadbar manufacturer 1030 (1030 N/mm^2 yield) and Williams All-Thread bars (Grade 75/97/100/150) are alternative threadbar systems used in Malaysia. Functionally interchangeable with threadbar in design - select based on project specification, lead time, supplier preference. All to BS EN 14199 (micropiles), BS 8081 / EAD 160004 (anchors), or BS EN 14490 (soil nails) execution standards.

Strand configuration

  • 4-strand: 600-700 kN typical working - residential / commercial walls
  • 6-strand: 900-1000 kN - highway / commercial walls
  • 8-strand: 1300-1400 kN - large commercial / federal works
  • 12-strand: 1900-2000 kN - tall walls, dam tieback, mega-projects
  • 15-strand and above: special applications (dam, bridge tieback, megaprojects)

Corrosion protection class

  • Class 1 (most common): single PE / HDPE sheath in free length, bare strand bonded in cement grout in fixed length
  • Class 1A: sheath plus grease in free length
  • Class 2 (double corrosion): additional outer corrugated duct around bond zone, double-corrosion-protected

Class 2 mandatory for: aggressive ground (sulphate / chloride / acid), critical permanent works, very long design life (100+ years), restrike / re-stress anchors.

Quality control

  • Mill certificate for every cement batch
  • Pre-construction trial mix - confirm flowability, bleed, set time, strength
  • Cube tests: 7-day and 28-day, minimum 3 cubes per batch
  • Density check by mud balance during pumping (target density 1.85-1.95 g/cm^3)
  • Bleed test: less than 2 percent at 3 hours per BS EN 445

Pumping & placement

  • Mixer: high-shear colloidal mixer for stable, low-bleed grout
  • Pumping pressure: 5-10 bar typical at the borehole collar
  • Tremie placement: bottom-up, displace air / drilling fluid
  • Grout return at collar - confirms full bond zone fill
  • Re-grouting (post-grouting / regrouting) for high-load anchors / nails in weak soil - 12-48 hours after primary, tube-a-manchette technique

Shotcrete vs Gunite. Both are sprayed concrete. "Wet-mix shotcrete" - all ingredients (cement, sand, water, admixtures) pre-mixed; pumped to the nozzle; air added at nozzle. "Dry-mix shotcrete" (also called "gunite") - dry materials pneumatically conveyed; water added at the nozzle. Modern Malaysian practice favours wet-mix for volume work (better quality control, lower rebound, less dust); dry-mix for small-volume / repair / remote site (lower equipment footprint). To EN 14487 and ACI 506.

Specification checklist

  • Polymer (HDPE / PET) - for design life and creep behaviour
  • Tensile strength (UTS) - per ASTM D6637 / ISO 10319
  • Long-term design strength (T_LTDS) - after partial factors for creep, durability, installation damage, site-specific
  • Connection strength to facing block (for SRW / MSE) - 100 percent of design tensile factored
  • Junction strength - greater than 90 percent of rib strength
  • Aperture geometry for granular interlock
  • Roll dimensions and weight for handling
  • Long-term creep certified (ASTM D5262)
  • Installation damage Type B (ISO 10722)

Partial factors (BS 8006)

From UTS to T_LTDS (long-term design strength):

T_LTDS = UTS / (RF_creep * RF_durability * RF_damage * RF_other)
RF_creep: 1.5 - 2.5 (HDPE), 1.4 - 1.6 (PET)
RF_durability: 1.05 - 1.30 (depends on chemistry / pH / temperature)
RF_damage: 1.05 - 1.40 (depends on fill gradation and angularity)
RF_other: 1.0 - 1.1 (joints, end conditions)
Total RF: typically 2.0 - 4.0 - so T_LTDS is typically 25-50 percent of UTS.

Filtration, separation, drainage, protection.

TypePolymerMass (g/m^2)Use
Non-woven needle-punchedPP (polypropylene)100 - 400Filtration, drainage, separation, protection. Most common for geotech
Non-woven heat-bondedPP / PET100 - 200Lighter filtration, separation

Filtration design (Christopher-Holtz / FHWA)

  • AOS (apparent opening size O95) - per soil gradation
  • Permittivity (psi) - greater than 0.5 /sec drained, greater than 0.1 /sec for fines
  • Mass per unit area - 200-300 g/m^2 typical for residual soil filter
  • Tensile / puncture / burst strength for installation stress
  • UV resistance if exposed (otherwise bury within 14-30 days)

Separation / reinforcement design (BS 8006)

  • Tensile strength (wide-width per ISO 10319)
  • Strain at failure / strain at design load
  • Junction efficiency (woven only)
  • Long-term creep (PET more stable than PP)
  • Durability factors (chemistry, temperature, biology)

11 / Geocell

3D honeycomb confinement for slope and soil.

Geocell is a 3D cellular confinement system - HDPE strips welded into a honeycomb pattern. Expanded on site, infilled with soil / aggregate / topsoil / concrete. Provides lateral confinement that increases the effective strength of the infill.

PropertyRange
Strip thickness1.1 - 1.5 mm typical
Cell depth50, 75, 100, 150, 200 mm
Cell size (expanded)200 x 200, 300 x 300, 410 x 460 mm typical
PolymerHDPE (most common), NPA (novel polymer alloy - higher creep resistance)
StandardASTM D7864, GRI-GS5

Use cases: slope erosion control with vegetation in cells, working platform on soft soil (75-150 mm cell depth), retaining wall fascia (100-200 mm depth), channel armouring with concrete-filled cells. STRATA Geosystems produces NPA-polymer StrataWeb geocell, supplied in Malaysia by Starwall Sdn Bhd (sole STRATA Geosystems Malaysia distributor).

Frequently asked

Materials questions.

What grade of soil nail bar should I use? +
Standard Malaysian: 32 mm deformed threadbar Grade 670 (working load ~280-330 kN, ultimate ~530 kN). Alternative diameters: 25 mm (170-200 kN) for shorter / lighter nails, 40 mm (430-510 kN) for high-load nails. Hot-dip galvanised or epoxy-coated for permanent works. Centralisers every 1.5-2 m. To BS EN 14490.
What strand for ground anchors? +
7-wire low-relaxation 15.7 mm strand, Grade Y1860 (BS 5896 / EN 10138-3), breaking load 279 kN per strand. Working load typically 60 percent UTS = 167 kN. Multi-strand 4-12 typical. Class 2 dual corrosion protection for permanent / aggressive ground.
What's the standard grout mix? +
OPC CEM I 42.5N, w/c 0.40-0.45, plasticiser 0.5-1 percent (optional), target 30-35 N/mm^2 28-day cube. For permanent anchor bond: w/c 0.40-0.42, target 40-50 N/mm^2. Pre-construction trial mix and 7/28-day cubes. Density 1.85-1.95 g/cm^3 by mud balance during pumping.
What geogrid for MSE walls? +
Uniaxial HDPE (punched-and-stretched family) or PET (coated polyester woven family), UTS 60-200 kN/m typical, T_LTDS 25-90 kN/m after partial factors. Group company Starwall supplies StrataGrid PET as sole STRATA Geosystems Malaysia distributor. Connection strength to facing 100 percent of design tensile. Aperture for granular interlock. Long-term creep certified (ASTM D5262), installation damage Type B (ISO 10722). Design life partial factors per BS 8006-1.
What sheet pile grade is standard? +
S355GP (yield 355 N/mm^2) is the most common Malaysian default for permanent works. S270GP / S320GP for lower-stress / temporary. Higher grades (S430GP, S460GP) for specialist heavy applications. Local stockists hold AU and FSP series. Lead time for non-stock sections: 8-16 weeks ex-mill.
