Slope works combine multiple geotechnical systems to suit the failure mode of each slope. Six typical scenarios:

Utility infrastructure

TNB transmission corridor slope stabilization, Air Selangor reservoir slope works, IWK trunk corridor slopes, telco tower access slopes.

Residential development

Hillside township slope works, gated community slope stabilization, developer housing slope works, individual hillside lot stabilization. Cluster and terrace developments are the most frequent driver.

Commercial development

Commercial podium slope stabilization, retail park slope works, hillside commercial development slope works.

Industrial development

Industrial estate slope stabilization, factory compound slope works, warehouse pad slope works, data center compound slope works.

Public infrastructure

Highway and federal road slope works, bridge approach slopes, rail corridor slopes, road widening slope works. Live-traffic phasing standard.

Hillside and special terrain

Post-landslide slope remediation, monsoon-failed slope restoration, highland resort slope works, dam slope stabilization. Tropical residual soil with monsoon groundwater drives most remedial scope.
