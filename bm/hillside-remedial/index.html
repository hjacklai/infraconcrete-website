01 / Mengapa cerun gagal

Tiga punca utama.

Kegagalan cerun biasanya dicetuskan oleh salah satu atau gabungan: (1) kenaikan air bawah tanah, hujan meresap mengurangkan tegasan berkesan; (2) perubahan beban, pemotongan, beban tambah, getaran; (3) luluhawa & degradasi, luluhawa mengurangkan kekuatan batu dan tanah dari masa ke masa.

Reka bentuk pemulihan mesti mendiagnosis punca asal dahulu. Sekadar memasang soil nail pada cerun gagal tanpa menyelesaikan masalah air bawah tanah hanya menangguhkan kegagalan seterusnya.

02 / Aliran kerja pemulihan

Diagnosis, reka bentuk, laksana.

1. Diagnosis tapak

Ukur topografi, bacaan sensor jarak, ground penetrating radar, gerudi tanah. Kenal pasti permukaan gelongsor & paras air bawah tanah.

2. Pasang pemantauan

Sensor jarak, piezometer, inclinometer. Wujudkan baseline data.

3. Reka bentuk semula

Oleh perunding geoteknikal mengikut Eurocode 7 / JKR. Kami serta dalam semakan kebolehlaksanaan.

4. Pemulihan menyeluruh

Saliran ufuk (turunkan air bawah tanah) + soil nail (tetulang) + guniting (lapisan muka) + tembok penahan (bentuk semula geometri).

Where slope works typically appears.

Slope works combine multiple geotechnical systems to suit the failure mode of each slope. Six typical scenarios:

Infrastruktur utiliti

TNB transmission corridor slope stabilization, Air Selangor reservoir slope works, IWK trunk corridor slopes, telco tower access slopes.

Pembangunan kediaman

Hillside township slope works, gated community slope stabilization, developer housing slope works, individual hillside lot stabilization. Cluster and terrace developments are the most frequent driver.

Pembangunan komersial

Commercial podium slope stabilization, retail park slope works, hillside commercial development slope works.

Pembangunan perindustrian

Industrial estate slope stabilization, factory compound slope works, warehouse pad slope works, data center compound slope works.

Infrastruktur awam

Highway and federal road slope works, bridge approach slopes, rail corridor slopes, road widening slope works. Live-traffic phasing standard.

Bukit dan kawasan khas

Post-landslide slope remediation, monsoon-failed slope restoration, highland resort slope works, dam slope stabilization. Tropical residual soil with monsoon groundwater drives most remedial scope.

Rujukan berkaitan: compare slope stabilization, compare slope reinforcement, slope failure modes, slope monitoring, landslide prevention, post-landslide remediation, federal case studies.
