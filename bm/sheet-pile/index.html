Sheet Pile & Soldier Pile.

Sokongan tanah sementara dan kekal untuk pengorekan basement, perlindungan tebing, kerja jeti, dan stabilisasi cerun. Sheet pile profil U dan Z, soldier pile berkonkrit. Infraconcrete adalah kontraktor sokongan tanah pakar di Malaysia. Patuh BS EN 12063, Eurocode 7. CIDB G7. ISO 9001:2015.

01 / Bila digunakan

Bila tanah tidak boleh berdiri sendiri.

Sheet pile ialah penyelesaian sokongan tanah berterusan. Setiap sheet pile dikunci dengan jiran melalui sambungan interlock, membentuk dinding kalis air berterusan. Sesuai untuk tanah lembut, paras air bawah tanah tinggi, tapak sempit.

Aplikasi tipikal: pengorekan basement (sokongan sementara), perlindungan tebing & sea wall (struktur kekal), jeti & pelabuhan, cofferdam sementara, sokongan parit untuk paip bawah tanah.

02 / Sistem

Dua pendekatan teras.

Sheet Pile

Profil U atau Z. Sambungan interlock membentuk dinding berterusan. Boleh dipasang semula (cabut dan kitar semula). Sesuai untuk cofferdam sementara, basement dalam.

Soldier Pile

Tiang H + lagging kayu / konkrit. Lebih murah, sesuai untuk tapak kering. Selalu untuk basement perimeter.

Sistem sokongan

Sokongan berlapis atau anchor. Mengawal anjakan dan ubah bentuk lateral.

Kaedah pemasangan

Vibratory hammer, hydraulic press, high frequency static. Pilihan ikut tanah dan kesan kepada bangunan jiran.

Use cases

Where geotechnical works typically appears.

Geotechnical works underpin most kinds of land development. Almost every project that touches sloping or marginal land needs at least one solution from the integrated toolkit. The five typical scenarios:

Utility infrastructure

TNB substation and pylon corridors, Air Selangor reservoir and pump stations, IWK trunk sewer and treatment plant compounds, telco tower foundations. Utility infrastructure is one of the largest geotechnical workload drivers.

Residential development

Developer housing platforms, hillside township stabilization, gated community access works, individual hillside lot works. Cluster, terrace, and bungalow lots all involve cut-and-fill engineering.

Hillside and special terrain

Post-landslide remediation, monsoon-failed slope restoration, highland resort works, coastal reclamation. Tropical residual soil with high groundwater is the common engineering challenge.

Commercial and industrial

Warehouse and logistics pads, industrial estate platforms, commercial podium retaining, data center site preparation. Heavy floor loads and tight programmes.

Public infrastructure

Highway and federal road embankments, bridge approach works, rail corridor works, road widening works. Live-traffic phasing and authority submission framework standard.
