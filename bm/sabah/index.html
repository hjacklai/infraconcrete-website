Use cases

Where this work typically appears.

Geotechnical works underpin most kinds of land development. Typical applications include developer housing platforms, commercial and industrial pads, TNB substation and pylon corridors, Air Selangor reservoir and pump stations, IWK trunk sewer and treatment plant compounds, highway and federal road embankments, and hillside township stabilization. Almost every project that touches sloping or marginal land needs at least one geotechnical solution from the integrated toolkit.
