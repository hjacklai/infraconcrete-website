Use cases

Where geotechnical works typically appears.

Geotechnical works underpin most kinds of land development. Almost every project that touches sloping or marginal land needs at least one solution from the integrated toolkit. The five typical scenarios:

Utility infrastructure

TNB substation and pylon corridors, Air Selangor reservoir and pump stations, IWK trunk sewer and treatment plant compounds, telco tower foundations. Utility infrastructure is one of the largest geotechnical workload drivers.

Residential development

Developer housing platforms, hillside township stabilization, gated community access works, individual hillside lot works. Cluster, terrace, and bungalow lots all involve cut-and-fill engineering.

Hillside and special terrain

Post-landslide remediation, monsoon-failed slope restoration, highland resort works, coastal reclamation. Tropical residual soil with high groundwater is the common engineering challenge.

Commercial and industrial

Warehouse and logistics pads, industrial estate platforms, commercial podium retaining, data center site preparation. Heavy floor loads and tight programmes.

Public infrastructure

Highway and federal road embankments, bridge approach works, rail corridor works, road widening works. Live-traffic phasing and authority submission framework standard.

Related references: federal project case studies, authority submission guide, climate and monsoon engineering, tropical residual soil reference, geotechnical design framework, geotechnical investigation, materials specifications, QA and testing, cost and programme guide, slope failure modes.
