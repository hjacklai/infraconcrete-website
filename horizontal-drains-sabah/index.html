Use cases

Where horizontal drains typically appears.

Horizontal drains dewater slopes and structures across many development types. Often the first intervention in any slope where high groundwater is the underlying instability driver. The five typical scenarios:

Utility infrastructure

TNB substation slope dewatering, Air Selangor reservoir embankment dewatering, IWK pump station foundation dewatering, dam slope drainage. Groundwater control is critical for utility-grade reliability.

Residential development

Hillside township groundwater control, developer housing slope dewatering, gated community slope drainage, hillside lot back-drainage. Often the first remedial response to perched groundwater.

Hillside and special terrain

Post-landslide slope dewatering, monsoon emergency intervention, retaining wall back-drainage, dam slope drainage. Most economic intervention for groundwater-driven instability.

Commercial and industrial

Warehouse pad embankment dewatering, industrial estate platform drainage, commercial pad slope drainage. Ground stability for heavy floor loads.

Public infrastructure

Highway embankment dewatering, bridge approach drainage, rail corridor slope drainage, road cut face drainage. Routine on every federal slope works contract.
