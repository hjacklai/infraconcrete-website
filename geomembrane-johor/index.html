01 / Johor applications

Petrochemical, aquaculture, landfill, port-side.

  • Pasir Gudang petrochemical secondary containment: HDPE 1.5-2.5 mm for full chemical compatibility with stored hydrocarbons; double-liner option with leak-detection geocomposite for high-risk effluents.
  • Pontian / Batu Pahat aquaculture pond liners: LLDPE 1.0-1.5 mm or PVC 0.75-1.0 mm depending on water chemistry and design life.
  • Iskandar / Johor landfill basal and cap systems: HDPE 1.5 mm basal + LLDPE 1.5 mm cap per DOE Cawangan Johor approval.
  • Port-side oil-and-gas containment (Pasir Gudang Port, Tanjung Pelepas adjacent): HDPE 2.0 mm secondary containment with full QA.
  • Industrial leachate evaporation ponds: HDPE with composite GCL or compacted clay backup.
