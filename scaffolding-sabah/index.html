01 / Districts served

Sabah mainland coverage.

  • West coast: Kota Kinabalu, Papar, Beaufort, Kuala Penyu.
  • Interior: Keningau, Tenom, Nabawan.
  • East coast: Sandakan, Lahad Datu, Tawau, Semporna, Kunak.
  • North: Kudat, Kota Belud.
02 / Common Sabah project types

Four key sectors.

  • Pan Borneo Highway infrastructure: bridge pier access, retaining wall construction, highway sign gantry work along the Pan Borneo alignment.
  • KK hillside residential and commercial: hillside condominium facade, hillside retaining wall construction, hillside residential renovation.
  • Palm oil mill support: non-petrochemical mill equipment installation, boiler maintenance, chimney stack maintenance (large-diameter plantation estates).
  • Government facility maintenance: JKR Sabah, Sabah state hospital network, school refurbishment.
03 / Logistics to Borneo

Sea and air mobilisation.

Equipment and crew mobilised from Klang Valley by sea (container, 7-14 days to KK or Sandakan) and air (crew, same day). Project minimum stay typically 4-8 weeks for sustained delivery. Staging base in Kota Kinabalu for west-coast and interior work; Tawau or Sandakan for east-coast. We partner with local Sabah logistics providers for last-mile delivery and local labour supplementation.
